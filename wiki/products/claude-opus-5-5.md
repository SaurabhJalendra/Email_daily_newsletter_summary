## Summary

Claude Opus 5.5 is Anthropic's Sep 22, 2026 Opus-tier launch — the first model in the Claude 5.5 family. It matches Claude Fable 5.1's performance on most tasks while costing about 40% less to run than Opus 5, generating output more than 30% faster, and pricing at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens (vs Opus 5's $5/$25 and Fable 5.1's $10/$50 — 60% below Fable 5.1). It ships with a 1M-token context window, up to 128,000 output tokens, and always-on adaptive reasoning. Anthropic reports Opus 5.5 has the best behavioral audit scores of any model the company has tested, with improved safety features and a lower likelihood of taking irreversible actions.

The release lands within minutes of OpenAI's simultaneous [[gpt-6-sol]] + [[gpt-6-luna]] launches at 40-50% price cuts, canonicalizing late-Sep-2026 as a *canonical concrete-price-cut duopoly-race inflection window* — extending the multi-cycle [[ai-pace-letter]] arc with a *concrete contradiction-tier canonical anchor* (Anthropic and OpenAI CEOs publicly agreed on the need to slow down AI development 8 days before shipping the price-cut duo). Per Theo t3.gg on X: *"Opus 5.5 is SMALLER than Opus 5?? Did Anthropic massively level up their post training? Huge."* — canonicalizes Opus 5.5 as *canonical post-training-leveraging-tier substrate rather than pretraining-scaling-tier substrate*. Available through Anthropic + AWS + Google Cloud + Microsoft Azure.
