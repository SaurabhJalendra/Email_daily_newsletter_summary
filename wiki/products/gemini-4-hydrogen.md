## Summary

Gemini 4 Hydrogen is [[google]]'s November 2026 lower-cost agentic model in the Gemini 4 family, designed for fast execution across agentic workflows with a 1-million-token context window. It is available through the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Google Search, and the Gemini app, positioning it as the family's inexpensive-tier counterpart to [[gemini-4-argon]] + [[gemini-4-carbon]] + [[gemini-4-pro]] for production-agent + coding + multi-step-workflow deployments. The structural significance is that it extends Google's four-SKU Gemini 4 family with a dedicated low-cost + fast-execution tier for agentic workloads, further compressing inference economics at the frontier while matching strong benchmark performance.
