## Summary

MiMo-V2.6 is Xiaomi's September 2026 open-weights AI model series, released 2026-09-22 across three variants: **MiMo-V2.6-Pro** (1.02-trillion parameters, 42B active MoE), **MiMo-V2.6-Flash** (smaller, balances intelligence/efficiency/cost), and **MiMo-V2.6-Pro-UltraSpeed** (up to 20× faster output at the same quality for extreme-generation-speed workloads). All three are natively omnimodal, designed for multimodal + reasoning + coding + agentic workloads, and ship with a **reported 1-million-token context window**. Xiaomi published under the permissive **MIT license** alongside model weights, technical documentation, reinforcement-learning code, more than 7,000 training environments, and a distilled 9B model for accessibility. Reported training cost is **$3M** — extraordinarily cheap for the parameter class.

MiMo-V2.6-Pro debuts as the **top open-weights model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (AA-II 46)**, on the Intelligence-vs-Cost-per-Task Pareto frontier at **$0.13 per Intelligence Index task** — matching [[grok-4-7]]'s intelligence score at costs *"one twentieth to one sixtieth of leading international models"*. The release crowns Xiaomi as a new Chinese frontier lab (per AINews' *"crowning a new Chinese frontier lab"* framing) — a lab not traditionally counted among the "six Chinese AI Tigers" that now ships top-open-weights-tier work. Fuli Luo, a former DeepSeek star engineer now at Xiaomi, is publicly attributed for the release; abnormal-for-the-industry transparency in internal metrics + published final RL training runs also framed as a canonical anchor.
