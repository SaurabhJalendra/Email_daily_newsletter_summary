## Summary

GPT-6 Sol is OpenAI's Sep 22, 2026 launch — a faster, lower-cost tier of the GPT-6 family built on the advances of [[openai-astra]] (the frontier-capability tier). Sol targets professional work, complex coding, research, and agentic workflows. OpenAI reports Sol makes about half as many mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol, and beats Claude Opus 5 on AutomationBench while costing roughly 91% less per completed task. It launched alongside [[gpt-6-luna]] (the high-volume tier) within minutes of Anthropic's [[claude-opus-5-5]], forming a *canonical concrete-price-cut duopoly-race* on late-Sep-2026.

Sol is available through the OpenAI API as `gpt-6-sol`, and in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users. It is not yet available in standard ChatGPT chat mode. Pricing is reported at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — 50% below GPT-5.6 Sol's promotional pricing and roughly half the price of [[claude-opus-5-5]].
