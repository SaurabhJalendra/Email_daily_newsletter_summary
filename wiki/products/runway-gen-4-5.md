## Summary

Runway Gen-4.5 is Runway's December 2025 text-to-video flagship. It surpassed both Google's Veo 3 and OpenAI's Sora on the Artificial Analysis text-to-video benchmark, placing Runway back at the top of the frontier video category. The model prioritizes physical accuracy — realistic momentum, fluid motion, and material coherence — areas where competitor models had been criticized for "uncanny" physics.
