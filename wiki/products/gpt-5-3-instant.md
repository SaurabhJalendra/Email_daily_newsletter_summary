## Summary

GPT-5.3 Instant is an update to OpenAI's default ChatGPT model, released in early March 2026. It prioritizes conversational quality and reduces hallucinations — OpenAI reports a 26.8% hallucination reduction with web access and 19.7% without — while cutting unnecessary refusals and long safety preambles in favor of direct answers. It sits in the "Instant" tier of ChatGPT's default routing.
