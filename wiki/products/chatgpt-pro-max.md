## Summary

ChatGPT Pro Max is a reported OpenAI subscription tier priced at **$500 per month** — 2.5× the current $200 Pro plan — surfaced on Sep 24, 2026 via references in ChatGPT's unreleased frontend and subscription interfaces (attributed to developer Tibor Blaho and TestingCatalog). Leaked descriptions point to *"Fastest Work and Codex"* positioning, targeting users running intensive **agentic-research + writing + software-development** workloads. Other reported benefits include **maximum memory + 100GB of file storage + priority access to frontier models + early feature access**. OpenAI has not publicly confirmed the plan, and its public pricing pages do not list Pro Max or a launch date as of this cycle.

The plan would extend the market's shift toward premium-AI-subscriptions priced for professional and enterprise-level usage — monetizing **speed + compute priority + long-running agent workloads** rather than simply adding model access. Timing aligns with strong demand and reported constraints around the existing Pro tier, suggesting OpenAI may be testing a high-margin option for developers, researchers, and organizations whose productivity gains justify the cost. Feature set, availability, infrastructure claims, and final price remain unverified until OpenAI makes an official announcement.
