## Summary

Googlebook is Google's premium AI-native laptop category, announced 2026-05-13 with shipping partners Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo. Built ground-up for "Gemini Intelligence" by merging Android and ChromeOS into one OS that runs Android phone apps and files alongside web/ChromeOS workflows. Features a system-wide "Magic Pointer" AI cursor that summons Gemini anywhere on screen and contextually understands what's being pointed at. Includes generative widgets that pull live data from Gmail/Calendar, AirDrop-style file sharing across Android/iPhone, 3D emoji, and a "Pause Point" anti-doomscrolling feature. Ships fall 2026. Positioned as Google's first new laptop concept in 15 years and a direct shot at Apple MacBooks. The launch is the most consequential consumer push for Google since launching Android in 2008, since it pulls AI out of dedicated chat interfaces and embeds it as the OS layer itself.
