## Summary

Muse Video is [[meta]]'s first generative video model, the video-tier addition to the [[muse-image]] / [[muse-spark]] / [[muse-glimmer]] Muse family from Meta Superintelligence Labs. It generates short video clips from text prompts, images, or existing footage — and, critically, produces **native, synchronized audio** (music, ambience, sound effects, sometimes voice) inside the same generation pass rather than requiring a separate audio stage. Meta emphasizes prompt adherence, visual fidelity, temporal consistency, and audio–video coherence. The model is in **closed beta / preview** as of August 2026 with selected partners and limited creator access; broad public availability is framed as "coming soon to creators and in Meta AI." Early demos show ad-style and cinematic clips with room tone, diegetic foley, and native voiceover, with the model ranking competitively on text-to-video leaderboards.

## Key Facts

- Vendor: [[meta]] Superintelligence Labs (Muse family)
- Modalities: text-to-video + image-to-video + video-to-video (extend existing footage)
- Native audio: music, ambience, sound effects, voiceover — generated in same pass
- Positioning: one of the first major audio-native video models from a Big Tech platform
- Availability: closed beta / preview (Aug 2026); "coming soon to creators and in Meta AI"
- Distribution surfaces: Meta AI, Instagram, WhatsApp (per third-party workflow guides)
