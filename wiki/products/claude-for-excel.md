## Summary

Claude for Excel is [[anthropic]]'s in-Excel assistant — a sidebar that can read, analyze, and modify spreadsheets directly. Released in beta 2025-10-28 with a finance-first surface area: seven new connectors to financial-data platforms (Aiera, LSEG, Moody's) plus several finance-specific Agent Skills (cash-flow models, company analysis, coverage reports). Together with [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] (launched the same week) and Microsoft Copilot's no-code App Builder upgrades (Oct 30), it marks the late-October-2025 push of frontier labs into the workplace productivity stack — Anthropic's wedge being financial analysis specifically.
