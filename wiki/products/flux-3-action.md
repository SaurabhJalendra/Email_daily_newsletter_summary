## Summary

FLUX 3 Action is [[black-forest-labs]]'s Oct 2026 dedicated world-action model — a 7-billion-parameter model that turns camera feeds and natural-language instructions into robot movements. Unlike the general-purpose [[flux-3]] multimodal substrate (image + video + audio + robotics), FLUX 3 Action is a specialized robotics-foundation-model tier. BFL reports it tops Nvidia's RoboLab simulation benchmark; the model is hosted on Hugging Face under BFL's FLUX Kommunity License.

The release graduates BFL from a multimodal content-generation lab into a *dedicated world-action-model vendor*, placing it in the robotics-foundation-model cohort alongside Nvidia Isaac GR00T, Google DeepMind Gemini Robotics 2, Alibaba Qwen Robot Suite, and Dyna-2.
