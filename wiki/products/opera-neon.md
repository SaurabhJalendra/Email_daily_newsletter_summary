## Summary

Opera Neon is a new AI-integrated browser from Opera that recalls browsing history and automates routine tasks. Launched as a subscription product in early October 2025 and positioned against AI features from Microsoft and Google, it is part of a broader shift toward AI-native browsers alongside [[perplexity-comet]].
