## Summary

ERNIE-4.5-VL-28B-A3B-Thinking (informally "ERNIE 4 Vision" or "ERNIE 4.5-VL-Thinking") is [[baidu]]'s open-source multimodal reasoning model, released in mid-November 2025. Coverage positions it as competitive with GPT-5 and Gemini 2.5 Pro despite being substantially smaller, extending the "Chinese open-source matches or beats US closed-source" pattern established by [[kimi-k2-thinking]] earlier the same month.
