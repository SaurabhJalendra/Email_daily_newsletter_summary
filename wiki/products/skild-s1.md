## Summary

**Skild S1** is [[skild-ai]]'s robot foundation model, framed as *"a significant step beyond typical robot foundation models"* because it performs **in-context learning for robotics** — a single 10-minute video of a task is enough to reproduce the behavior on real hardware, with no fine-tuning step. This lands S1 at a *demonstration-tier* substrate distinct from the *scale-tier* approach used by [[figure]] Index (which crowdsources millions of video uploads as training corpora). S1 productizes *language-model-style in-context learning* onto the embodied-AI surface, a novel positioning within the mid-2026 robot-foundation-model competitive cohort.
