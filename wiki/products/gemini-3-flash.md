## Summary

Gemini 3 Flash is the fast / cost-efficient tier of [[google]]'s [[gemini-3]] family, launched on 2025-12-19 — six days after [[openai]]'s [[gpt-5-2]] released. Google positioned it as "frontier-level intelligence at a fraction of the price": 3× faster than Gemini 2.5 Pro and outperforming the older Pro model on PhD-level reasoning and knowledge benchmarks. It became the default model powering both the Gemini app and Google's AI Search experience at launch. Coverage framed Flash as Google's same-week answer to GPT-5.2 — sustaining the December back-and-forth between the two labs.
