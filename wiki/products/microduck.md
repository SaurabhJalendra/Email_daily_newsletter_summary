## Summary

Microduck is Hugging Face + Pollen Robotics's Aug 2026 **$399 open-source retrainable bipedal robot** — a **25cm tall duck-style robot** with **15 actuators** and a **rich sensor stack** that runs an **onboard policy loop at 50 Hz** and ships with **seven trained moves** out of the box. It can **walk, crouch, grab objects with its beak, stand itself back up after a fall**, and (per some newsletters) waddle, pick up objects, and roller skate. Owners can **retrain behaviors at home in simulation and publish them for others to use**, extending HF's open-model-hub distribution primitive from software-model-weights into **shared-robot-behavior primitives**.

The launch productizes HF's May 2024 Pollen Robotics acquisition into a **consumer-price-tier open-source-robotics-hardware entry-point**, graduating HF's substrate from *open-model-hub* into *open-model-hub + open-robotics-hardware-tier two-substrate cluster*. It marks the first-in-wiki *frontier-open-source-hub-ships-consumer-price-hardware* canonical anchor and lands as **Daily Digest Top Story #4** on 2026-08-28.
