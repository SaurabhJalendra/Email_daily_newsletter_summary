## Summary

Veo 3 is [[google]]'s video-generation model family. In mid-October 2025 it moved from leak to official release: a 3.1 version leaked on 2025-10-13 and was publicly launched on 2025-10-16. It is [[google]]'s direct counter to [[openai]]'s [[sora-2]] in the generative-video race.
