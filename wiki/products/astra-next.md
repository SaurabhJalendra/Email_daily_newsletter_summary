## Summary

Astra-next is [[openai]]'s **unreleased, next-generation successor to [[openai-astra]]**, described by the company as "significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra." It was first surfaced publicly on 2026-09-08 when OpenAI announced that Astra-next — orchestrating a swarm of roughly **10,000 agents in parallel for 88 hours** and consuming ~**130 billion tokens** at estimated compute cost of **>$40M** — produced OpenAI's claimed proof of the **Navier-Stokes existence-and-smoothness Millennium Prize Problem**. GPT-6 Astra itself was used *afterward* (~17 hours of runtime) to help verify and formalize the proof in Lean, framing Astra-next as the *discovery-tier substrate* and Astra as the *verification-tier substrate*.

Per OpenAI's internal benchmark of difficult unsolved math problems: Astra solves ~10% while Astra-next solves ~50% — the first-in-wiki *concrete internal-benchmark differential* between a shipped frontier model and its unreleased successor. The proof is claimed but not yet independently validated by the broader math community.
