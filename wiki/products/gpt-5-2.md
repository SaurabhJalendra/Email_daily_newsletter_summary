## Summary

GPT-5.2 is [[openai]]'s December 2025 [[chatgpt]] upgrade — the immediate response to Google's [[gemini-3]] launch and the company's "Code Red" framing. Released on 2025-12-13 in a three-tier lineup (Instant, Thinking, Pro), it ships a 400K-token context window, claims a 38% reduction in errors on complex decision-making vs GPT-5.1, and tops coding/math/reasoning leaderboards at launch. Coverage repeatedly framed it as "making everyone else look slow." Within days, OpenAI quietly removed the GPT-5 model router for Free/Go users — defaulting all queries to GPT-5.2 Instant — and tied the launch into the broader "OpenAI Triple Strike" wave (GPT-5.2 + [[gpt-image-1-5]] + ChatGPT Apps Store).
