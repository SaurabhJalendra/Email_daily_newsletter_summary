## Summary

DeepSeek V4 is [[deepseek]]'s late-April 2026 next-generation LLM series, shipping as a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) family with V4-Pro and V4-Flash variants. It is open-sourced shortly after release at significantly lower cost than [[openai]]'s [[gpt-5-4]] and [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-opus-4-7]] while challenging them on reasoning, math, STEM, coding, and agentic benchmarks — coverage explicitly frames the launch as triggering a sector-wide "AI pricing collapse" and pressuring frontier-lab margins. The V4 release is bookended by Tencent and Alibaba reportedly in talks to back DeepSeek at a $20B valuation, signaling that Chinese open-source AI has reached strategic-investor-class stature.
