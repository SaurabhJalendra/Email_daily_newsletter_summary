## Summary

Grok Imagine is [[xai]]'s generative image/video product. An API preview began circulating in late January 2026 alongside broader Grok roadmap coverage; the v1.0 release in February 2026 added enhanced video and audio generation, positioning the product as xAI's direct entry into the [[ai-video-generation]] race against [[sora-2]], [[runway-gen-4-5]], and [[veo-3]]. It is typically framed as a capability inside the wider Grok surface rather than a fully standalone brand, and benefits from xAI's Colossus-scale compute for training.
