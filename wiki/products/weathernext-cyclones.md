## Summary

WeatherNext Cyclones is Google DeepMind's tropical-cyclone-specific AI forecasting model, introduced in late August 2026 with a companion Nature publication. Its headline capability is jointly predicting **track, intensity, and size** of tropical cyclones — a three-axis joint-forecasting scope broader than typical single-axis operational cyclone models. Using 2023–2025 data, the study reports *at least a one-day advantage over leading operational models for certain forecast types*, establishing a concrete lead-time-delta anchor against traditional physics-based cyclone forecasting.

The product ships as a sibling to [[weathernext-2]] (the general open-source high-impact weather model open-sourced 2026-08-07), together forming DeepMind's *two-line weather-forecasting product stack*: general (WeatherNext 2) + cyclone-specialized (WeatherNext Cyclones). The Nature-publication-tier validation graduates DeepMind's AI-for-science arc into *peer-reviewed life-safety-tier evidence* for AI-model-outperforms-physics-based-model on operational weather forecasting, extending the AlphaFold/AlphaMissense biomedical-AI-for-science lineage into the operational meteorology domain.
