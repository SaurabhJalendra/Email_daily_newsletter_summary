## Summary

Ideogram 4.0 is Ideogram's first open-source image model — trained from scratch and released alongside Reve 2.0 in early June 2026 as part of a same-week "layouts in image generation" inflection. It ships with structured JSON prompting (a new interface that constrains layout and composition deterministically rather than relying on free-form text), native 2K resolution, and standout multilingual text rendering — taking the top spot on the open-weight Design Arena leaderboard at launch. The shift to open weights is significant: Ideogram had previously been a closed product, and the model is now available for deployment via fal and Hugging Face. Coverage frames Ideogram 4 + Reve 2 together as evidence that the "AGI-Hard" image-composition gate (precise layout control + text rendering at scale) has fallen.
