## Summary

ChatGPT Images 2.0 is [[openai]]'s April 2026 image-generation upgrade — the successor to [[gpt-image-1-5]] (Dec 2025) and the first ChatGPT image model to include a **reasoning step before generation**, framed as enabling high-quality, contextually-accurate images that interpret prompt intent before rendering. It supports non-Latin languages, more image sizes, and improved text rendering. The release is framed across coverage as a major advancement in AI image generation with broad implications for AI-generated content across industries.
