## Summary

GLM-5.2 is [[zhipu-ai]]'s mid-2026 minor-version successor to [[glm-5]] (754B-parameter MIT-licensed open foundation model from Feb 2026). First surfaced in this wiki via TLDR AI's three-headline cycle bundle on 2026-06-15-evening, where it appears as slot-2 alongside the dominant Anthropic-Fable-shutdown cycle headline and the OpenRouter Fusion launch — placing GLM-5.2 inside the same week that a US frontier-lab flagship ([[claude-fable-5]]) is being globally pulled by US government order, a notable timing-juxtaposition for the Chinese-open-source-model lineage.

The "5.2" version label continues the Zhipu numbered-release lineage ([[glm-4-7]] Dec 2025 → [[glm-5]] Feb 2026 → GLM-5.2 Jun 2026), consistent with the ~4-month minor-version cadence pattern observed across other Chinese-frontier-lab numbered series ([[deepseek]] V3.2 / V4, [[moonshot-ai]] K2.5 → K2.6 → K2.7). Substantive details on parameter count, MoE active-vs-total split, license, benchmark results, and distribution channels were not retrievable from the subject-line evidence and remain to be reconstructed once a downstream summary pipeline run captures the body content.
