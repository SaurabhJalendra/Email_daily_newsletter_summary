## Summary

Claude Fable 5.2 is Anthropic's November 2026 successor to [[claude-fable-5-1]], first productized at wiki-tier by Ethan Mollick's November 9 2026 One Useful Thing post *"Fable 5.2 and Jagged AGI"*. Mollick's deep-dive profiles Fable 5.2 across six distinct domains — filmmaking, hardware hacking, entrepreneurship, literary scholarship, academic research, and creative writing — demonstrating that a single frontier model can autonomously complete deep expert-level work in radically different fields with limited human guidance. Mollick's "Jagged AGI" framing — "general, often expert, and sometimes jagged" — concedes that Fable 5.2 is powerful enough to produce engaging video content in one shot, hack a USB keyboard into a game console, launch a business, reconstruct a Harvard humanities class, and conduct original empirical research, while still exhibiting uneven taste + judgment + imperfect self-awareness of its own mistakes.

Mollick's post is the primary wiki-tier source on Fable 5.2 as of Nov 10 2026; no official Anthropic model-card, API-identifier, or pricing disclosure has been surfaced in the summaries yet (the 10-02-evening "Fable 5.5 LEAKS" rumor on [[claude-fable-5-1]] substrate anchors the stealth-routing lineage into which Fable 5.2 appears to formally land). The model's cross-domain capability tier productizes it as the canonical early-Nov-2026 reference-anchor for the "Jagged AGI" thesis, and it pairs cycle-structurally with the same-week Anthropic near-RSI-threshold disclosures ([[recursive-self-improvement]]) as the *concrete shipped-capability counterpart* to Anthropic's governance-posture canonical anchors.
