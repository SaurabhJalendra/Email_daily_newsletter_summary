## Summary

Imagine Enterprise is the November 2026 jointly launched platform from [[ibm]] + [[microsoft]] for building, operating, and governing industry-specific AI agents. The division of labor splits responsibilities across the two vendors: [[ibm]] contributes more than **6,000 prebuilt agents + consulting assets** drawn from its watsonx + IBM Consulting substrate, while [[microsoft]] provides the **cloud + AI + security technologies** underpinning deployment and governance. The platform targets regulated-industry enterprise customers that need governed agentic workflows connecting AI agents to proprietary data and business processes.

The launch pairs cycle-structurally with the broader enterprise-agent-platform cohort — Google [[gemini-enterprise]] + [[gemini-enterprise-agent-platform]], Anthropic [[claudeforce]], Salesforce [[agentforce]] — as *canonical early-Nov-2026 cross-vendor enterprise-agent-platform canonical anchor cluster*. For IBM, Imagine Enterprise productizes the [[ibm-bob]] AI-coding-partner lineage into a broader *build-operate-govern three-primitive platform scope* that extends IBM's enterprise-SDLC-tier footprint into cross-functional industry-specific agent workflows.
