## Summary

Robostral Navigate is [[mistral]]'s first publicly framed robotics-tier model, released in the July 11 2026 cycle as *"a new model built for factories, warehouses, and industrial automation"* per Superhuman + NLP Newsletter. The framing marks Mistral's push into the *physical-AI + industrial automation* vertical, structurally distinct from its prior LLM + code + OCR + verification-agent product lines ([[leanstral]] / [[mistral-ocr-4]] / Mistral Vibe). A defining architectural claim per Superhuman is that Robostral Navigate is *"built to work across hardware from multiple suppliers"* — cross-supplier hardware portability positions it in explicit contrast to hardware-locked physical-AI stacks (e.g. NVIDIA's [[isaac-groot]] reference design on Unitree H2 body + Blackwell GPU).

Robostral Navigate lands in the same cycle cluster as (a) Hyundai/Boston Dynamics **Atlas humanoid** at the 2026 World Cup halftime, (b) UC San Diego's first-ever surgeries by teleoperated humanoids, (c) Rémi Cadène's ex-Tesla-scientist **Northstar** faceless humanoid targeting European factories, (d) 1X's tendon-driven-hand demo, and (e) the Netherlands humanoid robotics center opening — the densest single-cycle European-humanoid + industrial-automation signal cluster in this wiki since 2026-06-21.
