## Summary

Ox Alpha is a **mysterious, free-for-a-limited-time reasoning model** that appeared on [[openrouter]] in August 2026 with no known creator or developer disclosure. The model is positioned as a *coding + sustained-agentic-work + production-workloads* reasoning model, suited for long-horizon software engineering, complex reasoning, and workflows that combine text with visual context. It supports a **1 million token context**, ingests text + images + video, and produces up to **131K output tokens**.

Independent testing reports Ox Alpha solving **8 out of 10 tasks on a deterministic 10-task DeepSWE subset**, outperforming [[claude-fable-5]], [[glm-5-3]], [[grok-4-6]], and [[gpt-5-6]] Sol on that specific benchmark. On the Kingbench eval it scored **87.5%** — second only to [[glm-5-3]] at 91.25%, ahead of Opus 4.8 and Qwen 3.8 Max. There is no official benchmark sheet, no Artificial Analysis row, and no DeepSWE public-leaderboard entry.

Behavioral analysis suggests Ox Alpha produces **identical tokenization patterns to GLM-5V-Turbo**, which has led to speculation that Ox Alpha may be a [[zhipu-ai]] variant or spin-off. This tokenization-fingerprint approach is a canonical mid-2026 *forensic origin-attribution* technique for stealth-launched anonymous models on multi-vendor routing substrates like OpenRouter.
