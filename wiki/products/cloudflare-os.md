## Summary

Cloudflare OS is [[cloudflare]]'s August 2026 launch of an **"operating system for AI productivity"** — an **open-source** platform that packages an **agent chat UI**, **sandboxed application development**, and a **security framework** into a single stack customers can fork and customize for their own company. Positioning is company-tier AI-agent workbench (build + deploy AI-agent-driven internal tools) rather than end-user consumer surface. Extends [[cloudflare]]'s mid-2026 agent-web-infrastructure suite (Monetization Gateway + Precursor + Wallets + Codex) into a customer-facing composable-productivity substrate.
