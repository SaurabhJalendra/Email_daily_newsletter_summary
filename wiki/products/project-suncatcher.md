## Summary

Project Suncatcher is [[google]]'s early-November 2025 research effort to build machine-learning infrastructure that runs entirely off solar power in space. The program explores putting TPU-based AI compute on satellites in low-Earth orbit, effectively creating data centers on-orbit that bypass terrestrial grid and cooling constraints. It sits in the same broad "compute supercycle" conversation as hyperscaler terrestrial buildouts, but reframes the problem as an energy-and-location optimization rather than a silicon-supply one.
