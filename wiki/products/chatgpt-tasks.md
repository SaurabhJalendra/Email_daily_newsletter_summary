## Summary

ChatGPT Scheduled Tasks is [[openai]]'s scheduled-tasks system inside [[chatgpt]] that lets users set **one-off or recurring tasks**, at a **specific time or across a window** like "morning" or "evening". ChatGPT can now also **proactively check the web or connected apps and ping the user** — a structural shift from session-based assistant-on-demand to **agent-on-a-schedule** (World of AI framing). The launch **replaces Pulse**, OpenAI's daily-summary feature, which is being **sunset within 14 days**.
