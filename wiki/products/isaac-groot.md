## Summary

The **Isaac GR00T Reference Humanoid Robot** is [[nvidia]]'s research-grade humanoid platform unveiled in the 2026-06-07 morning cycle. It is positioned as an **open reference design** for academic researchers and partners: a **Unitree H2 humanoid body** paired with an **onboard Blackwell GPU**, providing a unified hardware + software stack on which to develop humanoid skills (perception, manipulation, locomotion, planning). NVIDIA plans to extend the reference design to partners in the **US, Europe, and South Korea**, mirroring the open-platform playbook NVIDIA has used for [[nemotron-3]] and [[nvidia-cosmos]] — supply the open base, let third parties build vertical humanoids on top.

It is paired in the same cycle with **a NVIDIA full-stack platform for ramping development from simulation to real-world deployment** (sim-to-real), positioning [[nvidia]] across both the embodied-AI substrate (Cosmos world models + Isaac GR00T hardware) and the developer / academic surface that builds atop it. The launch lands on the same day as Sam Altman's [[alfred-robotics]] investment and OpenAI's robotics-program restart — concentrating the [[humanoid-robotics]] cycle in a single news window.
