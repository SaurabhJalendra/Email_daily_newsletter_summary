## Summary

GPT-6.1 Sol is [[openai]]'s Sep 29 2026 DevDay upgrade to [[gpt-6-sol]] — a lower-cost GPT-6 family model designed for agentic coding, computer use, document understanding, and complex professional workflows. OpenAI positions it as approaching [[openai-astra]] (GPT-6 Astra) performance at **one-fifth the cost** ($2/M input + $10/M output — same as GPT-6 Sol's pricing). Reported benchmarks include tying Astra on DeepSWE, beating [[claude-opus-5-5]] on AutomationBench at 1/3 the cost, and landing 2.1 points short of Astra on OSWorld 2.0 at ~1/7 the cost; error rate is within 1.9% of Astra across reasoning settings.

Structurally, GPT-6.1 Sol reflects a change in OpenAI's product strategy: [[gpt-6-1-astra]] was expected but has not launched (cancelled 09-29 for safety failures per prior ingest), leaving Astra as the higher-capability option and Sol as the cost-performance alternative. It pairs cycle-structurally with same-cycle [[claude-sonnet-5-5]] as *twin frontier-lab cheaper-mid-tier-matches-flagship-on-agentic canonical anchor pair*.
