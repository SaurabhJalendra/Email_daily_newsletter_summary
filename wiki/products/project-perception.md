## Summary

Project Perception is [[microsoft]]'s July 2026 forthcoming multi-model AI security tool, framed by newsletter coverage as a direct competitive undercut of [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-mythos]] cybersecurity-tier positioning. The name and multi-model architecture were first publicly framed by Evolving AI Insights on 2026-07-20: *"Microsoft is preparing 'Project Perception,' a multi-model AI security tool to undercut Anthropic's Mythos."*

The "multi-model" framing implies Microsoft is architecting Project Perception as an ensemble/router across multiple frontier substrates rather than a single-model tier — likely combining [[gpt-5-6]] (via OpenAI Foundry access), [[mai-thinking-1]] (Microsoft's in-house reasoning family), and possibly Anthropic Claude via Azure Foundry into a single security-oriented product surface. This is structurally distinct from Anthropic's single-model Mythos-tier positioning where Mythos itself is the differentiated capability tier.

Project Perception is Microsoft's first publicly framed direct-competitor product to Anthropic's Mythos cybersecurity offering — extending the [[claude-mythos]] arc from *unique cybersecurity-frontier-positioning* into a *contested cybersecurity-tier positioning*.
