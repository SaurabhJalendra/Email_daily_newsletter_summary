## Summary

Workspace Agents is [[openai]]'s April 2026 ChatGPT feature for **shared team agents** — agents that handle complex tasks and long-running workflows on behalf of an entire team rather than a single user. It launches alongside [[gpt-5-5]] (2026-04-25) and represents OpenAI's first explicitly multi-user agent surface inside ChatGPT, extending the parallel-agent surface introduced in the 2026-04-16 [[openai-codex]] superapp release into team collaboration territory. Coverage frames it as the team-collaboration counterpart to Anthropic's [[claude-managed-agents]] (cloud-hosted agents at scale).
