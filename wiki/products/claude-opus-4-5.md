## Summary

Claude Opus 4.5 (also styled "Claude 4.5 Opus") was Anthropic's late-November 2025 flagship Claude release — the model that beat the prior SWE-bench Verified high water mark (80.9%) and posted leading scores on T2 tool-use benchmarks (88.9% Retail, 98.2% Telecom). Beyond coding, it advanced graduate-level reasoning, visual reasoning, computer use, and multilingual Q&A. It launched into the same competitive window as Google's [[gemini-3]] and OpenAI's [[gpt-5-1-codex-max]], and its broader rollout (Nov 30) coincided with OpenAI's "Code Red" reaction to Gemini 3. Opus 4.5 was the immediate predecessor to [[claude-opus-4-7]] (Apr 2026).
