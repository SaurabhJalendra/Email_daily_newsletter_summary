## Summary

Claude Skills is [[anthropic]]'s framework for packaging reusable, composable capabilities that Claude can load at runtime. It surfaced in early October 2025 as part of a wider trend of model vendors shipping first-party agent scaffolding (alongside [[agentkit]] from OpenAI and [[jules]] from Google). On 2025-10-17 Anthropic extended the surface with "Agent Skills" for modular task automation — reinforcing Skills as Claude's preferred agent-building primitive.
