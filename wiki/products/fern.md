## Summary

**Fern** is [[mirendil-ai]]'s first publicly announced LLM — distinct from the earlier [[mirendil-base]] coding model — targeting **high-volume agentic tasks** with a context window of **up to 10 million tokens**. The 10M-token context ceiling places Fern in the top tier of long-context frontier models (sits alongside Gemini 4 Argon's 1M output + Google's prior 2M-input Gemini 1.5 Pro + several 1M-context Chinese open-weight models).

Positioning: Mirendil frames Fern as agentic-workload-optimized rather than general-purpose chat — complementing the lean-pretraining-tier Mirendil-Base coding model with a long-context agent model. Together they form the first two publicly announced Mirendil model-family members since the Jun 2026 $200M seed round and extend the Jack-Clark-elevated *"what RSI means"* framing on the Mirendil-side canonical arc into a *concrete-two-shipped-models cohort tier*.
