## Summary

Claude Slides is Anthropic's September 2026 beta presentation-authoring product surface, launched as part of the unified-Claude-app collapse that merged Chat + Cowork + Design into one interface. It ships in beta alongside [[claude-docs]] — the twin productization move canonicalizes Anthropic's push into the productivity-suite tier substrate competitive with Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint.

Structurally, Claude Slides extends Anthropic's multi-cycle vertical-productization arc into a *native-Anthropic presentation-authoring substrate*, sitting alongside [[claude-docs]] and [[claude-design]] under the unified Claude app. Given Anthropic's prior [[claude-design]] positioning (visual-design substrate) plus the Evolving AI Insights framing that groups Docs + Slides + Design as three surfaces merged into one interface, Claude Slides likely leverages Claude Design's rendering-and-layout substrate underneath.
