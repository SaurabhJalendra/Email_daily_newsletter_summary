## Summary

Gemini Notebook is [[google]]'s rebrand of NotebookLM — a grounded-summarization, citation-supported, multi-source-synthesis research and knowledge-work tool now integrated across the broader Gemini stack (Gemini app + Google Search). The rename lands as *"part of a wider cleanup of Google's AI branding ahead of the broader Gemini 3.5 rollout and deeper integration of Gemini models into productivity tools"* (researchFindings). Product focus continues to be grounded summarization, source citation, and cross-document synthesis for research workflows.

Positioning-wise, Gemini Notebook competes against [[chatgpt]] Company Knowledge, [[perplexity-brain]]'s persistent-context-graph, and [[claude-cowork]]'s local-file agent as an *AI-native research-notebook workflow layer*. Mindstream frames it as a *"save 10+ hours per week"* productivity tool that automates project organization, team-training material generation, and complex-image/content decoding.
