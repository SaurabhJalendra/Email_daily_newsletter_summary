## Summary

Company Knowledge is a [[chatgpt]] capability launched 2025-10-28 that connects the assistant to workplace apps — Slack, Google Drive, SharePoint, GitHub, Gmail, and more — so that responses are grounded in internal company data with citations. It is OpenAI's clearest enterprise-RAG product, replacing ad-hoc connector setups with a unified, time-aware index built around source-cited generation. Coverage frames it as the OpenAI counterpart to [[claude-for-excel]] (released the same week) — both labs targeting the workplace data layer in parallel.
