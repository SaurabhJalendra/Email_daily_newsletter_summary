## Summary

Muse Spark 1.3 is [[meta]]'s September 2026 flagship LLM release from Meta Superintelligence Labs — the first Meta model to publicly match OpenAI's [[gpt-5-6]] Sol on frontier coding and agentic benchmarks, and #3 in the world on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (AAII). It is exposed through [[muse-code]] (Meta's coding-agent environment) and the [[meta]] Model API, with rollout to Meta's consumer social platforms staged behind the developer launch. Zuckerberg framed the release as "the biggest jump we've made so far on coding and agentic work" and signaled that Muse Spark open-weights releases and the frontier-tier successor codenamed 🍉 ([[watermelon]]) are next in the pipeline.

The 1.3 release is positioned as an incremental but strategically significant update over [[muse-spark]] 1.2 — reportedly using ~25% fewer tokens than its predecessor and shipped with a ">90% training discount" pricing framing that Zuckerberg described as "frontier performance almost too cheap to meter." Combined with the AINews cycle-headline framing Meta Superintelligence as "the newest Frontier Lab," Muse Spark 1.3 canonicalizes Meta's arrival at parity with the OpenAI + Anthropic + Google frontier cohort on coding-tier substrate.
