## Summary

LaMem-VLA is a research architecture introducing a **dual latent memory vault** for vision-language-action (VLA) models targeting long-horizon manipulation. Published as an arXiv preprint circulated at end-July 2026, it sets SOTA on canonical long-horizon manipulation benchmarks: **97.6% on LIBERO** and **73.9% on SimplerEnv-Bridge** — a **+16.6-point gain over CogACT**. The dual-latent-memory-vault primitive extends the [[ai-memory]] research arc from LLM-tier into VLA-tier, providing a memory-substrate architectural anchor for extended, multi-step physical tasks.
