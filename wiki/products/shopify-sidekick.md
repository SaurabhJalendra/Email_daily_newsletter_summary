## Summary

Shopify Sidekick is Shopify's AI-enabled commerce assistant embedded directly in the Shopify admin, using natural language to help merchants with guidance, content generation, analytics, operational tasks, and action execution (running reports, creating segments, modifying store settings). Shopify frames Sidekick not as an assistant but as an **AI co-founder** — one that not only answers questions but executes actions on the merchant's behalf.

Sidekick is designed as a self-improving system: it uses past conversations and recent admin activity to tailor responses, and is trained via a **continual-learning loop** that automatically turns real-world failures into training signals via a self-healing pipeline, daily fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning (GRPO). Feature-tier: voice interaction + screen sharing + 20 languages.
