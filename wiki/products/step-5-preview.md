## Summary

Step 5 Preview is **StepFun**'s Oct 2026 preview-tier LLM, framed as *near-Opus-5 intelligence* on a 1M-token context window for coding agents, financial analysis, and long-running work. StepFun reports **80% lower input pricing and 89% lower output pricing** vs comparable frontier models. AINews reports Step 5 Preview scores **ahead of [[kimi-k3]] and [[glm-5-3]] on DeepSWE**, positioning it in the strongest-Chinese-frontier-tier alongside DeepSeek V4.1 Flash + GLM-5.3 Flash + MiMo V2.6 + Hy4 Preview.

Distributed via a six-harness cohort day-one — **OpenRouter + OpenCode + Kilo Code + Cline + omp + Hermes Agent** — Step 5 productizes at the *open-router distribution-tier* substrate rather than first-party-cloud-only tier.
