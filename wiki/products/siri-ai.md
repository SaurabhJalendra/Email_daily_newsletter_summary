## Summary

Siri AI is [[apple]]'s Sep 2026 ground-up generative-AI rebuild of Siri, positioned as the conversational-assistant core of the broader **Apple Intelligence** platform. It shifts Siri from a simple voice-command helper into a full-fledged AI assistant with a dedicated app, capable of natural back-and-forth dialogue, understanding on-device personal context (messages, emails, photos), responding to what's on screen, generating images, and fetching up-to-date information from the web. It launches as a beta on **September 14, 2026** with iOS 27, with **daily usage caps** and future **paid access** via iCloud+ signaling Apple's mid-2026 pivot toward usage-based AI monetization.

Under the hood, Apple is reportedly running a custom ~1.2T-parameter version of Google's **Gemini** foundation model through its "Private Cloud Compute" architecture — data processed in the cloud is inaccessible to Apple or third parties. It launches first in English on iOS 27 + other 2026 OS releases, with **French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish** following shortly. **EU and China are excluded at launch** due to regulatory and infrastructure concerns. Most advanced features are gated to newer high-end iPhones (16+, Air, 17 Pro, [[iphone-duo]]) — Siri AI serves as headline differentiator for Apple's next-gen hardware.
