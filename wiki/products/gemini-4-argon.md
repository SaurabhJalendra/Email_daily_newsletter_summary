## Summary

Gemini 4 Argon is [[google]]'s cybersecurity-vertical Gemini 4 variant launched on Sep 30 2026, initially restricted to cyber defenders and security firms. Google claims the system delivers coding performance ahead of [[openai-astra]] (GPT-6 Astra) in relevant evaluations, with specific strength in finding software flaws, confirming their validity, and patching them without human intervention. First concrete deployment framed in-cycle: Wiz used Argon to catch a critical flaw in hospital software.

Structurally, Argon is Google's first-in-wiki concrete Gemini-4-family variant to ship — it reaches partners *before* the broader Gemini 4 release that Koray Kavukcuoglu disclosed as "in post-training" on 09-26-evening. The staged partner-only availability signals Google is adopting a Trusted-Access / limited-partner-cohort release pattern for high-capability cybersecurity models, structurally parallel to the mid-2026 [[gpt-5-6-cyber]] + [[mai-cyber-1-flash]] cybersecurity-vertical frontier-model cohort.
