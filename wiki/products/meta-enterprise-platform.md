## Summary

Meta Enterprise Platform is [[meta]]'s Sep 2026 enterprise-vertical business unit selling Muse models and agents to companies for customer service and daily work automation. Launched on Sep 29, 2026 (the same cycle as [[claude-sonnet-5-5]] and OpenAI's [[gpt-6-1-astra]] cancellation), it is led by **former MongoDB CEO CJ Desai** and bundles three products: **Muse** (foundation models), **Meta Business Agent** (which can use apps and a browser to complete tasks), and **Muse Code** (for developers writing software).

The launch canonicalizes Meta's productization pivot from consumer-tier Muse app + open-weights into a *concrete enterprise-tier three-product-bundle* alongside [[chatgpt-work]] + [[claude-cowork]] + [[kimi-work]] frontier-lab enterprise-agentic-workspace cohort. It arrives during a saturated late-Sep-2026 productization cycle across three frontier labs — Anthropic launching Sonnet 5.5, OpenAI shelving GPT-6.1 Astra, and Meta launching the enterprise unit.
