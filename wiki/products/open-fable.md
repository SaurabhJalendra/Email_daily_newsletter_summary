## Summary

Open Fable is a planned **open-weights, Fable-class large language model** that [[zhipu-ai]] has signaled as the next step beyond [[glm-5-2]], with a target window of **December 2026** per Z.ai forecast (first publicly framed via AINews "GLM > GPT? GLM-5.2 passes vibe check; Z.ai forecasts Open Fable by December" 2026-06-19-evening). The model is positioned to **match or surpass [[claude-fable-5]]** in capability while keeping weights openly available (likely under a permissive license similar to GLM-5.2's MIT). The framing is explicitly a response to the US government's June 2026 export-control directive that took [[claude-fable-5]] and [[claude-mythos]] offline globally — Open Fable is positioned as the open alternative to tightly controlled Western frontier models, structurally aimed at the gap left by the Fable/Mythos pull.

The term *Fable-class* has emerged in industry discourse — popularized by Elon Musk — as shorthand for extremely advanced AI systems; Z.ai leadership has explicitly tied its roadmap to reaching this level. If shipped on the forecast timeline and if it delivers on the capability framing, Open Fable could accelerate global adoption of open-weight LLMs and intensify the US-China AI competition, giving enterprises and researchers outside the US a high-end alternative to closed frontier systems.
