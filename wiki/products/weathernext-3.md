## Summary

WeatherNext 3 is Google DeepMind's newest weather-forecasting model, introduced in early September 2026. Its distinguishing feature is the use of **live satellite images** as input and an **hourly refresh cadence** for forecasts — a structural step-up from prior WeatherNext generations that used ERA5 reanalysis input at coarser refresh cadences. On launch, WeatherNext 3 took the top spot on scoreboards against rival models and against both the U.S. and European national weather services, canonicalizing Google DeepMind's *scoreboard-top-tier vs national-weather-services* substrate at the frontier-AI weather-forecasting tier.

Structurally, WeatherNext 3 sits complementary to [[weathernext-2]] (open-source hurricane-lead-time-extender, Aug 2026) and [[weathernext-cyclones]] (track-and-intensity hurricane forecasting), completing Google DeepMind's three-substrate weather-forecasting product family. The live-satellite-driven-hourly-refresh substrate positions WeatherNext 3 as the productization of DeepMind's *real-time-remote-sensing-input weather-AI thesis*.
