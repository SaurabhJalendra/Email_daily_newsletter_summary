## Summary

Gemini 3 is [[google]]'s flagship next-generation [[gemini]] model. It leaked on 2025-10-13, was officially confirmed on 2025-10-20, and shipped on 2025-11-18 across the Gemini app, Android AI Mode, Chrome, Search, AI Studio, and Vertex AI. The launch model handles up to 1.5M tokens, includes a Deep Think reasoning mode, and ships alongside [[antigravity]] — a free agent-first IDE built around it. At release Gemini 3 set new highs on LMArena (1501 Elo), Humanity's Last Exam (45.8%), AIME 2025 (95%), GPQA Diamond (91.9%), MMMU-Pro (81%), ScreenSpot-Pro (72.7%), and LiveCodeBench Pro (2,439); it tied GPT-5.1 on SWE-Bench Verified (76.2% vs 76.3%). A "Pro" SKU tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Coverage frames Gemini 3 as Google's comeback in the AI race against [[openai]] and [[anthropic]].
