## Summary

OpenAI Deployment Company (DeployCo) is OpenAI's $4B professional-services subsidiary, announced 2026-05-12 as a direct competitive response to [[anthropic]]'s $1.5B Wall Street joint venture announced eight days earlier. DeployCo embeds specialized AI engineers ("Forward Deployed Engineers" — borrowing the Palantir term) directly inside Fortune 500 customers to redesign workflows around frontier OpenAI models and ship the integrations themselves rather than leave deployment to client engineering teams. Initial backing of $4B from TPG, Brookfield Asset Management, Advent International, and Bain Capital at a $10B valuation, with OpenAI as majority owner. Targets roughly 2,000 firms initially, focusing on finance, supply chains, customer support, operations, and internal systems. First M&A move: acquisition of London-based consulting firm Tomoro (~150 deployment engineers).

The launch crystallizes a frontier-lab pattern shift: enterprise AI is no longer a self-serve API motion but a professional-services build-out, with both OpenAI and Anthropic spinning up dedicated PE-backed deployment subsidiaries and major-lab competitors (Google, Anthropic) hiring Forward Deployed Engineers under the same playbook.
