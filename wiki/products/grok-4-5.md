## Summary

Grok 4.5 is xAI's next-generation Grok model, announced by Elon Musk on 2026-06-29 (per TLDR AI 2026-06-30-morning) as having entered private beta at SpaceX and Tesla. The model is built on a **1.5T-parameter "V9" foundation model**, and Musk-cited early evaluations place its capability "near or above" Anthropic's Opus tier. The private-beta-inside-Musk-portfolio-companies framing is structurally consistent with prior xAI roll-out patterns — using Tesla/SpaceX as the captive internal-customer surface before broader external release (cf the 2026-05 [[grok-build]] CLI-coding agent for SuperGrok Heavy subscribers). The 1.5T-parameter scale puts the underlying foundation model in the same scale tier as the broader frontier (cf [[claude-opus-4-8]], [[gpt-5-6]] Sol, [[gemini-3-5-flash]]).
