## Summary

Poke is a third-party AI assistant that, on **2026-06-04**, became the **first AI agent approved by Apple for the Messages for Business platform** — making it the first general-purpose AI assistant a user can chat with inside iMessage itself (no browser/app handoff). From within the Apple Messages app, users can have Poke send emails, set reminders, generate images, and perform other multi-step assistant tasks. The launch matters less for Poke's own capability set and more as the first concrete signal that **Apple is opening Messages as a distribution surface for third-party AI** — a structural shift adjacent to the broader Apple third-party-AI-provider story (Apple's separately reported moves to bring Google [[gemini]] and Anthropic [[claude-opus-4-7]]/[[claude-opus-4-8]] alongside [[chatgpt]] into Siri, and the rumored ~$1B/year custom Gemini Siri deal).
