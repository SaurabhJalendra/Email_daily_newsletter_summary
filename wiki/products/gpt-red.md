## Summary

GPT-Red is [[openai]]'s **dedicated adversarial red-team AI** — a model trained specifically to attack OpenAI's own production models and identify vulnerabilities before external attackers can. According to Evolving AI Insights' cycle-defining framing (2026-07-16), GPT-Red **learns through self-play**, was trained at the **compute scale of OpenAI's largest model runs**, and achieves a reported **84% success rate on test scenarios versus 13% for human testers** — a ~6.5× uplift over human red-teamers.

The launch positions OpenAI's safety-evaluation pipeline as a *coordinated adversarial-substrate* rather than manual red-teaming, and extends OpenAI's cybersecurity stack ([[gpt-5-4-cyber]] / [[gpt-5-5-cyber]] / [[openai-daybreak]] / [[codex-security]] / [[aardvark]]) into a *frontier-compute-scale self-hacking pre-release evaluation model* tier. If the 84%-vs-13% numbers hold, GPT-Red fundamentally changes AI-lab safety-evaluation economics: human red-teaming shifts from the throughput tier to the ceiling-check tier. Same cycle, [[anthropic]] announces a 32-role safety-hiring push (nuclear + explosives + cybercrime + fraud experts, mid-upper $200K) — reads as two US frontier labs simultaneously scaling their pre-release adversarial-evaluation stack.
