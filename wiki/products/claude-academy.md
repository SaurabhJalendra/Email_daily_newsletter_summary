## Summary

Claude Academy is [[anthropic]]'s AI-fluency school, opened for free to everyone in August 2026. The curriculum is built around the **4D AI Fluency Framework** and emphasizes **mindsets over prompting tricks** — covering how to verify AI-generated content, how to disclose AI collaboration to colleagues and customers, and how to build durable practical intuition for working with LLMs. Positioned as a broad on-ramp for individuals and organizations that complements Anthropic's paid programs ([[claude-corps]] + [[anthropic-fellows]]).
