## Summary

OpenAI Prism is a free AI-native workspace launched in June 2026, powered by [[gpt-5-2]]. Per AI Breakfast and Simple.ai cycle-coverage, Prism lets researchers convert whiteboard sketches into code and chat with an AI that understands the full context of manuscripts. It is positioned as a research-and-creative-work surface distinct from ChatGPT's general assistant mode.

Prism extends OpenAI's pattern of vertical surfaces for distinct work contexts — [[openai-workspace-agents]] for team collaboration, [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] for workplace-RAG, and now Prism for manuscript-and-sketch research workflows. Its free-tier launch contrasts with [[claude-cowork]] (paid Anthropic surface) and represents OpenAI's continued effort to expand free-tier surface area while monetizing through ads and enterprise upsell.
