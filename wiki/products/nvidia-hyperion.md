## Summary

**NVIDIA Hyperion** is NVIDIA's AV (autonomous vehicle) and robotaxi fleet-building platform, canonicalized at the GTC Berlin 2026 keynote (Oct 21) as the substrate on which **European mobility leaders are building safe autonomous vehicles and robotaxi fleets**. NVIDIA frames Hyperion as the end-to-end *build + validate + deploy* substrate for AVs at scale — spanning in-vehicle compute (DRIVE platform-tier predecessors), sim-to-real validation ([[nvidia-cosmos]] world models + Isaac robotics-platform expansion), and deployment-tier operations.

The GTC Berlin canonicalization pairs cycle-structurally with the broader keynote cohort of [[vera-rubin-dsx-pod]] (modular AI data-center POD), [[nvidia-minerva]] (AI-factory AIOps), expanded NVIDIA Isaac open-robotics platform, [[openshell]] (agent-safety reference architecture), [[rtx-spark]] (local-AI laptops), and the "five-layer AI cake" pedagogy (energy → accelerated computing → AI factories → models → applications). Hyperion productizes the *applications* layer in the automotive + mobility vertical, with explicit European-mobility-leader customer-cohort framing.
