## Summary

GPT-5.1 is the mid-November 2025 upgrade to [[openai]]'s flagship ChatGPT model family. It was first spotted on 2025-11-07 as an unlisted "GPT-5.1 Thinking" variant and officially rolled out on 2025-11-14 with two explicit modes — "Instant" for fast everyday responses and "Thinking" for deeper reasoning on complex tasks — plus eight personality presets for tone and style. Coverage framed 5.1 as a "full reboot of the ChatGPT experience" around conversational warmth rather than raw capability jumps, and OpenAI paired the release with a public prompting guide emphasising iterative refinement.
