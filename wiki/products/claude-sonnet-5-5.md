## Summary

Claude Sonnet 5.5 is [[anthropic]]'s Sep 2026 second model in the Claude 5.5 family, launched alongside a plugin-portal 110x growth signal, a physics-world-record announcement (Claude Science computing a nine-loop N=4 super-Yang-Mills amplitude on 96 CPUs for ~$1-2K), and the earlier [[claude-opus-5-5]] frontier-tier release from 09-23-evening. Sonnet 5.5 is described as **up to 30% faster and 30% cheaper per task than Sonnet 5 while retaining the same listed token prices** — i.e., cost savings come through efficiency (fewer tokens / faster throughput per task) rather than list-price cuts. TAAFT frames it as "the everyday model" with a 30% faster writing speed than its predecessor.

The launch positions Sonnet 5.5 as the *cost-efficient near-frontier* Claude within the Claude 5.5 family, complementing [[claude-opus-5-5]] at the frontier-agentic-tier and continuing the mid-2026 Sonnet-as-everyday-workhorse arc from [[claude-sonnet-5]] (whose $2/M input + $10/M output introductory pricing was made permanent in 08-24-evening). It intensifies competition with same-cycle OpenAI GPT-6 Sol/Luna as the frontier-lab price-and-speed race enters late-Sep-2026.
