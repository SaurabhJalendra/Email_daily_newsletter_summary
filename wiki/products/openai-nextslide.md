## Summary

**NextSlide** is a **presentation-authoring startup acquired by [[openai]]** in mid-2026, purchased to add polished slide-deck generation to ChatGPT — turning raw model outputs into presentation-ready decks. The acquisition is framed as one of OpenAI's steps toward building a **full office suite inside ChatGPT**, alongside prior productivity-tier products like [[chatgpt-work]] (agentic ChatGPT workspace), [[openai-prism]] (workspace/research), [[chatgpt-tasks]] (scheduled agents), and [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] (workplace-RAG).

The move mirrors the broader mid-2026 pattern of frontier labs bundling *content-authoring surfaces* (word-processor, spreadsheet, slide-deck) directly into the assistant surface rather than routing users to third-party tools — an implicit competitive posture against Microsoft 365 + Google Workspace + Canva at the *AI-native productivity substrate* tier.
