## Summary

Helix World is Noiz's **real-time interactive audio-visual world model** — a system that generates explorable scenes paired with spatial soundtracks. It extends the predominantly-video-only [[world-models]] arc (dominated by [[marble]] from [[world-labs]], [[nvidia-cosmos]], [[gwm-worlds-2]], and [[odyssey-3]]) into an *audio-visual canonical anchor tier* by generating audio alongside geometry/pixels and binding the soundtrack to scene geometry rather than overlaying it post-hoc. The explicit *real-time interactive* framing positions Helix World as a canonical substrate for interactive media, games, and VR/AR experiences rather than offline cinematic rendering.
