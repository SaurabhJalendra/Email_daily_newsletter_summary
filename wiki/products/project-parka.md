## Summary

Project Parka is [[anthropic]]'s **Mac-first meeting-recorder feature** that captures system + microphone audio during a meeting, streams speaker-attributed transcripts in real time, and creates *runnable work for Claude's agents* downstream. The core substrate move is turning a meeting into a *full implementation prompt* — the meeting notes are not just archived, they become executable agent-work items that Claude can act on.

Parka sits at the intersection of the meeting-recorder-tier (Otter, Fireflies, [[granola]]), the Anthropic Mac-productization arc ([[claude-cowork]], Claude Desktop, [[claude-tag]]), and the [[computer-use]] discourse. It is the first Anthropic product explicitly designed to convert *unstructured spoken conversation* into *structured agent-executable tasks*. The 2026-08-22 disclosure leaves open whether Claude begins acting on those tasks *automatically* or *waits for user approval* — a key ambiguity that determines whether Parka lands as a productivity-tier or approval-gated-workflow-tier substrate.
