## Summary

Copilot Cowork is [[microsoft]]'s Claude-powered multi-step task-execution layer for Microsoft 365, launched March 11 2026 as a limited research preview. Built on [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-cowork]] technology, it lets users hand off requests that get turned into plans and executed across Outlook, Teams, Excel, PowerPoint, and Word — with checkpoints for human approval. Microsoft will bundle it into a new $99/user E7 tier and has positioned it as how Claude reaches "90% of Fortune 500 offices" via Microsoft 365 distribution. It uses Work IQ to coordinate emails, meetings, and files — performing actions like rescheduling meetings and preparing briefing documents while keeping user control.
