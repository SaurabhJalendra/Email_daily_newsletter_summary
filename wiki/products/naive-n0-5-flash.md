## Summary

Naive-N0.5-Flash is Beijing-based NaiveAI's first widely reported open-weights model, released **September 27, 2026** under the **MIT license**. It is a mixture-of-experts model with **309B total parameters** and **15.5B active parameters** (~5% active-parameter ratio), and ships with a **native 1M-token context window**. The permissive license, long context, and sparse-activation design position it as a practical option for large-codebase, document, and agent workloads, and place NaiveAI directly in the mid-2026 Chinese-open-frontier cohort alongside [[deepseek]] + [[zhipu-ai]] + [[alibaba]] + [[xiaomi]] + [[moonshot-ai]].

The release lands during a broader inflection in open-model enterprise adoption. Per same-cycle Financial Times reporting summarized by AI Weekly: open-weight models accounted for **56% of Vercel AI Gateway tokens in August 2026** (up from 7% in December 2025), handled about **40% of AT&T's AI workloads**, and open-model mentions on U.S. earnings calls increased **sixfold year over year** — extending the multi-cycle 30%-of-US-companies-weekly-token-usage arc into a concrete-Vercel-Gateway-inflection + concrete-AT&T-workload + concrete-6×-YoY earnings-call-mention adoption-substrate anchor cluster.
