## Summary

Alexa Tablet is Amazon's new Android-based tablet line announced October 8, 2026, replacing the Fire Tablet brand. The lineup spans three SKUs — the 8.7-inch Alexa Tablet 8, the 11-inch Alexa Tablet 11, and the 12-inch Alexa Tablet 12 Pro — priced from $229.99 to $499.99, or $549.99 with the Pro's Nanomatte display. Unlike Fire tablets, Alexa Tablets ship with full Google Play Store access (Gmail, YouTube, etc.), aluminum designs, and optional keyboard and pen accessories. The devices began shipping October 14, 2026 in the US, Canada, and Mexico, with European availability following shortly after.

The AI/ML significance of the line is Alexa+, Amazon's generative-AI assistant, which is positioned as a delivery platform for Amazon's broader AI strategy — answering questions, controlling tablet functions, and executing natural-language actions. Reporting frames the Alexa Tablet as a credible iPad rival at a materially lower price point and as a strategic shift from low-cost Amazon-centric Fire hardware toward premium devices competing with Apple and Samsung while using Android compatibility to reduce the app-availability gap.
