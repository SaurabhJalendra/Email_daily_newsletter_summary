## Summary

Doug is [[openai]]'s **massive pre-training frontier model**, revealed as the second half of a two-model frontier split alongside [[openai-astra]]. Where Astra is characterized as a long-horizon multi-agent family, Doug is framed as a **raw-scaling base-capability run** — a large-parameter pre-training effort focused on foundational model quality rather than agentic orchestration. The split reads as OpenAI separating the *scale-the-substrate* concern from the *safety-around-autonomous-cyber-misuse* concern that has forced Astra into locked-weights sandbox development.

Doug has no announced release date, no confirmed parameter count, and no public technical documentation as of first-mention. Third-party reporting places it as a possible late-2026 target but that timeline is unconfirmed. It is likely to be the successor to [[gpt-5-6]] on the *pre-training-scaling axis* if the branding lands as GPT-6, though the GPT-6 label has separately been speculated for Astra.
