## Summary

Meta VR Glasses are Meta's Sep 2026 lightweight VR headset announced at Meta Connect 2026. Priced at **$1,299.99** with a **spring 2027** launch window, the device weighs approximately **100 grams** — its processor, cooling fan, battery, and other computing components live in an **external pack** to keep the eyewear light. The glasses carry many of the same features as Meta's existing Quest headsets — private cinema, multi-monitor workstation, and game-console experiences in a wearable form factor. Meta AI is integrated into the operating system, supporting **voice + eye tracking + hand gestures** for virtual screens, apps, search, entertainment, and spatial computing without traditional controllers. [[meta-muse-agent]] is coming to the glasses, giving users hands-free access to a personal AI assistant that can manage tasks, plans, and daily work. Meta VR Glasses shift Meta's AI strategy from phones and chat windows into **context-aware, multimodal wearables**, competing with Apple Vision Pro + Google glasses + other AI-enabled spatial-computing entrants.
