## Summary

**NVIDIA Minerva** is NVIDIA's Oct 2026 AIOps (AI operations) platform for **monitoring AI-factory infrastructure and workloads**. It was introduced at GTC Berlin 2026 (Oct 20-22) as part of the broader NVIDIA AI-factory substrate — alongside the new [[vera-rubin-dsx-pod]] modular partner-built data-center design, expanded [[nvidia-cosmos]] world-model-development tools, and local AI systems including [[rtx-spark]] laptops, [[dgx-spark]] desktops, and DGX Station.

Minerva productizes NVIDIA's AI-factory substrate at the *observability-and-operations* layer, graduating the multi-cycle NVIDIA developer-tooling cluster (NeMo Relay for tracing agent-harness behavior, NeMo-DCR for RL-training-refit speedup, NVIDIA AI Cluster Runtime v1.0 for version-locked K8s recipes) into a single named-AIOps-platform tier. Its launch pairs cycle-structurally with the GTC Berlin keynote cohort of [[openshell]] (agent-safety reference architecture), [[nvidia-hyperion]] (European AV + robotaxi platform), and the broader AI-factories layer of Jensen Huang's "five-layer AI cake" pedagogy.
