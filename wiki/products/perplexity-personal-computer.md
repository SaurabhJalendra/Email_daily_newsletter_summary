## Summary

Perplexity Personal Computer is a local, always-on variant of Perplexity's Computer AI agent, launched March 13 2026. It runs the Comet Assistant continuously on a dedicated Apple Mac mini, giving the agent 24/7 access to local files, installed apps, and active sessions while staying controllable from any device. Positioning: a more secure, locally controlled rival to [[openclaw]] — framed as a "persistent digital proxy of you." Safeguards include tracked activity logs and a kill-switch to terminate the system remotely. Coverage observed that dedicated Mac minis have become the de-facto hardware substrate for the local-agent era.
