## Summary

Grok 4.6 is [[xai]]/SpaceXAI's Aug 12–13 2026 flagship frontier model — positioned as a *long-running agentic model* for coding, knowledge-work, and long-horizon tasks. It ships with a **500K-token context window**, text + image inputs, and Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of **61** (matches [[gpt-5-6]] Sol Max; behind only [[claude-opus-5]] at 63 and [[claude-fable-5]] at 62). Priced at **$2/M input + $6/M output** — materially below frontier peers (~60% cheaper per Rundown AI) — and distributed across [[cursor]] + [[grok-build]] + xAI API + [[openrouter]] + [[vercel]] + [[cloudflare]] partner platforms. Benchmark anchors: **88.4% Terminal-Bench v2.1**, **1753 Elo GDPval-AA v2**, competitive AA-Briefcase at far lower cost. Trained with expanded agentic RL across coding + web-development + CAD + kernel-optimization environments, using curated model-generated reasoning data and high-quality engineering data. **Grok 4.7 ships in 3-4 weeks** with supplemental training on SpaceX internal data and a Musk claim it will "exceed all current models."
