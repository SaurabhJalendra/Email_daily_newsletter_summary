## Summary

GPT-Image-1.5 is [[openai]]'s December 2025 image-generation upgrade for [[chatgpt]], shipped on 2025-12-18 as part of OpenAI's "Triple Strike" wave alongside [[gpt-5-2]] and the ChatGPT Apps Store. It claims ~4× faster generation than the previous GPT-Image, sharper detail, materially better in-image text rendering, and a "Likeness Retention" feature for keeping subjects consistent across regenerations. Coverage explicitly framed the launch as OpenAI's answer to Google's [[nano-banana-pro]] and the recent rollout of advanced image features in Gemini.
