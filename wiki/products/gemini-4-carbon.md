## Summary

Gemini 4 Carbon is Google's cybersecurity- and complex-software-engineering-focused SKU in the Gemini 4 family. It originated as an internal Jetski-platform-tested checkpoint (first surfaced Oct 13 2026) alongside parallel candidates Argon, Barium, and Barium-B. Google publicly released Carbon in late October 2026 via the **[[fairwind-program]]** — a gated program for cyber defenders — with a broader rollout planned once Google tunes guardrails based on defender feedback. Reported benchmarks on launch put Carbon at **88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym, and 71.0% on CWE-bench**, framed as *"retaking the world's no. 1 spot on updated, harder benchmarks"*.

Carbon sits alongside publicly-rollout-bound [[gemini-4-argon]] as the second named Gemini-4 SKU to productize, and extends Google's cyber-tier substrate past [[gemini-3-5-flash-cyber]]. Compared to peer cyber-tuned frontier SKUs — [[gpt-5-6-cyber]] (OpenAI Daybreak), [[claude-mythos-5-1]] (Project Glasswing) — Carbon's distinguishing posture is the Fairwind defender-only gating and vulnerability-discovery + exploitation-analysis + patching workflow emphasis.
