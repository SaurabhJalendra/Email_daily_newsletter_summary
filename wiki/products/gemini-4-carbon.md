## Summary

Gemini 4 Carbon is an internal Google checkpoint — reportedly a variant of the next-generation Gemini 4 model — tested by Google employees through the company's internal **Jetski coding platform**. Early internal feedback described its programming and complex, long-horizon-task performance as *potentially comparable* to [[claude-opus-5-5]], though no public benchmarks independently verify those claims. Carbon is distinct from (or at least not confirmed to be the same release as) [[gemini-4-argon]], the Gemini-4 variant that Google is reportedly preparing for broader public rollout. Reports also mention other internal checkpoints — including Barium / Barium-B — making clear that Google is iterating across multiple candidate versions in parallel.

Carbon matters because strong coding performance remains central to competition among frontier AI providers, and an internal checkpoint approaching Opus-5.5-level capability could materially sharpen Google's position in developer tools and agentic software engineering if (or when) it ships.
