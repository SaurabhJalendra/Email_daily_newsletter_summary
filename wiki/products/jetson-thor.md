## Summary

Jetson Thor is [[nvidia]]'s **Blackwell-powered edge compute module family** — announced mid-July 2026 with two SKUs, **T3000** and **T2000** — targeting *compact edge systems* running robotics, visual AI, and agent workloads. It is the first publicly framed Blackwell architecture to reach NVIDIA's Jetson SoC lineup, extending Blackwell from the data-center tier (GB300 NVL72) into the edge/robotics form-factor. Positioning pairs naturally with NVIDIA's [[isaac-groot]] GR00T N1.7 open-source humanoid-robot foundation model and the [[humanoid-robotics]] cycle's dense mid-2026 activity.
