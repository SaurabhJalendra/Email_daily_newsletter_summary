## Summary

**Replit Missions** is [[replit]]'s Nov 2026 agent-swarm-orchestration tool for coordinating multiple AI agents on complex projects. Instead of chaining agent calls by hand, users define a *goal* and *constraints*, and Missions coordinates a swarm of AI agents to execute. Positioned as a way for *small teams to pursue ambitious projects* that would otherwise require much larger engineering orgs. The launch productizes Replit as a concrete *agent-swarm-orchestration platform substrate operator* and graduates the Replit Agent / Agent 4 single-agent-tier productization arc into a *multi-agent-coordination-tier*. Pairs cycle-structurally with [[claude-managed-agents]] [[dynamic-workflows]], [[cognition]] Devin Apps, [[vercel-eve]] 1.0, and [[xai]] Grok Agents as the mid-Nov-2026 developer-platform-for-autonomous-multi-step-workflows canonical cluster.
