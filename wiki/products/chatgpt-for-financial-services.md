## Summary

**ChatGPT for Financial Services** is OpenAI's vertical productization of [[chatgpt-work]] for financial-services workflows, distinguished by three baked-in data substrates: **PitchBook** (private-market data + M&A tracking + startup financing), **Crunchbase** (company/investor profiles + funding-round tracking), and **LSEG** (London Stock Exchange Group — public-market data, indices, refinitiv-successor financial data feeds). Target use cases include valuation models and pitch decks, positioning ChatGPT as a first-party workflow surface for investment banking, private equity, VC, and equity research users who currently jump between multiple data-provider terminals.

Structurally, ChatGPT for Financial Services graduates OpenAI's vertical-ChatGPT-Work productization arc alongside [[chatgpt-health]] + [[gpt-rosalind]] (life sciences) into a *finance-vertical canonical substrate anchor*. Pairs competitively with [[claude-for-legal]] Anthropic vertical package and [[jpmorgan-chase]]-Anthropic LLM Suite (OpenAI + Anthropic aggregation) as *canonical mid-2026 vertical-frontier-lab-productization cohort*.
