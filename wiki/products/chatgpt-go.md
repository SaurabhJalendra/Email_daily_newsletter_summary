## Summary

ChatGPT Go is [[openai]]'s entry-tier [[chatgpt]] subscription, positioned below ChatGPT Plus at $8/month. It launched globally in January 2026 as a step in OpenAI's tiering and monetization strategy alongside the ad-supported Free tier (ChatGPT Ads rolling out concurrently in the US for Free and Go users). Go users get a meaningfully higher rate budget than Free plus access to first-party launches such as the [[codex-app]] (for a limited-time at the Feb 2026 release), without paying the Plus price point — widening the paid funnel in price-sensitive markets.
