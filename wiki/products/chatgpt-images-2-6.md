## Summary

ChatGPT Images 2.6 is [[openai]]'s November 2026 image-generation upgrade — successor to [[chatgpt-images-2]] (Apr 2026) + [[gpt-image-2-5]], claimed to generate images ~50% faster than the prior generation. It ships alongside two new API models — **Flare** and **Sunburst** — expanding OpenAI's image-model API catalog. In parallel, the ChatGPT product gained three new image-workflow primitives: **GIF creation**, **frame editing**, and **reusable reference images** — graduating ChatGPT's multimodal production surface from still-image-only into animation + edit-loop territory.

The release positions OpenAI to broaden multimodal production workflows for developers and creative teams, with faster generation and editing primitives aimed at making image + animation generation viable inside production applications.
