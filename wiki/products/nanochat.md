## Summary

nanochat is [[andrej-karpathy]]'s open pedagogical repo showing how to train a ChatGPT-like assistant end-to-end — from raw data through tokenization, pretraining, fine-tuning, and chat-style inference — in a single readable codebase. Released in October 2025, it extends the nanoGPT lineage and functions as a reference implementation for "what a minimal ChatGPT actually looks like."
