## Summary

The **Anthropic Public Benefit Fund** is a wholly-owned LLC established by [[anthropic]] in October 2026 as a formal vehicle for channelling a portion of its equity into AI-safety and public-interest work. Anthropic has committed to contribute **5% of its shares** (subject to stockholder approval), with a **ratchet mechanism** allowing the board to add another **2% for each 50% increase in company value above a specified baseline** — tying the dollar value of the commitment directly to Anthropic's growing valuation (currently ~$2T per the Sep-30-2026 IPO prospectus).

Structurally, the Fund extends Anthropic's multi-cycle governance substrate — Benefit Corporation (public-benefit-corporation structure) + Long-Term Benefit Trust (board-appointment-rights trust) + Responsible Scaling Policy — into a **concrete named-vehicle-with-5%-equity-plus-valuation-indexed-ratchet canonical governance-plus-funding anchor tier**. Reporting at launch framed the move as *"an unusually large governance and funding mechanism for AI safety and public-interest work, potentially influencing how frontier AI companies structure social-benefit commitments"* — positioning it as a potential template alongside [[openai]]'s Public Benefit Corporation restructure and the OpenAI Foundation.

The Fund's stated purpose covers initiatives addressing AI's economic and labor impacts, which pairs cycle-structurally with Anthropic's internal workforce-stat disclosures (AI agents working ~6x employee hours; junior technical hiring 27% → 9% since early 2025; see [[ai-jobs-displacement]]) and the external-workforce [[claude-frontier-academy]] $100M / 10,000-engineers-by-2027 pipeline.
