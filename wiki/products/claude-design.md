## Summary

Claude Design is [[anthropic]]'s conversational design surface built on top of [[claude-opus-4-7]]. It turns natural-language prompts into pitch decks, prototypes, wireframes, app mockups, one-pagers, and marketing collateral, with a direct hand-off into [[claude-code]] to build the actual product. Newsletters framed the launch as a direct shot at [[adobe]] and Figma — multiple outlets noted Figma stock reacted on the announcement. A follow-on "Claude Design" update added brand-guide support, letting teams encode brand voice, styling, colors, and typography so generated artifacts stay on-brand.
