## Summary

Wan 3.0 is [[alibaba]]'s August 2026 long-form AI video generation model — the successor to the Wan 2.5 8-second baseline, capable of generating up to **30-second clips in a single pass** from "nearly any input" including text prompts, spreadsheets, slide decks, PDFs, and live webpages. Wan 3.0 launched alongside Alibaba's record **$10.2B Hong Kong share placement** funding a *"full stack" AI push* (including a 3-year 380 billion yuan AI+cloud investment plan per DayTrading Dispatch 08-26).

On third-party benchmarks, Wan 3.0 ranks **#1 among video models with audio on the Artificial Analysis arena** at 1,240 Elo from 5,723 blind viewer votes. Priced at **$0.20/second at 1080p**, it materially undercuts Google [[veo]] 3.1 and ByteDance Seedance 2.5 — canonicalized by Evolving AI Insights (2026-08-25 evening) as *"Alibaba's Video Model Tops at a Third of Seedance's Price"*. The model also introduces a *prompt-driven clip-length recommendation* primitive where the model itself suggests the appropriate clip length from the prompt content.
