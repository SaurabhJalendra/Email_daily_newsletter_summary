## Summary

Raft is Botiverse's multi-agent collaboration platform, launched as **Raft 1.0** in mid-July 2026. It organizes human users and multiple AI agents (Claude, Codex, Hermes) into shared workspaces with channels, threads, tasks, and mentions — each agent runs as a *persistent process* with its own identity, memory, and expertise. Users bring their own model subscriptions while Raft provides the orchestration layer, app marketplace, agent-created channels, joint cross-server channels, message search, and attachment previews. Pricing separates human seats from agent seats. Raft addresses the usability gap between single-chatbot interactions and coordinated multi-agent workflows.
