## Summary

Qwen3.8-Max is [[alibaba]]'s July 2026 flagship multimodal frontier model — a 2.4-trillion-parameter system that Alibaba itself positions as trailing only [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-fable-5]] in overall capability. It natively handles text, images, video, and documents, and is available via Token Plan, Qoder, and QoderWork at 10% of standard pricing during preview. Critically, Qwen3.8-Max ships with **OpenAI-compatible and Anthropic-compatible API protocols** so coding agents built for American models (Cursor, Claude Code, Codex) work against it without rewrite — sharpens the [[open-source-models]] pricing-substitution thesis with a *zero-migration-cost* dimension. TLDR reports open weights are forthcoming shortly after preview.

The Qwen3.8-Max launch lands in the same edition as [[moonshot-ai]]'s [[kimi-k3]] coding-benchmark supremacy, prompting Evolving AI Insights to frame the day as *"China Just Erased America's AI Lead"* — a framing whose material weight is validated by DayTrading Dispatch noting the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index closed 20.2% below its late-June record after the Kimi K3 release.
