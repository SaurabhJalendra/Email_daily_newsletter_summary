## Summary

Daybreak is OpenAI's umbrella cyber-defense platform announced 2026-05-12, pairing GPT-5.5 frontier models with the [[codex-security]] agentic harness to scan customer codebases, build editable threat models, identify realistic attack paths, and propose / test patches that route back into developer workflows. Positioned as the defensive counterpart to the offensive cyber capability that landed days earlier when Google's Threat Intelligence Group disclosed the first confirmed AI-built zero-day exploit in the wild against a popular open-source web admin tool. Daybreak is OpenAI's response in the cyber-AI arms race that [[anthropic]] entered earlier in 2026 with [[claude-mythos]] Preview + [[project-glasswing]] — a parallel "frontier AI for defenders only" play, with OpenAI emphasizing CI/CD-loop integration over the controlled-access model Anthropic chose for Mythos.
