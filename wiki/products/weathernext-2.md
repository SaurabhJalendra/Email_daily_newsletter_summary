## Summary

WeatherNext 2 is Google DeepMind's open-source weather-forecasting model, released in early August 2026. Its headline capability is providing **roughly an extra day of lead time on tropical cyclone forecasting**, framed by AINews as a "high-impact weather model" and by the daily digest as significantly improving forecasting for both research and practical applications.

WeatherNext 2 ships in the same week as DeepMind's separate unified track-and-intensity hurricane model (which already accurately predicted Hurricane Melissa's landfall), likely sitting as complementary halves of DeepMind's weather-forecasting stack. Structurally, the open-sourcing extends DeepMind's AlphaFold/AlphaMissense AI-for-science arc with a second concrete *life-safety-tier* product line — and does so under an open license, a first among frontier-lab lead-time-extending weather models in this wiki. The release lands the same week as [[demis-hassabis]]'s transition from DeepMind CEO to chair, reading as a signal that the science-mission cadence continues despite leadership changes.
