Watermelon is [[meta]]'s upcoming internal frontier-tier AI model, publicly framed for the first time on 2026-07-04 by Meta Superintelligence Chief Alexandr Wang via TLDR AI cycle-headline coverage. The Watermelon codename joins the growing frontier-lab fruit/mythic-figure codename cohort ([[claude-fable-5]] / [[claude-mythos]] / Nano Banana / GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna). Watermelon likely succeeds [[muse-spark]] as Meta's next-generation multimodal reasoning flagship, though the architecture family, parameter count, and weight-release-strategy (open-weights consistent with Llama-lineage, or closed-weights consistent with the Muse-Spark-era open-weights-pivot narrative) are not yet publicly framed.
