## Summary

Watermelon is [[meta]]'s upcoming internal frontier-tier AI model, publicly framed for the first time on 2026-07-04 by Meta Superintelligence Chief Alexandr Wang via TLDR AI cycle-headline coverage. The Watermelon codename joins the growing frontier-lab fruit/mythic-figure codename cohort ([[claude-fable-5]] / [[claude-mythos]] / Nano Banana / GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna). Wang announced Watermelon has *"caught up with OpenAI's GPT-5.5 on closely followed AI benchmarks"* — a comparative-benchmark-parity claim with OpenAI's late-April 2026 flagship, though Watermelon is still in training and no release timeline has been given.

The catch-up-to-GPT-5.5 framing is structurally significant because it reads as Meta's response to the same-week Zuckerberg admission that AI development is *"not progressing as quickly as expected"* (canonicalized 2026-07-03-evening on [[meta]]). The Watermelon-parity-catch-up + Zuckerberg-development-slowdown admission form a *tension-pair* in Meta's public AI-progress narrative — Meta admits current-cycle execution has stalled while simultaneously claiming frontier-parity progress on the next-cycle model. Watermelon likely succeeds [[muse-spark]] as Meta's next-generation multimodal reasoning flagship, though the architecture family, parameter count, and weight-release-strategy (open-weights consistent with Llama-lineage, or closed-weights consistent with the Muse-Spark-era open-weights-pivot narrative) are not yet publicly framed.
