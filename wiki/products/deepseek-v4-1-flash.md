## Summary

**DeepSeek V4.1-Flash** is DeepSeek's September 2026 efficient open-weight model — a sparse-activation MoE with **~552B total parameters** but only **8-16B active at a time**, delivering frontier-comparable performance at radically lower cost. Reports frame it as **~33× cheaper than [[openai-astra]]** (GPT-6) while outperforming [[claude-opus-5]] and [[gpt-5-6]] Sol on several agentic, coding, and cyber benchmarks. Multimodal (image understanding built-in per Vaibhav Sisinty coverage) and priced aggressively.

Structurally, V4.1-Flash extends the multi-cycle DeepSeek open-weight cost-disruption arc (V4-Pro / V4-Flash April 2026 → V4-Flash-Vision August 2026 experimental multimodal) into a **~33×-cost-differential canonical anchor** on the Chinese-open-frontier substrate — pairs cycle-structurally with [[glm-5-3-flash]] (Zhipu, Aug 26 2026, $0.15/$0.50 per M tokens) and [[kimi-k3]] as *canonical mid-2026 Chinese-open-frontier cost-collapse cohort* against Fable-5.1/Astra $10/$50 frontier-tier pricing.
