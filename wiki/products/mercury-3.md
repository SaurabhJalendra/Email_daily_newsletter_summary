## Summary

**Mercury 3** is **Inception**'s October 2026 diffusion-based large language model. Inception reports Mercury 3 generates **more than 1,000 tokens per second on NVIDIA GPUs** and supports **context windows of up to 1 million tokens**. The company claims performance **comparable to or better than** speed-focused frontier models such as [[gpt-6-luna]] and [[claude-haiku-5-5]].

Structurally, Mercury 3 is one of the first production-tier **diffusion-based LLMs** at frontier-adjacent capability — distinct from the autoregressive-attention substrate that dominates frontier labs (OpenAI GPT family + Anthropic Claude + Google Gemini + Meta Muse + DeepSeek) — placing it alongside the earlier [[diffusion-gemma]] canonical anchor arc as a *concrete non-autoregressive-architectural-tier substrate*. The high-throughput + 1M-context pairing targets real-time generation, coding assistants, and agent workloads where autoregressive-tier latency is a binding constraint.

The company's claims require independent benchmarking — but the release is strategically significant for practitioners evaluating high-throughput inference architectures, since a validated diffusion-LLM at Haiku/Luna comparator-tier could reshape the latency + cost-per-token substrate on real-time-interactive workloads.
