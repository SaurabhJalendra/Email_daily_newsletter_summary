## Summary

Claude Mythos 5.1 is Anthropic's September 2026 restricted-access successor to [[claude-mythos]] (Mythos 5), released alongside generally-available [[claude-fable-5-1]] as the higher-safeguards, higher-capability twin. Both models share the same underlying weights but expose different safeguard configurations: Fable 5.1 for broad deployment via standard safety classifiers, Mythos 5.1 by invitation only through [[project-glasswing]] for trusted-access work in cybersecurity and life sciences. The dual-track distribution is Anthropic's continuation of the [[claude-mythos]] restricted-tier template established during the Jun–Jul 2026 export-control saga.
