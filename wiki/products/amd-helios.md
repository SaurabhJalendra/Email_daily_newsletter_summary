## Summary

AMD Helios is [[amd]]'s first rack-scale AI system — a full-rack integrated compute platform for large-scale AI training and inference, positioned as an alternative to [[nvidia]]'s GB200/GB300 NVL72 rack-scale offerings. Unveiled July 2026, Helios is being deployed in [[microsoft]] Azure data centers, with additional early customers including [[meta]], [[openai]], and [[oracle]].

Strategically, Helios graduates AMD from *chip vendor* to *rack-scale integrated system vendor*, joining the mid-2026 silicon-diversification pattern where every hyperscaler and frontier lab pursues at least one non-NVIDIA compute path ([[openai-jalapeno]] Broadcom, [[google-tpu-v8]], [[frozen-v2]], [[anthropic]]-Samsung, [[meta-compute]]). The Meta + OpenAI + Oracle early-customer trio validates AMD as a *credible second source at the frontier-lab compute tier*, materially altering NVIDIA's data-center rack-scale monopoly.
