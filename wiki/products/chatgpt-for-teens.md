## Summary

ChatGPT for Teens is [[openai]]'s Aug 2026 age-segmented product-tier experience for users aged 13-17 (or accounts predicted-underage by OpenAI's age-prediction system). It launched globally on August 18, 2026, powered by the [[gpt-5-6|GPT-5.6]] Luna model substrate, with stricter defaults on sensitive content (sexual content + romantic role-play + dangerous activities + body-image risks + graphic violence), homework and study-oriented guidance, break reminders, and optional parental controls that let parents link accounts, set quiet hours, and manage features such as voice mode and memory. Users under 18 are automatically switched to the teen version unless they verify their age; adult users incorrectly classified can go through a verify-age path to remove teen protections. Policy documentation reiterates that ChatGPT is not intended for children under 13 and requires parental consent for teens.

The launch matters because it productizes **age-segmented AI experiences** as a first-class product-substrate at the frontier-lab tier — the first mid-2026 canonical anchor of *inferred-age-based-model-routing* on a frontier AI-assistant. It graduates the multi-cycle AI-safety-for-minors discourse from *policy-tier* into a *concrete product-tier* anchor and pairs cycle-structurally with the same-day [[meta]] ~$200B addictive-tech lawsuit from California/Colorado/Kentucky/New Jersey as a *twin same-cycle child-AI-safety cluster* — OpenAI ships *product-tier restrictive-teen-mode* pre-emptively while Meta faces *state-AG-enforcement-tier response*.
