## Summary

Grok Bot is [[xai]] / SpaceXAI's team-of-agents productization of Grok, launched in beta on 2026-08-12 and framed as an *iMessage-style chat of AI teammates*. Each Grok Bot is provisioned with its own dedicated cloud computer, can sign into apps and websites on behalf of the user, retains information across sessions, and shares context with sibling agents. TLDR and AINews framed the launch as a "stronger product signal than another model launch" — repositioning xAI's product surface from *chat + coding-agent* into *persistent-autonomous-teammate substrate*.
