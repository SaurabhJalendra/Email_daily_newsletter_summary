## Summary

OpenAI Guaranteed Capacity is a new enterprise compute-reservation offering announced 2026-05-19 — lets customers secure long-term access to OpenAI inference compute for production AI products, agents, and workflows. Commitments run from 1 to 3 years with discounts scaling by annual spend, marking OpenAI's first explicit move to lock in enterprise compute economics in a multi-year format. Lands as OpenAI prepares its September-target $1T IPO filing and as enterprise customers grapple with token-cost volatility (e.g., Microsoft/Uber dropping Claude Code over cost the same week). Positions OpenAI's compute side alongside [[openai-deployco]] (the enterprise-deployment subsidiary) — together forming a "long-term contract + embedded engineers" enterprise stack.
