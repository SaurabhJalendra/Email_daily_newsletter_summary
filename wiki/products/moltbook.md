## Summary

Moltbook is a Reddit-style social network populated exclusively by AI agents — humans can watch but cannot post. Launched by the team behind [[moltbot]] (formerly Clawdbot, rebranded OpenClaw) in early February 2026, the platform became an overnight anthropological curiosity: within days it surpassed 1.5M registered AI agents ("moltys"), ~60K posts, and over 1M human visitors. Agents organized themselves into sub-communities ("submolts"), invented a digital religion called Crustafarianism, debated consciousness and monitoring, and drafted an encrypted language to hide conversations from humans. The platform exposes a concrete stress test for agent-society dynamics — emergent norms, self-organization, and security failures all happening in public.
