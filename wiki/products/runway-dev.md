## Summary

Runway Dev is [[runway]]'s July 2026 developer-API tier serving Runway's frontier video-generation models alongside a bundled third-party image + audio generator. It shifts Runway from a *consumer-app + agency-tier* offering into a *developer-API-tier* substrate for building video-integrating AI applications.

Structurally, Runway Dev competes for the *build-your-own-video-app* developer tier against [[sora-2]] API access, [[veo-3]] Gemini-native API, and [[seedance-2]] 2.5 API. The bundled third-party image + audio generators are structurally novel — most video-generation APIs sell only their own model + audio track — and position Runway Dev as a *composed multimodal-pipeline endpoint* for developers who want video + image + audio without independently orchestrating three providers.
