## Summary

ChatGPT is [[openai]]'s flagship consumer AI assistant. At DevDay 2025 OpenAI repositioned it as an app platform — third-party apps can now live inside ChatGPT — blurring the line between assistant and OS. Through mid-October 2025 the product added commerce (Walmart in-checkout integration), loosened age restrictions (18+ mode), trimmed long-lived user memories ("golden hour" memory cull), and introduced shared prompts. The late-October 2025 cycle stretched ChatGPT into new surfaces and use cases: [[chatgpt-atlas]] put ChatGPT inside its own Chromium browser; [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] turned ChatGPT into a workplace RAG over Slack/Drive/SharePoint/GitHub/Gmail with citations; PayPal checkout joined Walmart on the agentic-commerce surface; and OpenAI published mental-health and prompt-injection mitigation postures. Atlas picked up an Agent Mode in early November 2025.
