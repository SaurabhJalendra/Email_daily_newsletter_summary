## Summary

ChatGPT is [[openai]]'s flagship consumer AI assistant. At DevDay 2025 OpenAI repositioned it as an app platform — third-party apps can now live inside ChatGPT — blurring the line between assistant and OS. Through mid-October 2025 the product added commerce (Walmart in-checkout integration), loosened age restrictions (18+ mode), trimmed long-lived user memories ("golden hour" memory cull), and introduced shared prompts — all in a two-week window.
