## Summary

The iPhone Duo is Apple's first foldable iPhone, unveiled on September 9, 2026 and positioned as a major design shift for the iPhone line. It opens book-style into a 7.6-inch internal display, with a 5.4-inch outer screen, and is built around the new A20 Pro chip and Apple's new C2 modem. Starting price is **$1,999**, with preorders on **October 16, 2026** and availability beginning **October 23, 2026**. Apple positions the device as the first flagship iPhone designed from the outset around its Apple Intelligence platform + Siri AI stack + Visual Intelligence — giving more space for AI-driven multitasking, on-screen visual reasoning, and a more conversational, context-aware Siri that can take actions across apps.

The launch matters because it puts Apple into the foldable-phone market rivals have occupied for years, and serves as a high-profile test of Apple's premium pricing, hardware engineering, and AI differentiation. It is also the first product-launch canonical anchor of the John Ternus CEO era (effective Sep 1, 2026), validating the multi-cycle Ternus AI-first roadmap.
