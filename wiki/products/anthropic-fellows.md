## Summary

The Anthropic Fellows Program is [[anthropic]]'s paid AI safety research fellowship — a 4-month program paying **$3,850 per week** with a **~$15,000/month compute budget** per fellow. It targets candidates with strong Python coding skills, ambiguity tolerance, and a genuine interest in reducing catastrophic risks from advanced AI. Per Anthropic's own reporting (surfaced via Abhijay's AI Action Letter), **over 40% of the first cohort was hired full-time at Anthropic** and **over 80% produced public research papers** — positioning the Fellows Program as a high-conversion talent pipeline rather than a pure outreach program.

The Fellows Program is structurally distinct from [[claude-corps]] — Claude Corps is Anthropic's **$85K AI-learning fellowship** (paid education/upskilling track); the Fellows Program is Anthropic's **research residency** (paid contribution to AI safety research with publication and hire-conversion outcomes). The two programs together form Anthropic's two-tier paid-fellowship architecture: learn (Corps) and research (Fellows). The Fellows Program runs a multi-stage interview process — timed coding assessment → live coding with an Anthropic engineer → LLM prompting round → research conversation — with Anthropic providing free preparation resources including the **Anthropic Academy**, **Prompt Engineering Interactive Tutorial**, and **AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations** course.
