## Summary

Inkling 2 Small is [[thinking-machines]]'s November 2026 successor to its original 276B-A12B [[inkling]]-Small — a **594-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model** with a **1-million-token context window**, positioned for strong visual interpretation + creation, agentic knowledge work, and finance / quantitative reasoning. It is available day-0 on [[ollama]] Cloud as `inkling-2-small:cloud` with zero data retention; Ollama never trains on user data. Vendor-reported benchmarks place it at **83.8% on ScreenSpot Pro** (ranked first at finding elements on a screen), **67.5% on Finance Agent v2.1** (financial analysis tasks), and **81.6% on MMMU Pro** (images + charts understanding).

Named use cases include OCR, grounding, infographic interpretation, visual reasoning, tool calling with MCP, finance spreadsheet reasoning, filings, and documents. Inkling 2 Small is listed as compatible with [[claude-code]], Hermes Agent, and [[openclaw]] via specific launch commands, positioning it as a drop-in open-weight substrate for agentic coding and knowledge-work stacks.
