GPT-5.3 Codex Spark is a smaller, ultra-fast Codex variant from OpenAI optimized for real-time use inside [[codex-app]] and Codex CLI. Spark is the first publicly visible OpenAI model to ship on Cerebras silicon, signaling a deepening of the OpenAI/Cerebras inference partnership announced earlier in 2026.
