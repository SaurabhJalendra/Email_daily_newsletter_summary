## Summary

Gemini 4 Pro is [[google]]'s next flagship Gemini model, internally codenamed **argon**. As of September 2026 it is under active development at Google DeepMind: Sundar Pichai confirmed the Gemini 4 pre-training run on Google's Q2 2026 earnings call, describing it as "a substantially larger base model" designed to keep pace with the frontier field and prioritizing coding and autonomous-agent capability. In mid-September 2026 an internal checkpoint of Gemini 4 Pro leaked publicly under the argon codename, with community trackers reporting a 2-million-token context window, a first-output latency of ~2.4 minutes on high thinking effort, and evaluations that reportedly beat [[claude-fable-5-1]] on coding and GPT-6 Astra on multi-step reasoning. None of the benchmark claims cite a Google source and should be treated as unverified leak-tier information.

Community trackers expect Google to publicly ship Gemini 4 Pro in October 2026, with a Flash-Lite variant and an updated image model arriving first. The previously-promised Gemini 3.5 Pro — which Sundar Pichai committed to at I/O in May 2026 and said would follow the next month — appears to have been shelved, with Google skipping directly from Gemini 3.8 Flash toward the Gemini 4 family.
