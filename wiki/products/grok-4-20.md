## Summary

Grok 4.20 is xAI's February 2026 Grok upgrade, rolled out as a public beta. Its headline feature is a new agent workflow that runs four agents in parallel to research and complete tasks — xAI's first explicit multi-agent surface. In the same cycle, Grok Build adds Parallel Agents and Arena Mode, repositioning the coding-assistant surface toward a full IDE.
