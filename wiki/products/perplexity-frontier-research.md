## Summary

Perplexity Frontier Research is a new in-house research initiative at [[perplexity]] announced on 2026-11-16 with the explicit mission of building an "automated scientist" — AI systems that can ask questions, form hypotheses, run experiments, analyze results, and update their conclusions autonomously. The agenda spans six areas: multi-agent orchestration, pre-training, continual learning, reinforcement learning, systems, and frontier evaluations. The effort is backed by 10,000 GPUs, with applications open to researchers until December 5. Perplexity says the work will be conducted openly, with results released as papers, blog posts, data, model weights, benchmarks, and code.
