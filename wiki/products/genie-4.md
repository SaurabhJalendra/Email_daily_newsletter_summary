## Summary

Genie 4 is Google DeepMind's next-generation entry in the Genie line of interactive world / 3D-environment generative models, announced on **October 27, 2026** as a successor to [[project-genie]] (which was powered by Genie 3). It generates realistic, interactive environments from text or image prompts, with real-time natural-language steering — users can change the weather, move the camera, or add characters as the simulation runs. Unlike Genie 3, it natively generates synchronized ambient audio, music, and interactive dialogue, while improving physics, human motion, and scene consistency. Google reports it can maintain a coherent scene for up to two minutes (twice Genie 3's reported limit).

The development matters because it moves generative AI beyond passive video generation toward controllable, persistent simulations that can be explored and modified as they run. Potential applications include game and film previsualization, interactive entertainment, robotics training, and other embodied-AI research. Genie 4 is a research preview for trusted testers at first surfacing, with planned access for Google AI Ultra subscribers through Project Genie and selected partners through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. Structurally, Genie 4 sits in the broader late-2026 **world-model / interactive-environment cohort** alongside [[marble]] (World Labs), [[gwm-worlds-2]] (Runway), [[solaris]] (Runway), [[odyssey-3]] (Odyssey), and [[helix-world]] (Noiz).
