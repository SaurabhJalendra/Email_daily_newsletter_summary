## Summary

Presence is OpenAI's July 2026 enterprise agent platform for building, deploying, and managing controlled AI agents across customer support and internal operations. Each deployment is scoped to a specific job with permissions, policies, evaluations, and escalation rules; a Codex-powered improvement loop proposes updates over time. It supports both voice and chat agents and complements [[chatgpt-work]] (agentic ChatGPT workspace) at the enterprise-orchestration tier. Positioning: OpenAI moving deeper into the agent-orchestration/management layer where enterprises struggle with production adoption — directly targets [[claude-managed-agents]] and Anthropic's enterprise agent stack. Some newsletters this cycle also refer to the offering as "Frontier" (see [[openai-frontier]] Feb 2026 launch); it is unclear whether Presence is a rebrand or a distinct new-generation platform — open question.
