## Summary

K2 Horizon is MBZUAI's (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, Abu Dhabi) September 2026 open-model release — a fleet of six fully-open models spanning 0.9B to 375B parameters. The release represents Abu Dhabi's push to establish a foundation-model presence in the fully-open-weights competitive cohort alongside Chinese open-frontier labs ([[moonshot-ai]] Kimi K2 + [[deepseek]] + [[zhipu-ai]] GLM) and [[meta]]'s Muse Spark open-weights forward-commitment.

The six-checkpoint scale span from 0.9B (device-tier) to 375B (frontier-tier) positions K2 Horizon as a *full-substrate open-weights fleet* rather than a single flagship — comparable in strategic scope to the Llama 3 family before Meta pivoted to closed-weights Muse Spark. This is Abu Dhabi's flagship contribution to the mid-2026 open-weights ecosystem alongside [[humain]] (Saudi Arabia's sovereign AI vehicle).
