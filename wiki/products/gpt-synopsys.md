## Summary

GPT-Synopsys is a specialized OpenAI model being co-developed with EDA incumbent [[synopsys]] under a reported multiyear strategic partnership announced in early Oct 2026. The model is intended to operate semiconductor-design tools and automate increasingly complex chip-engineering workflows — effectively treating EDA tool-chains as an executable environment for an agentic frontier model. The partnership extends OpenAI's chip-design posture beyond in-house silicon (see [[openai-jalapeno]], co-designed with [[broadcom]]) into the *EDA-vendor-plus-model-supplier* layer of the chip-design stack.
