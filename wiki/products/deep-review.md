## Summary

**Deep Review** is Google Research's Oct 2026 agentic AI system for rigorous feedback and technical verification of complex documents. Rather than *simple text generation* or copy-editing, Deep Review targets research and other knowledge-work workflows requiring **structured analysis** — literature analysis, technical writing, research validation. Google positions it as a concrete example of agentic systems moving into high-value knowledge work, extending the Google *Deep*-prefixed research-agent SKU family that already includes [[gemini-deep-research]], [[deep-think]], and [[deep-think-ultra]].

The release lands in a late-Oct-2026 cycle alongside Microsoft + Nvidia committing a combined ~$15B to Anthropic and OpenAI reportedly seeking a $30B round at ~$1.25T — productizing Google as *canonical shipping-side* of the simultaneous *frontier-lab-capital + frontier-agent-productization* substrate.
