## Summary

NVIDIA Cosmos is a foundation-model family from [[nvidia]] purpose-built for physical AI — robotics, simulation-to-real transfer, and embodied agents. Cosmos 2.5 is generally available as of April 2026 with faster inference, simplified fine-tuning workflows, Hugging Face Diffusers integration, and new product updates aimed at bridging synthetic data generation (SDG) to real-world deployment. NVIDIA has also pre-announced Cosmos 3, the next-generation release, for summer 2026.
