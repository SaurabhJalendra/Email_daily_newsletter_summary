## Summary

NVIDIA Cosmos is a foundation-model family from [[nvidia]] purpose-built for physical AI — robotics, simulation-to-real transfer, digital twins, and embodied agents. Cosmos 3 unifies reasoning, world generation, simulation, and action generation across text/image/video/ambient sound/action in a single Mixture-of-Transformers architecture, trained on ~20 million hours of real-world video. The family ships in a three-tier stack: **Cosmos 3 Edge** (4B on-device world model for robots), **Cosmos 3 Nano** (post-trainable in a day via NVIDIA TAO agent skills to reach 93.35% domain-tier accuracy on video Q&A benchmarks), and **Cosmos 3 Super** (~25× faster image/video generation for high-fidelity digital twins, top rankings on open-weight leaderboards). Cosmos 2.5 shipped GA in April 2026 with Hugging Face Diffusers integration and remains available as legacy tier.
