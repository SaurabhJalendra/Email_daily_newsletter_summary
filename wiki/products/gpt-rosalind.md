## Summary

GPT-Rosalind is [[openai]]'s first Life Sciences specialty model — a reasoning model designed for biochemistry, drug discovery, genomics, and translational medicine. Introduced alongside the 2026-04-16 Codex superapp release, it formally launches in late-April 2026 as a domain-tuned variant aimed at expediting processes from scientific breakthrough to clinical-application timelines. The name is a deliberate nod to Rosalind Franklin, the X-ray crystallographer whose work shaped the discovery of DNA's structure. Positioning places GPT-Rosalind alongside [[claude-mythos]]'s 94.6% science-PhD-test framing and [[gemini-3-1-pro]]'s STEM-reasoning headlines as the frontier-lab response to specialty-domain AI.
