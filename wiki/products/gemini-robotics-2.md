## Summary

Gemini Robotics 2 is Google DeepMind's July 2026 robotics-tier foundation model, positioned as a **single AI model that can control robots from feet to fingertips**, **adapt to new hardware in hours**, and **perform complex tasks with success rates up to 89.6% on precise insertion tasks**. It ships alongside **Gemini Robotics ER 2** (Embodied Reasoning 2), an *"embodied reasoning" model that acts as a planning brain for robots, enabling them to coordinate tasks in shared spaces* — together forming a **plan-vs-execute dual-model stack** (ER 2 = planner + Gemini Robotics 2 = executor). The pair marks Google's entry into the frontier humanoid-robotics-tier substrate cohort alongside [[nvidia]] [[isaac-groot]], [[alibaba]] [[qwen-robot-suite]], and [[mistral]] [[robostral-navigate]].
