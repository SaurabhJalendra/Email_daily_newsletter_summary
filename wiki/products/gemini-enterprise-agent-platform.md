## Summary

Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform is Google Cloud's Aug 2026 consolidated agent-tier stack, unifying Vertex AI and Agentspace into a single platform for building, scaling, and governing enterprise-grade AI agents grounded in corporate data. Announced at Cloud Next 2026 alongside the [[gemini-3-7-flash]] agent-tier workhorse model, it repositions Google's enterprise-AI substrate away from a fragmented multi-product surface and toward a consolidated agent-authoring + agent-governance product-tier — matching the enterprise-agent posture of competitors on the *data-grounded, governed, production-grade agent* axis.

The platform's strategic value is less about individual features and more about the *consolidation move itself*: Vertex AI (Google's canonical model-serving + ML-platform substrate) and Agentspace (Google's agent-workspace layer) merging into one product removes a common enterprise-buyer objection that Google's enterprise-AI story required stitching multiple products together. Paired with Gemini 3.7 Flash's aggressive introductory pricing, the platform positions Google to compete against enterprise-tier agent stacks from OpenAI, Anthropic (via Bedrock/Vertex/Azure partnerships), and dedicated enterprise-agent vendors like [[glean]].
