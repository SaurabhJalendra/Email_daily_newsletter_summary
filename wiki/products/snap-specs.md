## Summary

Snap Specs are Snap Inc.'s $2,195 **standalone augmented-reality glasses** positioned for the **"post-phone era"** — World of AI's framing of a major social-platform company explicitly betting on wearable AI as the smartphone successor. Specs are **fully standalone** (no tethered phone required), run **two Snapdragon chips**, and ship with a **51-degree field of view** and **7-millisecond latency**. They are powered by **contextual AI** that can see what the user sees, understand what they are trying to accomplish, and help in the moment.
