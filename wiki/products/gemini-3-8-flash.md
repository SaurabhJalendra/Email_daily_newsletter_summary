## Summary

Gemini 3.8 Flash is [[google]] DeepMind's September 2026 Flash-tier successor to [[gemini-3-7-flash]] — smaller, cheaper, and faster to run than the Pro tier, with a targeted boost to coding performance relative to prior Gemini releases. Reports indicate Google's internal testers prefer Gemini 3.8 Flash over Anthropic's Opus tier for coding tasks, an inversion of the usual size-vs-quality assumption at the Flash-vs-Opus boundary. The model was extensively internally tested on Google's *Jetski* coding platform throughout August 2026 before the September 2 public reveal.
