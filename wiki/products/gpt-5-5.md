## Summary

GPT-5.5 is [[openai]]'s late-April 2026 flagship model release, framed by [[openai]] as "a new class of intelligence for real work" — designed for agentic computing, long-horizon tasks, and tool-using execution rather than purely chat-shaped Q&A. It launches with strength on knowledge work, computer use, and coding benchmarks, and is positioned across coverage as OpenAI's first model explicitly designed for "human-centric AI" (intent recognition, independent planning, output verification). The release follows roughly five months after [[gpt-5-4]] (Mar 2026) and lands the same week as [[deepseek]]'s open-source [[deepseek-v4]] series — which triggers a parallel cost-performance pricing narrative against GPT-5.5.
