## Summary

Mistral Large 4, codename "Le Chonk," is [[mistral]]'s October 2026 trillion-parameter natively multimodal mixture-of-experts flagship — roughly 1T total parameters with ~49B active per forward pass. The model was trained on 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs and launched API-available, with reported performance comparable to [[zhipu-ai]]'s GLM 5.3 on certain benchmarks. It graduates Mistral's open-weight/sovereign-frontier product ladder — previously anchored by [[mistral-3]] (41B active / 675B MoE), [[ministral-3]] edge, [[leanstral]] verification, [[robostral-navigate]] robotics, and Shieldstral moderation — into the trillion-parameter frontier-cohort tier.

Large 4 is positioned as "a major European alternative to leading American and Chinese systems," sustaining Mistral's multi-cycle European-sovereign-AI thesis against the backdrop of its mid-2026 neocloud pivot and the broader European-sovereign-AI tension arc (see [[mistral]] 09-10-evening "European Sovereign AI is Dying" deep-dive).
