## Summary

Hark Handoff is the first product from AI startup Hark, launched in early August 2026 as a computer-use agent designed to take on time-consuming everyday tasks such as ordering food, shopping, and recruiting talent. Positioned as an "everything agent" for personal and small-business workflows, it competes in the consumer-tier computer-use category alongside Anthropic's Claude Computer Use, Google's [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]], and Perplexity's [[perplexity-comet]]. Coverage frames Handoff as targeting *"tasks that eat up your day"* rather than dev/coding workflows — a lifestyle-tier positioning distinct from the coding-agent cohort.
