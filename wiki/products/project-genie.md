## Summary

Project Genie is a Google DeepMind prototype that turns text and image prompts into fully interactive 3D worlds, rendered one frame at a time in response to user input. Built on the latest Genie 3 world model, the prototype lets users sketch a scene, walk / fly / drive through it, and remix environments either by changing prompts or by building on other creators' worlds. Unlike prior image or video-generation tools, Genie creates state-ful interactive spaces — positioning Google's world-models line (alongside [[marble]] and Meta's lab work) as a distinct category from [[ai-video-generation]] and a credible step toward general-intelligence training environments.
