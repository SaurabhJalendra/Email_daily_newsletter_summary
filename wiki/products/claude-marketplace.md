## Summary

Claude Marketplace is [[anthropic]]'s enterprise hub for discovering and buying Claude-powered third-party tools and agents while consolidating them against existing Anthropic commitments. Originally launched March 10, 2026 with a handful of launch partners (GitLab, Harvey, Replit, Snowflake), it relaunched at scale on Sep 25, 2026 with **2,000+ plugins** — including MCP connectors to Google Drive, Slack, and Salesforce, plus Claude-powered agents from [[cursor]], CrowdStrike, and Accenture. Purchases bill against an existing Anthropic budget rather than requiring separate contracts, positioning Marketplace as the canonical enterprise-procurement layer for the Claude ecosystem.
