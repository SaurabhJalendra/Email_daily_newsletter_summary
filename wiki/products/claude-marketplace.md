## Summary

Claude Marketplace is [[anthropic]]'s centralized hub for enterprises to discover and buy specialized Claude-powered third-party tools while consolidating them against existing Anthropic commitments. Announced March 10 2026 alongside [[claude-code]] scheduled tasks and loop commands, it's framed as the enterprise procurement layer for the Claude ecosystem — letting customers spend existing spend commitment on partner tools rather than separate contracts. Launch partners include GitLab, Harvey, Replit, and Snowflake.
