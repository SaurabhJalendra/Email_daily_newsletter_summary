## Summary

n8n is a no-code / low-code workflow automation platform (open-source core + hosted cloud) that has become the default substrate for the [[no-code-ai-automation]] cohort — freelancers, agencies, and SMB operators building AI-powered workflows without writing full applications. It exposes a visual node graph over HTTP APIs, LLM providers, databases, and business SaaS (CRM, email, Slack, Notion, etc.), which practitioners use to string together lead-generation pipelines, content workflows, and AI-agent routing.

n8n surfaces recurrently in AI-education communities like [[ai-automation-society]], where it is paired with [[claude-code]], OpenAI/OpenRouter APIs, Clay, and Hermes for lead-gen, scraping, and voice-dictation systems. Its relevance to the wiki is as the *canonical no-code glue layer* between LLMs and business workflows — the automation-tier counterpart to the developer-tier [[agent-frameworks]].
