## Summary

OpenRouter is a model-routing platform that gives developers unified API access to **400+ AI models** across vendors — a single endpoint that lets applications switch between frontier LLMs (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Kimi, DeepSeek, GLM, etc.) without rewriting integration code. It has functioned as one of the canonical distribution surfaces for open-weight frontier models in the 2025-26 cycle (e.g. [[deepseek-v4]] V4-Pro-0813 GA distribution).

On 2026-08-17 [[stripe]] announced an agreement to acquire OpenRouter for **over $7B**, giving the payments company a strong footing in the fast-growing AI infrastructure sector. The deal is being framed by newsletters as a sign of accelerating industry consolidation on model-routing / provider-abstraction as core enterprise infrastructure — cycle-structurally adjacent to [[cursor-router]], [[model-routers]] AlphaSignal essay, and OpenRouter's own Fusion Router.
