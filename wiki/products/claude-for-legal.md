## Summary

Claude for Legal is Anthropic's vertical packaging of Claude for law firms and corporate legal teams, launched 2026-05-13. Ships with 20+ MCP connectors and 12 practice-area plugins covering contracts, litigation, AI governance, and other domains, with direct integrations into Microsoft Word, Outlook, iManage, NetDocuments, Ironclad, DocuSign, Box, and e-discovery systems. Concrete workflows include vendor-contract review, claim-chart building, DSAR responses, and court-docket connectivity. Together with [[claude-for-excel]] (Oct 2025), [[claude-for-word]] (Apr 2026), and [[claude-for-small-business]] (May 2026), forms the "Claude for X" enterprise-vertical stack — a deliberate move from horizontal API-only sales toward bundled, workflow-aware products.
