## Summary

Dyna-2.1 is [[dyna-robotics]]'s semi-humanoid **physical agent** — the first system in the Dyna family to pair the company's brand-new semi-humanoid hardware with an agentic system built around the [[dyna-2]] World-Action Model. It targets **reliable super long-horizon whole-body autonomy** on ultra-long real-world workflows, with the Sep 29 2026 reveal showing *uncut footage* of Dyna-2.1 completing a commercial-laundry shift. The system graduates Dyna Robotics's substrate from *foundation-model-tier productization* ([[dyna-2]] as a general WAM) into *shipped workflow-level robotic system tier* — distinct from a general humanoid platform in that Dyna-2.1 is explicitly designed around hour-long whole-body workflows rather than open-ended humanoid autonomy.
