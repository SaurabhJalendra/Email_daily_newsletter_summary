## Summary

Buzz is [[block]]'s open-source, self-hosted workspace launched August 2026 where humans and AI agents operate in the same rooms. Announced by Jack Dorsey's Block, it combines four primitives into a single surface — **chat**, **search**, **Git events** (repo activity as first-class workspace events), and **YAML-based automation** — deployed on the user's own server rather than a hosted-cloud service. Positioned as the *open-source, self-hostable* alternative to cloud-hosted AI-agent-workspace substrates like [[claude-tag]] (Slack), [[claude-cowork]], and Perplexity Comet.

The launch sharpens Block's multi-year *"AI-replaces-middle-management + builders/problem-owners/player-coaches"* thesis (2026-04-03 Dorsey framing) into a *concrete open-source workspace-substrate* that other AI-forward orgs can self-host — extending Block's internal *Goose* + *Builderbot* AI-agent motion into a productized-community-tier substrate. Structurally distinct from Anthropic's Claude Tag / Cowork Slack-first + closed-hosted posture, Buzz's *self-hosted + open-source + YAML-declarative automation* combination positions it as a *canonical open-source alternative* in the mid-2026 shared-agent-workspace substrate cohort.
