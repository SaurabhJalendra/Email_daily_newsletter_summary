## Summary

Grok 4.1 is [[xai]]'s mid-November 2025 update to the Grok model family. Coverage highlights a "personality overhaul" plus measurable capability gains: the highest emotional-intelligence score on public EQ benchmarks, reduced hallucination rate, and the top spot on LMArena at launch. It lands alongside improved reasoning accuracy (94.1%) and video generation updates noted earlier in November, framing a steady xAI product cadence ahead of the postponed Grok 5 (now targeted for 2026).
