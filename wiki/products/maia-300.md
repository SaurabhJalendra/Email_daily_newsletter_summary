## Summary

Maia 300 is [[microsoft]]'s custom AI chip designed for **inference workloads**, which Microsoft is preparing to deploy at **multiple-gigawatt scale** across its data centers. The chip delivers **40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU-based systems**, a materially better tokens-per-watt efficiency ratio that could substantially reduce the cost of running AI inference at hyperscaler scale. Maia 300 extends Microsoft's custom-silicon strategy as an alternative to NVIDIA GPU-only infrastructure for inference workloads, pairing with the Azure co-deployment of [[amd-helios]] rack-scale systems announced earlier in 2026.
