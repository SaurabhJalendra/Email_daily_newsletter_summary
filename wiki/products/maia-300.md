## Summary

Maia 300 is [[microsoft]]'s custom AI chip designed for **inference workloads**, which Microsoft is preparing to deploy at **multiple-gigawatt scale** across its data centers. The chip delivers **40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU-based systems**, a materially better tokens-per-watt efficiency ratio that could substantially reduce the cost of running AI inference at hyperscaler scale. Maia 300 extends Microsoft's custom-silicon strategy as an alternative to NVIDIA GPU-only infrastructure for inference workloads, pairing with the Azure co-deployment of [[amd-helios]] rack-scale systems announced earlier in 2026.

## Key Facts

- **Vendor**: [[microsoft]]
- **Generation**: Third-generation custom AI accelerator (Maia 100 → Maia 200 → Maia 300)
- **Target workload**: Inference — long-context reasoning + memory-intensive workloads + conversational AI + multi-step agentic tasks
- **Architecture**: multi-chiplet + eight HBM4 stacks
- **Memory bandwidth**: up to 18 TB/s
- **Thermal design power**: 950W (reported)
- **Rack throughput**: 96-accelerator rack delivers up to **7× the token throughput of Maia 200**
- **Efficiency**: 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU-based systems
- **Scale**: multi-gigawatt deployment planned
- **Production timeline**: 2027
- **Positioning**: Microsoft's **primary inference platform** for its own products and services
- **System scope**: complete co-designed silicon + software + networking + rack infrastructure + data-center stack
