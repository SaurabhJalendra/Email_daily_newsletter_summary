## Summary

AlphaGenome 2 is [[google]] DeepMind's next-generation genomics AI model, announced on November 11, 2026. It predicts how DNA sequences and genetic variants affect gene regulation in specific human or mouse cell types and states — gene expression, chromatin accessibility, histone modifications, and DNA methylation — conditioning predictions on one cell type at a time. The model was trained on more than 100 million cell profiles spanning 68 tissues, a shift from its predecessor's broader tissue-level framing.

On the TenK10K immune-cell benchmark, DeepMind reports AlphaGenome 2 recovers ~30% of putatively causal regulatory variants at 90% precision (vs 16% for the previous approach) and correctly predicts the direction of gene effects 43% of the time (vs 23%). The release matters because most disease-associated DNA variants lie outside protein-coding regions, where cellular effects are hard to interpret, and improved variant-effect prediction is a long-standing bottleneck for translating GWAS hits into drug targets. Non-commercial research access is available via the AlphaGenome API and interactive tutorials; commercial research is in limited private preview. DeepMind cautions that AlphaGenome 2 is a research tool, not a clinically validated diagnostic system.
