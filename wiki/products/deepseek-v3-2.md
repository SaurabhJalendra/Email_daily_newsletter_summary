## Summary

DeepSeek V3.2 is DeepSeek's open-source frontier release of early December 2025, shipped in two variants: a base V3.2 model that performance-matches GPT-5, and a reasoning-specialized V3.2-Speciale that rivals Google's Gemini 3 Pro and earned gold medals at IMO 2025, IOI 2025, and ICPC 2025. The pair extends DeepSeek's track record of pushing open-source models to the leading edge against closed-weight frontier labs.
