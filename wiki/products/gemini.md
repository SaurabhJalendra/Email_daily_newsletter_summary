## Summary

Gemini is [[google]]'s flagship multimodal AI model family, spanning consumer (Gemini app), enterprise ([[gemini-enterprise]]), and developer (API) surfaces. In early October 2025 Google revamped the consumer Gemini app, shipped [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]] for UI control, launched [[gemini-enterprise]], and announced bundling changes. Later in the month the API picked up real-time location via Google Maps data, and [[gemini-3]] moved from leak to confirmed. Security researchers also publicly disclosed [[prompt-injection]] vulnerabilities in three Gemini models.
