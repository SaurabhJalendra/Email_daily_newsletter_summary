## Summary

Gemini is [[google]]'s flagship multimodal AI model family, spanning consumer (Gemini app), enterprise ([[gemini-enterprise]]), and developer (API) surfaces. In early October 2025 Google revamped the consumer Gemini app, shipped [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]], launched [[gemini-enterprise]], and announced bundling changes. Later that month the API picked up Maps-powered location data, and [[gemini-3]] moved from leak to confirmed. In April 2026 Gemini expanded across desktop and browser surfaces: [[gemini-3-1-flash-tts]] for full-stack audio, a native Gemini Mac app with window-sharing and screen analysis, and a Chrome "Skills" feature that saves and reuses Gemini prompts across pages.
