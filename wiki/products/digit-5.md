## Summary

Digit 5 is [[agility-robotics]]' fifth-generation general-purpose humanoid robot, announced Aug 2026 and publicly unveiled Sep 2026. It is designed for **cooperatively safe work at scale** in manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics — operating in close proximity to human workers without the traditional safety cages required for industrial robots. Digit's cumulative >65K production hours across customer sites position it as one of the most operationally validated humanoid platforms in the mid-2026 [[humanoid-robotics]] cohort.

Digit 5 hardware upgrades: **~5'11" tall**, **50-lb repeatable-lift payload** (+40% over Digit 4), new leg design tuned for repetitive lifting cycles, **~90-minute battery runtime**, and **~9-minute rapid recharge** enabling **>20 working hours per 24h**. **Interchangeable end-effectors** (paddle, pincher, claw, five-finger hand) expand the task envelope; **onboard sensing** enables safe operation around people. Commercial trajectory is deep: **>$300M multi-year orders**, Toyota among named customers, first international rollout to **EU + UK** with CE marking, early access H1 2027 → broader availability by end of 2027.
