## Summary

Firefox is Mozilla's web browser, positioning itself as the **privacy-first entrant** in the AI-browser race that includes [[chatgpt-atlas]] (now discontinued by OpenAI), [[perplexity-comet]], [[opera-neon]], and Cloudflare's [[kitesurf]]. In August 2026 Mozilla partnered with the semantic-search startup **Exa** to power two AI-tier features: **Smart Window** on desktop (an optional chatbot that pulls live information from the internet and consolidates open tabs into categories) and **Quick Answers** on iOS (source-backed answers surfaced without leaving the current page). Mozilla frames Smart Window as an *optional, private AI browsing experience* rather than a default AI overlay.

Firefox's differentiation is user-control and transparency rather than raw capability. It introduced global AI-opt-out controls in early 2026, positioning itself against Chrome's Gemini-tier defaults, [[chatgpt-atlas]]'s bundled ChatGPT substrate, and [[perplexity-comet]]'s Perplexity-search default. The launch matters because it graduates Mozilla into the mid-2026 AI-browser cohort at the *privacy + user-choice tier* — a distinct competitive axis from feature-parity or model-power races — and pairs with the same-cycle Atlas discontinuation as a signal that the AI-browser market is beginning to consolidate and reposition around distinct value-propositions.
