## Summary

Terafab is a **jointly-developed AI chip factory** between [[tesla]] and [[spacex]], sited in Grimes County, Texas outside Houston, with an initial **$16.8 billion investment** confirmed in August 2026. Unlike traditional semiconductor fabs positioned as general-purpose foundries (TSMC, Samsung, Intel Foundry), Terafab is scoped from day one as an **AI-workload-specific fab**: it handles manufacturing + packaging + testing of *both logic and memory chips* explicitly designed for AI workloads across three named customer verticals — Tesla's Optimus humanoid robots + Cybercab autonomous-taxi program + SpaceX's space-based data centers.

The facility is planned to exceed **100 million square feet** and employ at least **3,000 people**, positioning it as one of the largest single-facility AI-substrate footprints in this wiki. Structurally, Terafab productizes Musk-integrated-vertical AI supply chain — completing the *chip → data-center → application* stack under Musk-owned entities, and pairing with the mid-2026 US semiconductor-sovereignty policy backdrop (Trump executive order same-cycle on US polysilicon protection with 15% tariff + minimum-import-prices) as *coordinated domestic-fab-tier economics*.
