## Summary

Dyna-2 is [[dyna-robotics]]'s **World-Action Model (WAM)** — a general-purpose physical-agent foundation model pretrained on **over one million hours of egocentric human video** and designed to predict how actions change the physical world before a robot acts, connecting visual understanding with contact physics and manipulation. Dyna reports **hour-long autonomy** on ultra-long real-world workflows when Dyna-2 is combined with the company's agentic system. The model is positioned to **transfer across robot platforms and adapt to new tasks with limited local robot-data fine-tuning**, supporting Dyna's thesis of a *human-to-robot scaling law* — that scaling human-video data yields predictable improvements in robot performance and reduces the industry's dependence on expensive teleoperation data.

Dyna-2 serves two layers: (1) a WAM foundation at **87% real-world zero-shot pass rate on unseen robot hardware** at launch (Aug 2026), and (2) the agentic substrate underneath **[[dyna-2-1]]** (Sep 29 2026) — Dyna's semi-humanoid physical agent that executes full real-world shifts (demonstrated as a commercial laundry workflow in uncut footage). The release sits on the *scaling-laws-for-robotics* axis alongside Google's [[gemini-robotics-2]] whole-body-control, NVIDIA's [[world-action-models]] family + [[isaac-groot]] + [[alpamayo]], and Figure's [[helix-2-5]] crowdsourced-human-video control model.
