## Summary

Perplexity Computer is Perplexity's multi-model orchestration platform, launched in late February 2026 for Max subscribers on the web. It dispatches tasks across 19 separate AI models to run full workflows end-to-end, framing Perplexity as a task-execution layer rather than just a search surface. In early March 2026 it expanded with "Skills" — reusable markdown-based workflow instructions. By April 2026 it had added a tax agent (drafts federal returns on IRS forms, reviews pro-prepared filings, flags missed deductions), Plaid / bank-credit-loan integrations for financial analysis, and a "notebooks" feature for organizing chats, files, and custom instructions — positioning Perplexity Computer as a general agentic-work layer (search + task + finance + tax) rather than just orchestration plumbing.
