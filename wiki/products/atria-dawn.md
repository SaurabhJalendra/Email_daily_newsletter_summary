## Summary

Atria Dawn Preview is a **744-billion-parameter agentic mixture-of-experts (MoE)** model released by **Shanghai AI Laboratory** in the 2026-09-16-morning cycle. It is built on **GLM-5.2** and uses a **Verifiable Experience Pipeline** to *ground tool use in executable environments* — a training design that emphasizes verifiable, reproducible outcomes on real-tool-use trajectories. Positioned by the lab as *rivalling [[claude-opus-5]]* on research-focused agentic benchmarks, and pitched at **researchers** rather than general consumer/enterprise use. Released same-cycle as the Amodei "pace-the-frontier" essay saturates the discourse — validating the multi-cycle Chinese-lab-does-not-slow-down counter-narrative on [[ai-pace-letter]].
