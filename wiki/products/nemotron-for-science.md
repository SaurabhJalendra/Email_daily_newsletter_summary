## Summary

Nemotron for Science is [[nvidia]]'s November 2026 open starting point for building specialized scientific agents, combining the [[nemotron-3-5-super-vl]] open multimodal model with an open chemistry dataset containing nearly 50,000 reasoning questions and associated post-training recipes. It is positioned to reduce the work required to adapt multimodal foundation models for chemistry, materials, and adjacent scientific-research workflows.

The release sits inside NVIDIA's broader open-model push and extends the Nemotron productization arc from general-capability model + hardware substrate into a domain-specialized scientific-agent package — complementing adjacent AI-for-science substrates such as [[lila-sciences]], [[periodic-labs]], and the Alibaba Recursa + DiscoveryRSI life-science research track.
