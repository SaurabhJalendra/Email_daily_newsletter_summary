## Summary

NVIDIA Ising is [[nvidia]]'s open AI models for **quantum computing** — described as the world's first open AI model family designed to help researchers and enterprises build reliable quantum processors. It includes **Ising Calibration** and **Ising Verification** models that automate calibration and error correction tasks — the two largest bottlenecks in scaling quantum-processor reliability. Distribution is broad and open: GitHub, Hugging Face, and NVIDIA's website. Ising lands as NVIDIA's first AI-for-quantum surface and reinforces the company's "AI as the control surface for advanced compute domains" thesis — extending the [[nvidia-cosmos]] physical-AI footprint into quantum engineering.
