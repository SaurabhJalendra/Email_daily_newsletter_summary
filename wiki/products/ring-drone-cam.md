## Summary

Ring Drone Cam is [[amazon]]'s $249.99 indoor flying security camera, announced November 10 2026 under the new [[ring-robotics]] category and launched as the consumer-tier SKU of the Ring Robotics two-product lineup. The drone flies between rooms (including upstairs) from a charging dock, records 2K video over a 148-degree field of view, follows scheduled or user-requested routes, and responds to triggers from Ring cameras, door/window sensors, leak detectors, or alarms. Autonomy is handled by Ring's **AI Pilot system**, which combines cameras, onboard sensors, specialized vision models, and cloud processing to map a home, identify patrol points, and navigate obstacles. The product ships to the US on January 27 2027, pending FCC approval.

Ring Drone Cam is structurally significant as the concrete GA-commercialization — six years later — of Ring's 2020 Always Home Cam concept, which was demonstrated but never reached general availability. It extends AI-enabled home security from fixed cameras to mobile, event-driven inspection, potentially improving coverage of rooms that lack cameras while raising familiar privacy, safety, reliability, and indoor-surveillance concerns.
