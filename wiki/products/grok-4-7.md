## Summary

Grok 4.7 is [[xai]]/SpaceXAI's Grok 4.6 successor. Per Elon Musk on 2026-08-13-evening, initial training is complete and the model is *"already in flight"*, with supplemental training on **SpaceX internal data** planned before shipping in **3-4 weeks** (early-September 2026 target). Musk claims the model will *"exceed all current models"* — a distinct competitive posture from [[grok-4-6]]'s *"objectively No. 1 on intelligence, speed & cost"* Pareto-frontier claim (Grok 4.7 targets *absolute-capability-leader* rather than *cost-efficiency-frontier*). The pre-launch parameter framing from 2026-08-05 was **2.1 trillion parameters**, though vendor-confirmed sizing at ship is pending. Structurally significant: Grok 4.7 productizes the Feb-2026 [[spacex]]/xAI corporate merger into a *training-corpus vertical-integration* anchor — first Grok-model-trained-on-vertical-corporate-corpus (likely Falcon/Starship telemetry + Starlink ops + Boca Chica facility documentation).
