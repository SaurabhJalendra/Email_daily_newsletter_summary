## Summary

Harbor is an app in testing by **Claris** (an [[apple]] subsidiary) that turns Apple Silicon Macs into private AI servers, enabling larger models and higher concurrency to be hosted locally rather than relying on third-party cloud inference. First surfaced in the Nov 11 2026 TLDR digest, Harbor extends Apple's on-device AI narrative — previously anchored around [[apple-intelligence]], [[apple-m5]], and the broader Claris / Siri AI arc — into a *concrete private-server-tier canonical anchor* positioning the Mac as a local inference host for users who want to keep AI workloads off third-party clouds.

The product's structural significance is that it reframes Apple Silicon — historically a consumer-device inference substrate — as a *private AI model-hosting platform*, aligning Apple with the local/private-AI canonical arc that includes [[ghost]] + [[core]] + [[ollama]] + [[perplexity-personal-computer]]. It targets data-privacy-sensitive users who need larger-model access with higher concurrency than typical single-user setups allow.
