## Summary

Weave Isaac 1 is Weave Robotics's home humanoid robot, unveiled in early July 2026 with fall-2026 shipping starting in California. Priced at **$8,000 upfront or $449/month** subscription — the first publicly framed sub-$10K humanoid consumer pricing tier in this wiki (prior enterprise/research humanoids have been $50K+). The robot performs household chores including folding clothes, tidying living spaces, and making beds; adjusts height from **3 to nearly 6 feet** depending on the task; and may require remote human assistance for certain edge-case tasks.

Structurally novel three ways: (a) sub-$10K consumer pricing lowers the humanoid-in-the-home barrier substantially — creates a new humanoid-consumer tier separate from the enterprise/research market currently dominated by Figure 03 / Tesla Optimus / Unitree H2 / NVIDIA [[isaac-groot]] reference design; (b) $449/month subscription pattern productizes humanoid-as-a-service, an unusual commercial model for physical hardware that keeps upfront capital barriers even lower and maintains an ongoing revenue relationship for firmware updates + skill additions + remote-teleoperation assistance; (c) *"may require remote human assistance"* is a structurally honest qualifier acknowledging that fully autonomous home humanoid operation is not yet reliable — Weave uses teleoperation as a fallback for edge cases, similar to Serve Robotics's teleoperation pattern for sidewalk delivery robots.

The naming collision with NVIDIA's [[isaac-groot]] reference design (both "Isaac" for a humanoid platform) is potentially trademark-collision-worthy or intentional co-branding — body recovery needed to disambiguate.
