## Summary

**ERNIE 5.5 Flash** is [[baidu]]'s November 2026 preview model unveiled around Baidu World — a **sparse mixture-of-experts architecture** with approximately **280 billion total parameters**, **14 billion active parameters**, and a **roughly one-million-token context window**. It is positioned for long-context coding, knowledge work, 3D, and content creation, with particular relevance to Chinese-language and multimodal workflows.

Structurally, ERNIE 5.5 Flash marks Baidu's first publicly framed ERNIE-5.5-generation product entry in this wiki, graduating the ERNIE-4.5-VL-Thinking canonical anchor into a Flash-tier preview SKU alongside late-2026 frontier open-weight and preview peers [[longcat-2-5]] (1.6T / 48B active), [[inkling-2-small]] (594B / 17B active), and [[kimi-k3-1-pro]] (2.8T / 104B active) on the ~1M-token-context axis.
