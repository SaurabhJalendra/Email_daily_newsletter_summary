## Summary

Claude Haiku 5.5 is [[anthropic]]'s Oct 7, 2026 small-model tier launch — the third model in the Claude 5.5 family after [[claude-opus-5-5]] (09-23-evening) and [[claude-sonnet-5-5]] (09-29-morning). Positioned for high-volume, latency-sensitive, cost-conscious workloads (classification, routing, extraction, summarization, database queries, subagent-for-coding), it is Anthropic's first Haiku with effort settings and adaptive thinking, priced at **$0.10/M input + $0.50/M output for prompts up to 100,000 tokens**, with higher rates for larger prompts. Anthropic reports it is roughly **75% cheaper to run on average than [[claude-haiku-4-5]]**.

Haiku 5.5 enters the market **price-matched against [[gpt-6-luna]]** at the ≤100K-token tier but reportedly outperforms Luna on Anthropic-reported benchmarks (GDPval-AA v2.1: 1,620 vs 1,437; OSWorld 2.1: 72.4% vs 48.9%; Terminal-Bench 4.0: 39.2% vs 16.4%) — vendor-reported, not independently verified. Artificial Analysis scored it **43 on the Intelligence Index**, up 26 points from the previous Haiku and slightly ahead of GLM-5.3 Flash, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and GPT-6 Luna; it trails [[claude-sonnet-5-5]] at max effort by 13 points. Available at launch on Anthropic's platform, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure using the model identifier `claude-haiku-5-5`.
