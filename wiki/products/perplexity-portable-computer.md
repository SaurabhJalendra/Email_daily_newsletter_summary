## Summary

**Perplexity Portable Computer** is Perplexity's fully local AI agent, co-launched with [[nvidia]] and designed to run entirely on [[nvidia]] DGX Spark consumer hardware — model weights, user data, and agent execution all stay on the local machine, with the agent only escalating to the cloud when strictly necessary. Positioning: *zero-token-cost, privacy-preserving Computer-tier substrate* aimed at users whose sensitive files should never leave the device. Available for **Pro, Max, Enterprise Pro, and Enterprise Max** subscribers on **Linux at launch**, with **Windows support arriving September 2026**.

Structurally, Portable Computer is distinct from [[perplexity-personal-computer]] (which runs the Comet Assistant continuously on an Apple Mac mini): Portable Computer is a *fully-local-agent on Nvidia hardware* rather than an *always-on cloud-facing agent on Mac mini*. The launch productizes Perplexity + Nvidia's mid-2026 strategic-partnership arc — arriving days after reports of Nvidia weighing a multi-billion-dollar investment in Perplexity — and canonicalizes DGX Spark as a **consumer-tier substrate for local frontier-agent workloads**.
