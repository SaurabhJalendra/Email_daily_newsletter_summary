## Summary

LFM3 is [[liquid-ai]]'s October 2026 on-device model family — a set of **open-weight text, vision, audio, omni, and decision models** sharing a single **tri-hybrid backbone**, starting around the **2B parameter range**. The release extends Liquid AI's prior non-transformer edge-tier posture (LFM 2.5 230M + LFM2-2.6B-Exp 3B tier) with a *multi-modality + multi-task* family under a shared backbone, aiming at phones, laptops, embedded devices, and edge inference substrates where frontier GPT-/Claude-tier models cannot run locally.
