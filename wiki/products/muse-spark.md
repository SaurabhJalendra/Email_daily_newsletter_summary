## Summary

Muse Spark is [[meta]]'s natively multimodal reasoning model introduced in April 2026, notable less for the model itself than for what it signals: Meta is visibly pivoting away from the open-weights strategy that defined the Llama era. The model supports tool use and multi-agent orchestration and reportedly leads on some health and multimodal benchmarks, but trails frontier competitors on coding and agentic tasks. A headline architectural feature is "Contemplating mode" (also marketed as toggling between "Instant" and "Thinking"), which runs multiple sub-agents in parallel to solve complex problems without increasing end-to-end latency — Meta's differentiation bet against serial-reasoning frontier competitors. Launched as the first model from Meta Superintelligence Labs and positioned as the foundation for a more advanced personal Meta AI assistant.
