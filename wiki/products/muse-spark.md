## Summary

Muse Spark is [[meta]]'s natively multimodal reasoning model introduced in April 2026, notable less for the model itself than for what it signals: Meta is visibly pivoting away from the open-weights strategy that defined the Llama era. The model supports tool use and multi-agent orchestration and reportedly leads on some health and multimodal benchmarks, but trails frontier competitors on coding and agentic tasks. Coverage repeatedly paired the launch with the phrase "Meta pivots from open weights" — a meaningful strategic shift for a lab that was previously the open-model standard-bearer.
