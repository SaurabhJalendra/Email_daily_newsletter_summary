## Summary

**MAI-Base-2** is Microsoft AI's second internally developed large-scale foundation model, with pretraining reportedly completed around October 17–18, 2026. The release — reported through third-party industry coverage rather than a Microsoft-authored announcement — marks the successor to Microsoft's prior in-house base-model substrate ([[mai-thinking-1]] reasoning model + the earlier MAI-1 foundation model). It extends Microsoft's multi-year effort to reduce reliance on [[openai]] for frontier-model access by developing its own full foundation-model stack in parallel.

Structurally, MAI-Base-2's pretraining-complete milestone pairs with the same-week [[maia-300]] custom-silicon canonical anchor as a *twin-substrate Microsoft-self-reliance-tier anchor pair* — concrete in-house silicon plus concrete in-house foundation model — that productizes mid-Oct-2026 as the inflection window for Microsoft positioning itself as a full-stack frontier operator alongside, and no longer fully dependent on, OpenAI.
