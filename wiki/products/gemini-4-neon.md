## Summary

Gemini 4 Neon is Google's mid-tier Gemini 4 generation model announced on November 18–19, 2026, positioned for software engineering, knowledge work, and scientific tasks. It offers a **1 million token context window**, **long outputs up to 1 million tokens**, **multimodal input**, and **four configurable thinking levels**. Pricing is **$3 per million input tokens** and **$15 per million output tokens** with a **95% cached-input discount** ($0.15 cached). Google reports **61 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index**, **70.5% Terminal-Bench Science**, **84.0% RiemannBench**, and **71.3% HLE-Diamond**; independent reports also place it first on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at launch. Distribution spans the paid Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity, and paid Gemini Pro/Ultra app plans. Positioned between the faster, lower-cost [[gemini-4-hydrogen]] and the more expensive, long-horizon [[gemini-4-carbon]], Neon targets agentic enterprise workflows and multi-file coding in the cost-efficient-coding tier alongside [[mai-code-2]], [[claude-haiku-5-5]], and Tencent Hy4 flash.
