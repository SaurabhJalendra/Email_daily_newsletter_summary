## Summary

Groq 3 LPX is [[nvidia]]'s AI inference accelerator chip (Language Processing Unit — LPU), built on Groq LPU technology that NVIDIA acquired in a ~$20B deal in November 2025 and rebranded under NVIDIA's LPX platform naming. Groq 3 LPX **entered full production in August 2026** as an extension of the [[vera-rubin]] platform, positioned specifically for *ultra-fast token generation in response-sensitive agentic workloads* — delivering a **4× boost in response times versus the nearest alternative platform**.

The launch productizes NVIDIA's *training-plus-companion-inference-chip dual-substrate architecture* first framed in the 2026-07-15 [[jonathan-ross]] Groq-origin-story cycle (which established ~25% of GPU workloads would eventually pair with Groq-style inference chips). Groq 3 LPX now materializes as the *response-latency-tier companion-chip* to Vera Rubin's training + high-throughput-inference substrate — validating agentic-workload response-latency as a first-order design goal rather than a throughput or cost-tier metric.
