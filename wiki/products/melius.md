## Summary

Melius is a node-based creative canvas for AI-generated digital assets, positioned as a *team creative-workflow tool* rather than a single-user chat surface. The pitch: top-tier image and video models are exposed as composable nodes on a canvas, letting teams generate assets in bulk while suppressing the *excessive-prompting + inconsistency* failure modes that dog chat-first creative-AI tools. Launched into the mid-2026 crowded consumer + prosumer creative-AI market with peers like [[canva]] AI 2, [[muse-image]] (Meta, now-killed), and Runway's [[runway-dev]] developer API.
