## Summary

Computer History is [[openai]]'s Aug 2026 feature that **logs clicks and typing to give ChatGPT and Codex a memory of recent work** on the user's computer. Framed as an efficiency layer — the assistant no longer needs to be re-briefed on what the user has just been doing across applications — it extends [[chatgpt]]'s persistent-memory arc from cross-conversation recall ([[ai-memory]] tier) into cross-application activity recall on the local device. Cycle-structurally it pairs with the mid-2026 desktop-agent cohort ([[chatgpt-work]] + [[claude-cowork]] + [[kimi-work]]) that positions frontier assistants as *"knows what you were just working on"* substrates.
