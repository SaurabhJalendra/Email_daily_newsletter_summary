## Summary

IBM Bob is IBM's AI coding partner for enterprise development teams, launched in the 2026-07-10 evening cycle. It is designed to help enterprise teams coordinate the full software development lifecycle — planning, coding, testing, and validation — as a unified agentic workflow rather than a code-completion assistant. Framed by The Rundown AI as evidence of *"the integration of AI in software development to enhance productivity and collaboration"* at enterprise scale.

Structurally, Bob is IBM's entry into the increasingly crowded frontier-lab-plus-hyperscaler *agentic coding platform* competitive surface where [[claude-code]] (Anthropic), [[cursor]] (Cursor), [[openai-codex]] (OpenAI + ChatGPT Work), [[github-agent-hq]] (Microsoft), Warp Oz ([[warp]]), and [[cognition]]'s Devin are the dominant incumbents. Bob's differentiation appears to be enterprise-lifecycle-coordination (planning + testing + validation loops) rather than pure code-generation — consistent with IBM's historical positioning as an enterprise-software-lifecycle vendor (WebSphere, Rational, watsonx).
