## Summary

Muse Image is [[meta]]'s first *proprietary* AI image-generation model, launched July 8 2026 from Meta Superintelligence Labs as part of the new **Muse family** that replaces the earlier open-weights Llama models. It is tightly integrated with the [[muse-spark]] large language model, enabling *agentic* behavior where the system can reason through prompts, search the web, and plan before generating images. Muse Image natively interprets complex conversational instructions, blends multiple photos, supports in-image editing, and can produce legible styled text and functional QR codes within images.

The model is embedded on day one in the Meta AI app + website, Instagram, and WhatsApp, with planned expansion to Facebook and Messenger. Creative surfaces include transforming user photos, redesigning rooms, drawing directly on images, and mentioning Instagram accounts so their likeness can be incorporated into AI-generated photos. Independent AINews coverage places Muse Image #2 on Image Arena at launch, with the paired [[muse-video]] at #3 on Video Arena — Meta Superintelligence Labs "having the top 2/3 image/video models" per swyx (AINews). Rundown AI also flags Meta's use of the model to power **Advantage Plus** advertiser-tuned image generation.

Structurally, Muse Image marks the *productization of Meta's post-Llama pivot* — the Muse family is closed-weights and platform-integrated, structurally distinct from the Llama-era open-weights strategy. Pairs cycle-structurally with [[watermelon]] frontier-LLM catch-up + [[meta-compute]] cloud-monetization + smart-glasses $14B bet as Meta's fourth simultaneous consumer-tier AI-substrate move.
