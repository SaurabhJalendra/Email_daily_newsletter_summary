## Summary

Rime Prism is [[rime]]'s fine-tunable speech-to-speech model for enterprise voice agents, launched October 28 2026. It combines low-latency audio interaction (~300ms response time) with an inspectable text layer, parallel tool calls, turn detection, and enterprise controls for vocabulary, pronunciation, safety, deployment, and data residency. Rime says Prism is designed to let companies run voice systems within their own infrastructure — positioning it against pure-API voice vendors for regulated or latency-critical enterprise workloads.

Prism targets a key limitation of realtime voice agents: natural conversation often comes at the expense of control, auditability, customization, and predictable enterprise behavior. The inspectable text layer gives engineers visibility into what the model perceived + intended to say, while parallel tool calls + turn detection make Prism usable as a drop-in voice agent inside larger orchestration stacks. The model is available through developer infrastructure such as [[livekit]] Agents and [[together-ai]], signaling broader integration into the voice-agent stack rather than vendor lock-in.
