## Summary

Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data are new artifact types introduced by [[anthropic]] in November 2026 that let teams build spreadsheets, organize ideas, and create dashboards directly inside Claude without switching apps. They shipped alongside the [[claude-fable-5-2]] and [[claude-mythos-5-2]] frontier model releases and are positioned as the workspace-productivity surface of the Claude platform — extending the existing Claude Artifacts substrate into structured, collaborative data-and-visualization objects. The release also introduces **interactive connectors** that embed partner apps like Adobe and Atlassian directly in Claude.

The suite productizes Anthropic as one of the first frontier labs to ship embedded partner-app connectors inside its chat surface, a cycle-parallel to OpenAI's Businesses-in-ChatGPT direction, and consolidates Claude's horizontal workplace stack alongside [[claude-cowork]] + [[claude-for-excel]] + [[claude-for-word]].
