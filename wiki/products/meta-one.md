## Summary

Meta One is [[meta]]'s Sep 2026 **paid tier bundle** across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp — offering expanded access to Meta AI (image, video, editing tools) plus creator tools across Meta's app family. Plans range from **$2.99/month at the entry tier to $499/month at the top tier**, spanning consumer-tier casual usage to prosumer/creator-tier bulk AI usage. Meta One productizes Meta's mid-2026 pivot from *free-with-ads* AI-in-the-app framing into a *paid-subscription monetization tier* — following the $14.3B Scale AI investment that seeded the [[muse-spark]]-family model substrate that Meta One now monetizes.

Structurally reads as Meta's answer to [[chatgpt-go]] + ChatGPT Plus subscription tier and [[claude-money]]-adjacent consumer-AI-tier substrate, but with a wider price band ($2.99 → $499) and a cross-app bundle framing rather than a single-product-tier substrate.
