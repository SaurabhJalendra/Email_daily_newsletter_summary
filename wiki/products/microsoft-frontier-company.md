## Summary

Microsoft Frontier Company is a Microsoft unit launched July 2026 to deploy AI solutions to enterprise customers by embedding a team of 6,000 engineers and industry experts inside client teams, backed by a $2.5B budget. The unit's stated focus is *delivering measurable results and not just sales*, and Microsoft frames it with an explicit "client data and IP will remain private and will not be used to train its models" commitment — an upfront no-train-on-customer-data guarantee designed to preempt concerns about proprietary-data harvesting (structurally consistent with the same-week [[palantir]] Alex Karp "tokenmaxxing / overcharging enterprises + harvesting proprietary data" critique of the frontier-lab default posture).

The unit joins the cross-vendor *forward-deployed engineering* (FDE) motion pioneered by [[openai]] + [[anthropic]] and normalized cycle-structurally by [[amazon]]'s $1B AWS Forward Deployed Engineering unit (2026-07-01-evening) + [[cursor]]'s FDE 10× scaling + [[warp]]'s Oz platform — collectively named as *[[software-factories]]* in cross-vendor newsletter coverage (2026-07-02-morning Warp CEO Zach Lloyd + Cursor FDE lead Pauline Brunet Latent Space interview cohort). Microsoft Frontier Company is the largest single-vendor FDE unit surfaced in this wiki by budget and headcount as of July 2026.
