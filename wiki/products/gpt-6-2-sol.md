## Summary

**GPT-6.2 Sol** is [[openai]]'s Nov 17 2026 efficient, lower-cost companion model to flagship [[gpt-6-2-astra]], designed for everyday tasks rather than demanding professional work. The two models form the GPT-6.2 twin-SKU family and extend the GPT-6 generation's three-tier productization (Astra flagship + Sol professional-and-coding + Luna high-volume) into a refreshed Nov 2026 cadence. GPT-6.2 Sol is available through **ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the OpenAI API**, with the pair reported to produce **better results with fewer output tokens** compared to prior releases.

Independent reporting from Artificial Analysis notes **unchanged pricing for Sol relative to GPT-6.1 Sol**, while Astra sees a 20% price reduction — positioning GPT-6.2 as primarily a flagship-tier upgrade with the Sol tier held at existing economics for continuity. The launch sits inside a frontier-model cluster alongside [[anthropic]]'s Claude Fable 5.2 + Mythos 5.2 and [[google]]'s Gemini 4 Carbon + Gemini 4 Hydrogen, intensifying price-performance competition on general-purpose and professional-tier models.
