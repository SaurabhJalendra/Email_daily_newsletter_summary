## Summary

Claude Science is Anthropic's July 2026 dedicated science-research hub for Claude — a unified platform combining paper review, database access, figure-making, and long-running computing jobs, with a distinguishing *result-provenance-tracking* capability that traces every result back to the code and data behind it. Positions Anthropic as a direct competitor to OpenAI's [[gpt-rosalind]] life-sciences vertical product and complements Anthropic's broader AI-for-science posture including the [[john-jumper]] Nobel-tier hire (June 2026) and the DOE + multi-national-lab evaluation partnership (June 2026).

The *"auditable results"* + *"traces every result back to the code and data behind it"* framing reads as a *research-provenance-tracking* surface — structurally novel among frontier-lab science products because it treats the *audit trail* (which code ran on which data to produce which figure) as a first-class product surface rather than a scientist-side afterthought. This aligns with reproducibility-crisis concerns in scientific publishing and positions Claude Science for adoption in workflows where result-verification is regulatory (drug discovery, clinical trials) or reputational (published research).
