## Summary

DeepSeekMath-V2 is DeepSeek's open-source mathematical-reasoning model, released late November 2025, that achieved IMO 2025 gold-level results — comparable to Google's Deep Think. It uses a two-part training architecture: a generator that produces candidate proofs and a verifier that checks reasoning correctness, addressing the long-standing problem of unverifiable chain-of-thought outputs in mathematical proof generation.
