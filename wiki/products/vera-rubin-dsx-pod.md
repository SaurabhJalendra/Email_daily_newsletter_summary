## Summary

The **Vera Rubin DSX POD** is [[nvidia]]'s Oct 2026 modular, partner-built AI data-center design, announced by Jensen Huang at the GTC Berlin 2026 keynote (Oct 21). It is positioned as the concrete productization of the *AI-factories* layer of NVIDIA's five-layer AI stack (energy → accelerated computing → AI factories → models → applications), with the explicit goal of reducing full AI-factory deployment time from months to days. NVIDIA reports an operating target of **14 days vs the prior six-month baseline** — a ~13× deployment-speed anchor — achieved by shipping the data-center as a *modular, partner-built* pod rather than a bespoke per-site build.

The DSX POD sits inside NVIDIA's broader AI-factory substrate alongside the [[vera-rubin]] GPU platform, [[nvidia-vera-cpu]] agentic CPU, Run:ai scheduling layer (open-sourced within DSX AI Factory in Oct 2026 per 10-19-evening canonical anchor), [[nvidia-minerva]] AIOps for monitoring, and the "AI factory" OEM bundling partnership with Dell and Supermicro established in Jul 2026. It is unveiled on the same GTC Berlin keynote that landed [[openshell]] agent-safety reference-architecture framing, the [[rtx-spark]] laptop refresh, the Isaac robotics-platform expansion, and the [[nvidia-hyperion]] European AV + robotaxi substrate.
