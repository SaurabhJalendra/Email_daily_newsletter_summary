## Summary

Cohere Command A+ is Cohere's first frontier-tier model released as open weights under Apache 2.0, announced 2026-05-21. A 218B-parameter dense model engineered for complex reasoning and agent-based tasks, with an architecture that includes a parallel transformer block and large shared expert usage. Released with permissive licensing that lets teams fine-tune, deploy, and commercialize without restriction. Initial benchmarks are mixed — strong in non-hallucination behavior but weaker in scientific reasoning and coding vs the leading closed frontier models ([[claude-opus-4-7]], [[gpt-5-5]]). The release positions Cohere as one of the first frontier-tier Western labs to ship Apache-2.0 weights at this scale, joining the open-model wave that includes [[deepseek-v4]], [[gemma-4]], [[kimi-k2-6]], MiMo 2.5, GLM-5.1, and [[mistral-3]].
