## Summary

Grokipedia is [[xai]]'s AI-generated encyclopedia, launched in late October 2025. Built on the Grok model and drawing from X posts for "real-time" updates, it positions as an explicitly alternative editorial regime to Wikipedia — covering politically loaded topics (gender, ideology) with framings xAI argues Wikipedia handles with bias. By the end of October it claimed 800K+ entries and was being framed as open for collaborative editing/expansion. Reception split predictably along the lines of pre-existing Musk-era debates about Grok's moderation choices and whether Wikipedia's editorial regime needs replacing.
