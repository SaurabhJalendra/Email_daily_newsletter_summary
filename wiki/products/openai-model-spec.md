## Summary

The OpenAI Model Spec is a public specification for intended model behavior across OpenAI's products — ChatGPT, Codex, GPT-6 Astra/Sol/Luna/Terra, and future SKUs. The late-October 2026 revision reorganizes model behavior around five core values: **faithfulness to human intent**, **harm minimization**, **honesty and transparency**, **human agency**, and **care**. The spec functions as both an engineering guide for OpenAI's training + evaluation pipelines and a public commitment governing how models should behave in ambiguous or adversarial situations.
