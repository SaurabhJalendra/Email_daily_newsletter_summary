## Summary

GPT-6 Luna is OpenAI's Sep 22, 2026 launch — the low-cost, high-volume tier of the GPT-6 family, positioned below [[gpt-6-sol]] and [[openai-astra]]. It targets summarization, information extraction, and quick answers, and is priced substantially below both GPT-5.6 Luna and Anthropic's [[claude-opus-5-5]]. OpenAI reports Luna improves on GPT-5.6 Luna by 5.4 percentage points on AutomationBench while reducing cost per task by 58%.

Luna is available through the OpenAI API as `gpt-6-luna`, supports text and image input with text output, and offers roughly a 1.05-million-token context window. It is also available to Free and Go users through the desktop app. It launched alongside [[gpt-6-sol]] within minutes of Anthropic's [[claude-opus-5-5]], intensifying competition around inexpensive high-volume inference rather than only maximum-benchmark performance.
