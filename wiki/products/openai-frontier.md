## Summary

OpenAI Frontier is the company's February 2026 enterprise platform for building and running custom, high-functioning AI agents inside an organization's internal systems. It pitches itself as the start of the "AI Employee" era: agents with system integration, persistent context, and action-oriented capabilities (not just chat). Initially limited to select enterprise customers, Frontier extends OpenAI's enterprise stack — currently anchored by [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] (workplace RAG) and [[agentkit]] (agent toolkit) — into production-scale deployment of custom agents with internal-data access.
