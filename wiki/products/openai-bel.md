## Summary

**Bel** is OpenAI's next-generation pre-training model reported at **~10 trillion parameters** — a step-change in scale beyond the GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna) family — described as the successor to [[openai-doug]] (the base for [[openai-astra]] after reinforcement learning) and positioned as the **foundation-tier anchor for GPT-6**. Bel is reported as a *post-Doug pretrain-substrate* that could underpin systems near an AGI threshold. As of 2026-08-27 there is no formal launch, model card, or pricing — coverage is limited to newsletter-attributed pretrain-completion signals and researchFindings-tier web-search context.

Structurally, Bel matters because it graduates OpenAI's frontier-substrate roadmap from *GPT-5.x point-release iteration* into an *order-of-magnitude scale-jump anchor*. It pairs with the [[openai-jalapeno]] custom-inference-silicon deployment as the *"train-tier + inference-tier"* twin foundation for OpenAI's *next-generation frontier stack*.
