## Summary

Cue is [[manus]]'s Sep 2026 standalone personal-AI-agent product, launched alongside the [[manus]] 2.0 platform rebuild on 2026-09-28/29. It is Manus's first named consumer-facing product following the July 2026 Chinese-authorities block of the Meta $2B acquisition, and the first agent-substrate in this wiki to give each agent its own email address, phone number, digital wallet, and computer — enabling it to send messages, make calls, complete workflows, and make payments within user-defined budgets.
