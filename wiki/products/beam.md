## Summary

Beam is [[reflection]]'s open-weight flagship model unveiled in early October 2026, built as a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture with **501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active parameters**. The model is positioned for coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads and is explicitly framed as a US open-weight alternative to leading Chinese open-source frontier models such as DeepSeek and Qwen. Beam is the first publicly shipped model under Reflection's $6.3B SpaceX Colossus 2 GB300 compute commitment, operationalizing the June 2026 flagship-customer deal into a shipping artifact.
