## Summary

Beam is [[reflection]]'s open-weight flagship model unveiled in early October 2026, built as a text-only sparse mixture-of-experts architecture with **501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active parameters**, pretrained on **23.8 trillion tokens** with a **1-million-token context window**. The model is positioned for coding, reasoning, scientific work, and agentic workloads, and is explicitly framed as an "American open model" alternative to leading Chinese open-source frontier models such as DeepSeek and Qwen. Reflection reports performance comparable to [[zhipu-ai]]'s GLM-5.2 at roughly **3-4× lower inference compute** than competing Western open models — the central competitive claim behind the US open-weight thesis — though these claims have not yet been independently verified. Beam is the first publicly shipped model under Reflection's $6.3B SpaceX Colossus 2 GB300 compute commitment; full weights are expected under **Apache 2.0** later in October 2026, with early access via waitlist in the interim.
