## Summary

Kimi K3 is [[moonshot-ai]]'s formally released frontier open-weight model — a **2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with ~50B active parameters per query (2.8T-A50B)**, released mid-July 2026 as the largest open model ever released. Architecturally, K3 combines **Kimi Delta Attention (KDA)** — a hybrid linear attention mechanism — with **Attention Residuals** and **LatentMoE**, allowing it to scale over 2× beyond prior Kimi models while preserving performance.
