## Summary

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS is a low-latency text-to-speech variant in the [[gemini]] family, released in April 2026. It supports 70+ languages and exposes fine-grained controls via natural-language "audio tags" for tone, pacing, emotion, vocal style, and accent, plus multi-speaker dialogue and scene-direction prompts. At launch it scored an Elo of 1,211 on the Artificial Analysis TTS leaderboard. The release is notable as a visible push by [[google]] beyond text into a full audio stack — newsletters framed it as Gemini moving to "control the full loop" of input and output modalities.
