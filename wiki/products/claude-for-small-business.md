## Summary

Claude for Small Business is Anthropic's SMB-vertical packaging of Claude, launched 2026-05-14. Connects Claude directly to QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, DocuSign, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365, with 15 ready-made workflows across finance, operations, sales, marketing, HR, and customer service — covering payroll planning, invoice chasing, month-end accounts, contract reviews, sales campaigns, and marketing-asset creation. Targets the ~36M US small businesses and is paired with a "roadshow" outreach motion. The launch closes a gap with [[microsoft]] 365 Copilot at the SMB tier and competes head-on with the SMB-focused [[chatgpt]] Business tier; in coverage it lands the same week [[anthropic]] passes [[openai]] in Ramp business-adoption indexing (34.4% vs 32.3% for April 2026).
