## Summary

Ring Robotics is [[amazon]]'s November 2026 autonomous-security-device category, built around Ring's AI Pilot system — a stack combining cameras, specialized vision models, on-device sensors, and cloud processing to map spaces, identify patrol points, detect events, and navigate around obstacles autonomously. The category launches with two SKUs: the **[[ring-drone-cam]]** ($249.99 indoor flying camera for homes) and the **Ring Rover Cam** ($999.99 patrol robot for businesses). The announcement revives and substantially commercializes the Always Home Cam concept Ring first demonstrated in 2020 but never shipped to general availability.

Ring Robotics is structurally significant as Amazon's extension of AI-enabled home-and-business security from fixed cameras to mobile, event-driven inspection. It sits alongside [[amazon]]'s broader early-Nov-2026 robotics push — the Nov-09 [[fauna-robotics]] acquisition for a dynamic-home-navigation robot (successor to Astro), the Sep-2026 $100M+ Greenwood Indiana robotics plant, and the Oct-2026 1M+ warehouse-robot-fleet canonical anchor — productizing Amazon as the canonical early-Nov-2026 hyperscaler with a concrete consumer-plus-business-facing autonomous-security-robot product line.
