## Summary

Meta Compute is [[meta]]'s forthcoming cloud infrastructure business, disclosed in press reporting across the July 2–3 2026 cycle. The offering rents out surplus data-center capacity from Meta's $183B AI infrastructure buildout and provides access to Meta-hosted models — structurally competing with the dominant hyperscaler tier ([[amazon]] AWS, [[microsoft]] Azure, [[google]] Cloud) and the neocloud tier ([[coreweave]]). Framed as Meta's *revenue-diversification response* to underwhelming external demand for its own AI models and services; Meta stock jumped ~9% on the initial report.

The product inverts Meta's historical *infrastructure-consumer* posture (CoreWeave $21B customer + AWS + Azure + Google Cloud consumer) into *infrastructure-vendor* posture. Structurally significant because Meta's data centers were purpose-built for internal large-scale AI training workloads — external customers get access to compute *purpose-built for AI* rather than the general-purpose compute at AWS / Azure / GCP baseline, though the offering is constrained by Meta's own internal capacity needs.
