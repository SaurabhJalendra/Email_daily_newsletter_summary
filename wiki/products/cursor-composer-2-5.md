## Summary

Cursor Composer 2.5 is the May 2026 update to [[cursor]]'s in-house coding model, trained on Moonshot's Kimi K2.5 substrate with targeted reinforcement learning, 25× more synthetic tasks, and new distributed training techniques. Reaches 79.8% on SWE-Bench Multilingual — approaching frontier coding models ([[claude-opus-4-7]], [[gpt-5-5]]) at roughly **10× efficiency**: pricing starts at $0.50/M input tokens and $2.50/M output tokens (vs ~$11/task on competitors), with sub-30-second task completion. Lands alongside Cursor's new cloud development environments (Dockerfile-based configs, version history, audit logs, cached builds) and the disclosure that Cursor is training a "significantly larger model" with SpaceXAI using 10× more compute. Cursor's annualized revenue hits $3B in late April 2026, and SpaceX holds a 30-day option to acquire Cursor for $60B after the SpaceX IPO begins trading.
