## Summary

Muse for Small Business is [[meta]]'s Sep 29 2026 small-business expansion of its [[meta-muse-agent]] personal AI agent, positioning Muse as an operations-automation substrate for entrepreneurs rather than just a consumer assistant. It extends Muse's connector platform with SMB-focused tools: **Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe, Canva, Asana, Zoom, Box, and Slack**, and plugs directly into Facebook and Instagram analytics + ad accounts for Meta-owned-platform-native marketing automation.

Structurally, Muse for Small Business lands the same day as [[openai]]'s [[dots]] (per-user persistent agents) and [[xai]]'s [[grok-team-bots]] (shared team coworkers), canonicalizing Sep 29–30 2026 as a three-frontier-lab shared-and-persistent-agent inflection window — OpenAI consumer-individual + xAI team-tier + Meta small-business-tier. The move positions Muse as a direct competitor to Dots in the enterprise-agent market and extends Zuckerberg's broader [[meta-enterprise-platform]] "Business AI" push into a concrete SMB-tier productization anchor.
