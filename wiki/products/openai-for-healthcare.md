## Summary

OpenAI for Healthcare is [[openai]]'s HIPAA-compliant enterprise platform for hospitals and health systems, launched January 2026 as the enterprise counterpart to consumer [[chatgpt-health]]. It uses GPT-5-class models targeted at clinical workflows — documentation, triage support, patient communication, records summarization. Together with [[chatgpt-health]] it represents [[openai]]'s "two-track healthcare play": consumer insights + provider workflows.
