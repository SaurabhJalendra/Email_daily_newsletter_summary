## Summary

GPT-5.4-Cyber is [[openai]]'s defensive-cybersecurity fine-tune of [[gpt-5-4]], launched April 2026 under the expanded **Trusted Access for Cyber (TAC)** program. It is optimized for tasks such as binary reverse engineering, malware inspection, and vulnerability identification, with lowered refusal thresholds available specifically to verified security professionals. It lands as OpenAI's direct counter-positioning to [[claude-mythos]] Preview's cybersecurity capability — where Mythos is held back from public release over offensive-vulnerability concerns, GPT-5.4-Cyber democratizes a defender-tuned model under a tiered-identity-verification program. The release extends OpenAI's existing security-agent stack ([[aardvark]], [[codex-security]]) into a fine-tuned model surface.
