## Summary

ChatGPT Spaces is [[openai]]'s Sep 29 2026 DevDay-launched **shared workspace surface** where people, ChatGPT, and agents collaborate together. Announced alongside **ChatGPT Pages** (a companion surface that lets users spend their ChatGPT plan quota inside partner apps), Spaces sits at the intersection of the multi-cycle [[chatgpt-work]] agentic-workspace arc and the new [[dots]] persistent-agent surface — Spaces is the *shared canvas* where humans and always-on agents co-work.

The launch canonicalizes OpenAI's post-DevDay 2026 pivot from *individual-user chatbot* to *collaborative workspace tier* — parallel with [[meta-muse-agent]]'s Muse-Charm shared spaces + Anthropic's [[claude-cowork]] on the frontier-lab enterprise-agentic-workspace cohort.
