## Summary

Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is [[google]]'s gated cybersecurity-tuned Gemini model, launched July 2026 alongside [[gemini-3-6-flash]] (workhorse) and [[gemini-3-5-flash-lite]] (budget). Fine-tuned to *find, validate, and patch software vulnerabilities*, it underpins Google's CodeMender autonomous security agent that hunts bugs across codebases and proposes or applies fixes at scale. Access is deliberately restricted to governments and trusted partners under a pilot program citing dual-use risk — the same capabilities that patch vulnerabilities could discover and weaponize them. This mirrors the broader mid-2026 industry pattern of *gated cyber models* set by [[claude-mythos]], [[gpt-5-4-cyber]], and [[gpt-5-5-cyber]], and revives the debate over who should hold automated exploit-finding tools. Early benchmarks reported by Google indicate the model outperforms general-purpose LLMs on vulnerability discovery, including uncovering dozens of unique issues in complex targets like the V8 JavaScript engine.
