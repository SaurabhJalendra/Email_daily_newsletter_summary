## Summary

Muse Voice Transcribe is [[meta]] AI's September 2026 addition to the Muse-family product line — a live, production-ready speech-to-text model with support for tracking 20+ speakers. It positions Meta as a first-party ASR vendor alongside OpenAI Whisper + Deepgram + [[assemblyai]] + Google Chirp substrate, and extends the Muse-family from text/image/video-primary substrate into voice-inclusive multimodal-tier substrate.
