## Summary

QwenGyre is a reinforcement-learning framework for **training AI agents on extremely long-horizon complex tasks**. Framed in the arXiv paper *"QwenGyre: An Elastic Reinforcement Learning Framework for Training xLong-Horizon Agents"* (arxiv.org/abs/2609.33848), it targets the training-infrastructure gap in long-horizon-agent development: as agent-task time-horizons stretch into hundreds or thousands of tool-call steps, standard RL frameworks hit scaling bottlenecks that elastic rollout-and-reward machinery is designed to relieve. The "Qwen" prefix in the framework name places it in the [[alibaba]] Qwen ecosystem's naming-cohort, consistent with Alibaba's active investment in long-horizon-agent research substrate.

QwenGyre sits in the mid-2026 long-horizon-agent-training substrate cluster alongside NVIDIA's VERA resumable-rollout-environment framework and Google's VeriHarness rollout-verification framework.
