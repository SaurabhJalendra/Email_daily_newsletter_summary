## Summary

ChatGPT Health is [[openai]]'s consumer-facing health experience, launched in January 2026. It is a dedicated space inside [[chatgpt]] that securely connects to users' medical records, wellness apps, and (via partnerships) Apple Health data, providing context-aware health insights, reminders, and conversations. It is the consumer half of [[openai]]'s two-track health strategy, paired with the enterprise product [[openai-for-healthcare]] for hospitals. OpenAI acquired health-records startup Torch in January 2026 to provide "persistent medical memory" for ChatGPT Health.
