## Summary

GPT-5.4 is OpenAI's March 2026 flagship, launched 2026-03-07. Headline capabilities: 1M-token context window, native computer-use that drives desktop apps autonomously, embedded use inside Excel and PowerPoint, and a new "x-high" (extreme) reasoning-effort setting that burns additional compute for long-horizon agent work. On OSWorld-Verified it scores 75%, surpassing the human baseline, and tops FrontierMath. The release ships as two ChatGPT modes (Thinking for everyday, Pro for complex) plus two API models (`gpt-5.4`, `gpt-5.4-pro`); knowledge cut-off is 2025-08-31. An earlier leak (2026-03-06) surfaced rumors of a 2M-token window and cross-session "Stateful AI"; the shipped product ended at 1M tokens with no stateful-AI claim, suggesting the leak anticipated a longer roadmap.
