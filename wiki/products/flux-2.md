## Summary

FLUX.2 is [[black-forest-labs]]' late-November 2025 image-generation model, positioned for creative-production workflows. It improves on FLUX.1 in three areas the lab targeted as the gating factors for professional adoption: realism, in-image text rendering, and character consistency across multiple generations. Launch landed in the same week as Google's [[nano-banana-pro]] and Anthropic's [[claude-opus-4-5]], underscoring late-Nov 2025 as a category-wide image-and-multimodal cycle.
