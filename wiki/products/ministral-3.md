## Summary

Ministral 3 is [[mistral]]'s January 2026 family of dense small language models, designed for low-resource / edge environments. The family includes base, instruction-tuned, and reasoning variants — positioning Mistral in the same bracket as Google's [[function-gemma]] (on-device) and Liquid AI's LFM2 family while complementing its flagship [[mistral-3]] (MoE, enterprise).
