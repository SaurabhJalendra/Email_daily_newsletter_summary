## Summary

Marble is [[world-labs]]' flagship commercial world model, launched in mid-November 2025 under Fei-Fei Li's "spatial intelligence" thesis. It generates persistent 3D environments from text, images, video, or 3D layouts — environments that stay stable across interactions rather than hallucinating new geometry on each step. Marble is the first production-grade world-model release to reach consumer framing, and arrives alongside Yann LeCun's departure from Meta to start his own world-model lab, marking world models as the next major frontier-lab battleground after LLMs.
