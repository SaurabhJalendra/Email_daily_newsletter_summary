## Summary

Gemini 3.1 Flash Live is [[google]]'s real-time audio model built for voice-first agents, released 2026-03-28. It powers Gemini Live and Search Live — Search Live launched in parallel across 200+ countries and territories, adding voice + camera conversations over Google Search. The model is positioned as Google's highest-quality voice model to date: 90+ languages, ~2× longer conversation-following than the prior version, better handling of pitch/tone/emphasis, background-noise filtering, and low-latency dialogue tuned for real-world conditions. Benchmarks cited at launch: 90.8% on ComplexFuncBench Audio for multi-step function calling. Developers access it via the Gemini Live API in Google AI Studio through GenAI SDKs, direct WebSocket connection, or partner WebRTC integrations. It's framed as Google's sixth major AI drop in March 2026 alongside [[gemini-3-1-pro]], [[gemini-3-1-flash-lite]], Search Live, Stitch 2.0 with voice design, and Gemini-in-Maps.
