## Summary

WorldParticle is a deep-learning system from MIT, the University of British Columbia, and collaborators designed to simulate diverse physical phenomena — including granular materials and protein folding — using a single shared architecture rather than one specialized simulator per domain. The newsletter coverage frames it as a general-purpose physics model that could reduce the need to maintain separate simulators for each domain and accelerate engineering, scientific discovery, and robotics workflows.
