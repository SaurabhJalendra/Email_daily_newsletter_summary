## Summary

Kolibri is [[aleph-alpha]]'s first open-weight release following the Sep 2026 definitive merger with [[cohere]]. The model is a **78.1-billion-parameter English–German mixture-of-experts** supporting tool calling and contexts up to **one million tokens**, shipped on Hugging Face under the **Apache 2.0** license. Positions the merged cross-Atlantic entity on the open-weight European-sovereign-AI substrate — Kolibri's bilingual English+German focus retains Aleph Alpha's historical European-regulated-enterprise substrate while reaching for the long-context + tool-calling capability bar set by Cohere's Command A+ and Mistral 3.
