## Summary

OpenAI's first consumer hardware product is a screenless, portable smart speaker pitched internally as a "humanlike AI companion that lives in the home", shaped by the ex-Apple industrial design team that once built the iPhone (Jony Ive's group, following the [[openai]] io Products acquisition). Per reporting, it will cost **$200–$300**, ship in **early 2027**, run on [[gpt-live]] (full-duplex listen-and-speak voice), and integrate with the user's emails and broader digital life. Capabilities include controlling smart-home devices, playing media, answering questions, handling messages via ChatGPT, and self-moving mechanical elements with a personality. The device lands the same cycle Apple's trade-secret lawsuit against OpenAI (see [[apple]] cycles 1-5) alleges former Apple hardware staff helped OpenAI develop "consumer hardware plans" — this is the first concrete public sketch of what those plans look like.
