## Summary

Qwen 3.7-Max is Alibaba's May 2026 flagship Qwen, a proprietary closed-weight model capable of **35 hours of continuous autonomous execution** trained via environment scaling — engineered for long-horizon reasoning without logic loops. Ships with a 1-million-token context window, supports cross-harness generalization, and reportedly beats [[claude-opus-4-7]] (Max tier framing) on key math benchmarks. Sits fifth on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index by 2026-05-22 — ahead of [[gemini-3-5-flash]] but trailing Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5. Marks Alibaba's first explicit two-track strategy: Qwen Plus remains open-source while Max moves behind a paywall. Lands alongside Alibaba's new **Zhenwu M890** AI chip (3× performance vs predecessor, 144GB memory, 800GB/s bandwidth) designed for agent workloads, and the Qwen 3.7-Max-Preview hitting #13 on global rankings with strong math/coding.
