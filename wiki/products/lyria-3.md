## Summary

Lyria 3 is Google's music-generation model, integrated directly into Gemini in late February 2026. The initial version ships short 30-second tracks from text prompts or images, with SynthID watermarking and 8-language support. On 2026-03-26/27, Google releases Lyria 3 Pro, extending the model to full-track generation (up to 3 minutes) with explicit structure control — users define intro/verse/chorus/outro arrangement and the model maintains continuity across transitions. Lyria 3 Pro rolls out across Gemini, AI Studio, and Google Vids, positioning Google's music stack against Suno and Udio inside Google's own consumer surfaces.
