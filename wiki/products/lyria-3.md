## Summary

Lyria 3 is Google's music-generation model, integrated directly into Gemini in late February 2026. It creates 30-second music tracks from text prompts or images, supports eight languages, and carries SynthID watermarking — positioning Google's music stack against the broader generative-audio field and grounding music generation inside the main Gemini consumer surface.
