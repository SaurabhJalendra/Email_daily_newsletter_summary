## Summary

Grok Team Bots is [[xai]]'s Sep 30 2026 launch of **shared AI coworkers that learn a team's workflow over time** — a team-scoped evolution of the per-user [[grok-bot]] substrate from August 2026. Each Team Bot is addressable by multiple teammates and accumulates workflow-specific knowledge across the team's history rather than operating from a single user's context, structurally extending Grok Bot's iMessage-style per-agent-dedicated-cloud-computer substrate into a shared-tenant primitive.

The launch lands the same day as [[openai]]'s [[dots]] (persistent per-user cloud-computer agents) and [[meta]]'s Muse for Small Business expansion (see [[muse-for-small-business]]), making Sep 30 2026 a canonical three-frontier-lab shared-and-persistent-agent-substrate inflection window: OpenAI individual-tier + Meta small-business-tier + xAI team-tier. Pairs cycle-structurally with [[claude-corps]] and [[openai-workspace-agents]] as the mid-2026 team-of-agents substrate cohort.
