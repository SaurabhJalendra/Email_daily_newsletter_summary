## Summary

NitroGen is [[nvidia]]'s open-source foundation model for generalist gaming agents, released in early January 2026. Trained on roughly 40,000 hours of gameplay footage spanning more than 1,000 games, NitroGen complements NVIDIA's other open-source pushes — [[nemotron-3]] for agentic LLMs and [[nvidia-cosmos]] for physical-AI — as the gaming-agent tier of the stack and a research substrate for generalist agents that must perceive, plan, and act in highly varied visual environments.
