## Summary

Gas Town is a coding-agent orchestrator built by veteran engineer Steve Yegge (formerly of Google, Amazon). It ran during mid-2026 as one of the higher-profile *"ultra vibed orchestrator"* platforms — a category of coding-agent tools that layer autonomous multi-step planning + tool-use on top of frontier coding models. Yegge shut Gas Town down in mid-September 2026 with a public admission that he had *"never successfully built anything with Gas Town"* despite spending thousands of dollars on coding-agent subscriptions.

Structurally, the shutdown reads as a *canonical mid-2026 reliability-tier failure anchor* for the ultra-vibed-orchestrator category.
