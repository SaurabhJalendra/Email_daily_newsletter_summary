## Summary

Claude Opus 4.6 is Anthropic's February 2026 flagship model — an upgrade to [[claude-opus-4-5]] focused on agentic coding, longer-task persistence, large-codebase performance, and a 1M-token context window (in beta). The model is positioned as the "agent teams" generation: it self-corrects during complex workflows, builds polished UIs from single prompts (Pomodoro app, Pokémon clone demos), and is delivered inside [[claude-cowork]] and Claude.ai. A "fast mode" variant runs ~2.5× faster at higher cost. It is Anthropic's direct counterpart to OpenAI's same-week [[gpt-5-3-codex]] release. Opus 4.6 is succeeded in April 2026 by [[claude-opus-4-7]].
