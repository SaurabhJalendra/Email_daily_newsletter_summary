## Summary

The **Horowitz Andreessen Academy (HAA)** is a16z's Sep 2026 SF-based alternative to traditional college education, officially launched via a16z substack (Ben Horowitz + Marc Andreessen framing). Co-founded by **Gagan Biyani**, HAA is not a college or university but a residential program where students come to San Francisco, learn how to build things, and join a like-minded peer group. Positioned as a *"Nashville for Nerds"* — students pursue their own passions with access to experienced professionals and resources.
