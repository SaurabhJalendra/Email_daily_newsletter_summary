## Summary

GPT-5.6 is the anticipated next iteration of [[openai]]'s flagship GPT-5.x series, expected to follow [[gpt-5-5]] (late April 2026) on a roughly 6-week cadence consistent with OpenAI's rapid model-refresh strategy. As of 2026-06-20, OpenAI has not officially announced GPT-5.6: references come from backend traces (a `gpt-5.6` routing entry briefly appeared in OpenAI's Codex backend logs), leak trackers, and newsletter coverage rather than formal documentation. TLDR AI frames the upcoming launch as scheduled for "Tuesday" (2026-06-23), with potential Mini and Pro variants. The model is reportedly aimed at undercutting Anthropic's pricing — a notable competitive positioning shift given the post-[[claude-fable-5]]-ban + [[claude-mythos]]-deadlock cycle context.

Leaked expectations focus on a significantly larger context window (~1.5M tokens — surpassing [[gpt-5-5]]'s 1M and matching the [[gemini-3-5-flash]] / [[glm-5-2]] 1M tier), better long-horizon tool use and agent behavior, faster Codex response times, stronger multimodal grounding, and improved efficiency and safety. These rumored capabilities target current bottlenecks: working over entire codebases or book-length documents, reducing reasoning drift in long tool-calling sequences, and competing with frontier models like [[claude-opus-4-8]] and Google's Gemini 3.5 in both scale and reliability.
