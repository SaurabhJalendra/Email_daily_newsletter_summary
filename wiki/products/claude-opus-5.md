## Summary

Claude Opus 5 is [[anthropic]]'s July 24 2026 Opus-tier flagship, positioned as **near-frontier intelligence at half the price of [[claude-fable-5]]**. It is Anthropic's fourth model release in under two months (after Mythos 5, Fable 5, and Sonnet 5), and ships as the new default on **Claude Max** and the strongest everyday model on **Claude Pro**. Pricing is held at **$5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens** — identical to [[claude-opus-4-8]] and exactly half of Fable 5's $10/$50 — with a **1M-token context window**, up to **128K output tokens** (300K in batch API beta), and a **May 2026 knowledge cutoff**. New product primitives include an **effort setting** (low/medium/high) for trading off speed vs depth of reasoning, a **Fast mode** in research preview at 2× the token price for higher-speed inference, and **automatic safety fallbacks** that route disallowed requests to lower-tier models rather than hard-blocking — resolving the [[honeycomb]] leak's tiered-safety-instrumentation pattern into a canonical ship-tier feature.

Benchmark posture on launch: new SOTA on **Frontier-Bench v0.1 (43.3%)**, **ARC-AGI 3**, and **GDPval-AA** for coding and knowledge tasks; surpasses Opus 4.8 and narrows the gap with Fable 5, while explicitly still behind Mythos 5 / Fable 5 on cybersecurity and advanced biology. Artificial Analysis called it the new leader on both the **Intelligence Index** and the **AA-Briefcase agentic knowledge work benchmark**, outperforming Fable 5 by nearly 150 Elo while reducing Cost per Task by 20%. Industry framing (Uncovering AI): *"Anthropic is now selling capability in tiers — the model you get access to depends on how much risk the company is willing to hand you, not just how much you're willing to pay"* — first publicly framed *capability-in-tiers-gated-by-risk-tolerance* posture on Anthropic in this wiki. Demo highlight: chetaslua on X showed Opus 5 building a Minecraft replica with detailed physics and lighting, outperforming Fable 5 on the task.
