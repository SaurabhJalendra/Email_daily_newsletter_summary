## Summary

MiniMax H3 is [[minimax]]'s Jul 2026 next-generation multimodal video model. It ships with **native 30-second** clip generation and **consistent three-minute** long-form video, **interactive frame editing**, and **up to 50 multimodal references** per generation. The 50-reference multimodal control surface + interactive frame editing position H3 as a director-tier video-generation substrate, structurally analog to [[seedance-2]] 2.5's 30-second-single-shot + 50-reference-piece capability envelope. The three-minute consistent framing is materially longer than the mid-2026 single-clip-generation frontier (Seedance 2.5's 30 seconds; Veo 3 / Sora 2 sub-minute clips) and pushes MiniMax into the *long-form-consistent-video-tier* on the AI-video-generation cohort.
