## Summary

**Mirendil-Base** is [[mirendil-ai]]'s first publicly disclosed coding model, trained from scratch — notably **by a single researcher** on **less than $10 million in compute** per Jack Clark's Import AI 478 (2026-10-27-morning). Clark frames the result as *"what RSI means"*: the ability for a very small team with modern AI tooling + stronger-base-model-assisted-research to produce a "decent coding model" that **outperforms other models in its class** at a budget two to three orders of magnitude below typical frontier-lab pretraining spends. The blog accompanying the release is titled *"Pretraining at scale without a pretraining team"* (Mirendil).

The result sits structurally alongside the multi-cycle [[recursive-self-improvement]] arc as a *concrete small-team-plus-stronger-base-model-tool-use → competitive-coding-model canonical anchor* at the lean-pretraining tier.
