## Summary

MatrAIx is a Harvard + MIT research project that ships a **digital world simulating Earth's population with 8.3 billion agents**, each designed to mirror real humans and their behavior at scale. The simulation combines real human profiles from public records with synthetic agents, each having its own persona — a canonical *multi-agent-at-planet-scale* social-simulation substrate.

The scale-anchor is the load-bearing signal: 8.3B agents matches the current human-population count, positioning MatrAIx as a *"digital twin of Earth's population"* — structurally distinct from prior agent-simulation platforms (Stanford's Smallville, Meta's simulated-village studies) that operate at the *N-hundred-agent* tier. If validated, MatrAIx would establish planet-scale multi-agent simulation as a viable substrate for policy modeling, epidemiology, behavioral economics, and adversarial-social-dynamics research.
