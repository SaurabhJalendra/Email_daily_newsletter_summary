## Summary

AI Edge Gallery is [[google]]'s offline, on-device AI capability for phones — a runtime and model catalog that lets users run AI models directly on their devices without sending data to the cloud. Released in late December 2025, it positions Google alongside the broader wave of on-device AI frameworks (e.g. Cactus) and aligns with the company's specialist small-model releases such as [[function-gemma]] and T5Gemma 2.
