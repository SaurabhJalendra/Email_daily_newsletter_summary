## Summary

Koa is a Sep 2026 **enterprise reasoning model** jointly built by [[salesforce]] and [[nvidia]], **post-trained on NVIDIA's open-weight [[nemotron-3]] Super** foundation for sales, marketing, and customer-support workflows. It powers Salesforce's sales and support agents — updating deals, routing tickets, performing structured CRM actions — and is positioned as an **enterprise-focused alternative** to frontier chat models from [[anthropic]] ([[claude-opus-5]]) and [[openai]] ([[chatgpt]]).

Structurally significant as *Salesforce's first named own-branded reasoning model* — extending the [[claudeforce]] joint-Anthropic-plugin arc (Aug 2026) into a *native-Salesforce-authored own-model tier*. Reads as Salesforce hedging its Anthropic-first strategic-vendor bet (April 2026 investment + $2.6B gain + [[claudeforce]] Aug 2026 shipment) with a *NVIDIA-Nemotron-Super substrate own-model* to reduce single-vendor-dependency at the reasoning-agent tier.
