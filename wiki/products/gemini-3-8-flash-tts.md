## Summary

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS is Google's Sep 2026 text-to-speech + voice-design tool. On launch, it grabbed the **#1 spot on Hume AI's voice-design benchmark** and top rankings in blind listening tests across Japanese, Hindi, and Arabic.
