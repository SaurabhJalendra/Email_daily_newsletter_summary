## Summary

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS is Google's Sep 23-24, 2026 text-to-speech + voice-design tool. It supports **custom voice design from natural-language descriptions**, **voice cloning from a 30-second audio sample**, **multi-speaker dialogue with natural turn-taking**, and **over 100 languages and dialects**. Line-by-line delivery control includes acting cues, pacing, accents, dialect changes, and conversational backchanneling. A companion **Flash-Lite TTS** variant is optimized for faster, lower-cost, high-volume production and real-time voice applications. On launch, Flash TTS grabbed the **#1 spot on Hume AI's voice-design benchmark** and top rankings in blind listening tests across Japanese, Hindi, and Arabic. Both models are generally available through the Gemini API + Google AI Studio, with Gemini Notebook + enterprise availability expanding. Consent-plus-provenance safeguards: replication requires a matching verbal-consent recording from the voice owner; generated audio includes SynthID watermarking; replicated voices carry C2PA content credentials. Feature is restricted in some jurisdictions (UK, EEA, India, Texas, Illinois). Successor to [[gemini-3-1-flash-tts]] on the Gemini-3.x TTS family arc.
