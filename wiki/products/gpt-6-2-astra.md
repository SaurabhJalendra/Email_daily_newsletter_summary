## Summary

**GPT-6.2 Astra** is an unannounced [[openai]] model variant and apparent successor to [[gpt-6-1-astra]], reportedly spotted in Codex usage analytics and related telemetry in late October and early November 2026. Reports indicate that some users saw the identifier **`gpt-6.2-astra`** alongside GPT-6 Astra, potentially reflecting an **A/B test, canary deployment, or staged rollout**; however, OpenAI had not officially announced the model or listed it in its public API catalog as of **November 3, 2026**.

The apparent sightings matter because they may signal an imminent frontier-model release focused on **coding, long-running agentic tasks, and improved reliability** — the same capability axes along which GPT-6.1 Terra (fast/cheap computer-use variant) and GPT-6 Rosalind Research (life-sciences specialty) have already been productized. Claims about benchmarks, architecture, pricing, safety improvements, or a canceled GPT-6.1-Astra checkpoint remain unverified, so the immediate industry impact is primarily **speculative**: developers and competitors are monitoring the model as a possible near-term escalation in AI coding and agent capabilities rather than treating it as a publicly available product. Primary-source confirmation from OpenAI is still absent.
