## Summary

**GPT-6.2 Astra** is [[openai]]'s Nov 17 2026 flagship frontier model for demanding professional work and the successor to GPT-6 Astra on the [[openai-astra]] substrate. Launched alongside the lower-cost companion [[gpt-6-2-sol]], Astra targets **complex reasoning, coding, computer use, and producing documents, spreadsheets, presentations, websites, and 3D designs**. OpenAI reports a **1.05-million-token context window**, support for **text and image input**, and distribution through **ChatGPT Work, Codex, the OpenAI API, Microsoft Foundry, and Amazon Bedrock**. Pricing is **$8 per million input tokens**, **$0.40 per million cached input tokens** (a 60% discount), and **$40 per million output tokens** — 20% less than GPT-6 Astra on input and output tokens.

Astra is positioned as OpenAI's most capable externally deployed model, achieving stronger professional-work and scientific-research evaluation results than GPT-6 Astra at **under one-third of the cost per task**. The accompanying system card classifies Astra at the **Critical threshold for cybersecurity**, and **High thresholds for biological and chemical capabilities and AI self-improvement**, underscoring both its capabilities and the need for heightened deployment safeguards. The launch intensifies competition with recent frontier-model releases from [[anthropic]] (Claude Fable 5.2 + Mythos 5.2) and [[google]] (Gemini 4 Carbon + Gemini 4 Hydrogen) and is likely to accelerate enterprise adoption of AI agents for end-to-end knowledge work, while raising scrutiny around model safety, evaluation standards, and operational controls.
