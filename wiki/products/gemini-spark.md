## Summary

Gemini Spark is Google's always-on personal AI agent announced at Google I/O 2026 (2026-05-19) — runs 24/7 on dedicated Google Cloud VMs and continues working when the user's device is closed. Powered by [[gemini-3-5-flash]] and Google's Antigravity agent harness, it taps directly into Gmail, Google Docs, Calendar, Slides, Chrome, and third-party apps via MCP, performing background tasks like parsing credit-card statements for hidden subscriptions, monitoring school emails for deadlines, drafting reports across documents, and scheduled multi-step workflows. Bundled in the AI Ultra plan at $100/month (down from initial $250 price) and an upcoming $200 enterprise tier. Pairs with [[gemini-omni]] (any-input video) and [[antigravity-2]] (parallel desktop agents) as the "agentic Gemini era" trifecta from I/O.
