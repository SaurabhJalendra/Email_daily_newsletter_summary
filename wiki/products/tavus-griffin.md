## Summary

Griffin is [[tavus]]'s October 2, 2026 "Human Interaction Model" — a real-time video+voice avatar system that can look, listen, and talk back live on a video call. The model combines computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning to understand user input and respond with video output that includes nodding and references to objects visible on the user's screen. In live video-call tests, nearly half of participants (~48%) reportedly mistook Griffin for a human — the first-in-wiki Turing-test-adjacent concrete datapoint on any live-video avatar substrate.

Structurally, Griffin productizes the Turing-test-adjacent live-video-interaction tier, competing with Synthesia Sessions (same-cycle launched for roleplay and survey interviews) and the broader avatar-and-voice-agent cohort. Tavus is currently gating a Griffin-Lite variant to trusted users while safety and disclosure features (likely watermarking + "AI participant" disclosure) are refined ahead of broader rollout.
