## Summary

Pi 1.0 is [[earendil]]'s first stable release of its open-source AI coding-agent harness, announced October 1, 2026 and covered as the AINews cycle headline on October 2. It retains Pi's deliberately minimal, extensible design while adding native [[model-context-protocol]] (MCP) support, Codemode, image-model support, virtual-model extensions, deferred tool loading, [[anthropic]] cache warming, transcript-aware mid-conversation system messages, and a redesigned full-screen terminal interface. The harness is MIT-licensed and user-customizable rather than a prescriptive, vendor-locked platform.

Structurally, Pi 1.0 enters the mid-2026 coding-agent-harness cohort alongside [[claude-code]], Codex CLI, Aider, and other user-controllable harnesses — differentiated by its minimal substrate design and extension-first architecture. A companion [[pi-durable]] TypeScript port was announced simultaneously, offering crash survival, portability, concurrency, extensibility, context management, and multiplayer/state sync — targeting production-grade reliability on the same minimal-core design.
