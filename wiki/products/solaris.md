## Summary

Solaris is Runway's September 2026 research preview of a new "Interface World Model" (IWM) family — a class of generative video models that render website and application UIs as live, frame-by-frame video conditioned on real user input (clicks, drags, typing, voice) rather than executing traditional HTML/CSS/JavaScript. Built on Runway's [[runway-gen-4-5]] video model, Solaris targets ~720p resolution and sub-500ms response latency to feel like a real-time UI rather than a pre-rendered animation. Commentators frame Solaris as a potential step toward a "no-code internet" and productize a canonical two-stage architecture: an LLM decides what should happen next (reasoning), and a video model draws the next frames (rendering).
