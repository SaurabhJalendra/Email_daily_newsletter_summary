## Summary

Ironwood is [[google]]'s seventh-generation TPU, announced 2025-11-07 alongside the Axion VM line. It targets large-scale AI training and inference and sits at the center of Google's custom-silicon story — the hardware side of deals like the multi-year TPU supply relationship with [[anthropic]]. Alongside Ironwood, Google used the same launch window to drop AlphaEvolve (evolutionary-coding + LLM solver) and the File Search built-in RAG feature for the Gemini API, bundling silicon, model, and product announcements into a single "full-stack" moment.
