## Summary

Nano Banana Pro is the production name for Google's Gemini 3 Pro Image model — a studio-grade image generator/editor that runs a reasoning step before rendering, applying design knowledge, geolocation context, and physics understanding to keep details consistent. It produces 4K visuals, accepts up to 14 inputs at once, holds character consistency across up to 5 subjects, and ships SynthID watermarks plus C2PA provenance. Distribution is unusually broad at launch: native in Gemini, Google Search, Workspace, Google Ads, and through partners Canva, Figma, Adobe Firefly/Photoshop, and Antigravity. Subsequent coverage (Nov 26) emphasizes outfit swaps, high-detail 4K rendering, and direct competition with [[adobe]]'s Firefly stack.
