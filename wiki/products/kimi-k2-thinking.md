## Summary

Kimi K2 Thinking is an open-source reasoning, coding, and agent-style model from China-based [[moonshot-ai]] (branded "Kimi"). Released in early November 2025, it became the first open-source Chinese model to explicitly outperform leading US closed-source offerings — including OpenAI's GPT-5 and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5 — on several public benchmarks. Coverage through mid-November framed Kimi K2 Thinking alongside [[deepseek]] and [[alibaba]]'s Qwen line as evidence that the "Chinese open-source catches up" narrative had hardened into a reproducible pattern. A production API with high uptime / scalability claims followed the open-weights release.
