## Summary

Google TPU v8 is [[google]]'s eighth-generation Tensor Processing Unit, formally announced 2026-04-24 with significantly improved performance and efficiency for AI workloads. It launches as the explicit Google response to [[nvidia]]'s GPU dominance — coverage frames it as "cost-efficient, full-stack AI infrastructure" that competes head-to-head with NVIDIA in AI inference (Apr 21 preview) and AI workloads broadly (Apr 24 formal launch). The release continues the [[ironwood-tpu]] (7th gen) → v8 progression and underpins both Google's internal AI workloads and Anthropic's TPU-class compute commitments.
