## Summary

The Machine Age Fund is a $1.1B dedicated venture vehicle raised by [[andreessen-horowitz|Andreessen Horowitz]] and announced Aug 2026, targeting the **physical infrastructure of AI**: processors and other semiconductors, memory, networking gear, storage, data centers, cooling, robotics, edge devices, and AI-enabled home appliances. TechCrunch and TLDR Hardware frame it as a *"notable pivot for a firm best known for backing software"* — a16z's structural bet that AI's next phase requires large-scale capital in compute, data-center capacity, and specialized hardware rather than more model-layer plays.

The fund sits alongside a16z's separate **$8.5B fifth Growth fund** (closed Aug 2026) and stacks onto ~$15B of aggregate 2026 a16z fundraising, with roughly 40% of new capital going into AI-related bets across foundation-model labs, infrastructure platforms, and AI-native applications. Announced by Ben Horowitz, Martin Casado, Raghu Raghuram, David Ulevitch, and David George; explicit precedent-portfolio companies cited include Skydio, SpaceX, Anduril, and Waymo.
