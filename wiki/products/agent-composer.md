## Summary

Agent Composer is Contextual AI's agent-building tool aimed at expert engineering workflows — root-cause analysis, test-code generation, diagnostic reasoning — in high-stakes sectors like semiconductors, aerospace, logistics, and finance. Early deployments reportedly reduced 8-hour root-cause investigations to ~20 minutes and achieved up to 60× faster issue resolution, positioning Agent Composer as a vertical-specialist complement to horizontal agent products like [[openai-codex]] and [[claude-code]].
