## Summary

MAI-Transcribe-2 is [[microsoft]]'s September 2026 speech-recognition model shipped under the MAI (Microsoft AI) family alongside the [[mai-thinking-1]] reasoning-tier substrate. Microsoft positions the model as market-disrupting on three axes simultaneously — faster, more accurate, and cheaper than existing competitors — pricing it at **$0.10 per hour of audio** across **60 languages**. The launch places Microsoft into direct competitive posture against [[assemblyai]] (Voice Agent API), [[fish-audio]] (S2.1 Pro), and [[elevenlabs]] on the enterprise-tier speech-transcription substrate.

The model has potential to reshape the speech-recognition market by offering a more efficient and cost-effective solution for large-scale transcription workloads (call-center + meeting-transcription + media-captioning + accessibility use cases), while continuing to build out Microsoft's MAI foundation-model family as a Nvidia-decoupled enterprise-AI substrate.
