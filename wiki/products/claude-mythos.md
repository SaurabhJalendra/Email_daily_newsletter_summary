## Summary

Claude Mythos is an unreleased [[anthropic]] model first surfaced via a leaked (later deleted) Anthropic blog post on 2026-03-28 and maintained in controlled/preview-only state through April 2026. It is framed as the most powerful Claude model built to date — outperforming peers on coding, academic-reasoning, and cybersecurity benchmarks — and positioned above [[claude-opus-4-7]] in Anthropic's lineup. Release is reportedly held back to give cybersecurity organizations time to harden systems against AI-driven attacks the model enables. By mid-April Anthropic reports Mythos Preview has uncovered thousands of previously undetected zero-day vulnerabilities and powers [[project-glasswing]] — a critical-software security coalition with AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA — with regulators warning major banks about cybersecurity risks tied to Mythos exposure.
