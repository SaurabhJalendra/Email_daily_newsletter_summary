## Summary

**Tivadar Danka** is a mathematician and machine-learning educator and the creator of **The Palindrome**, one of the fastest-growing mathematics and ML newsletters on [[substack]] (reportedly >48,000 subscribers as of October 2026 per research context). His writing emphasizes *durable mathematical intuition and from-scratch implementations* — graph theory, neural networks from scratch, and the mathematics of machine learning — at a time when AI education is dominated by rapidly-changing tooling and hype. The posture is explicitly code-first + math-first: narrow the gap between *using* AI systems and *understanding* the mathematics and algorithms behind them.

Danka sits in the same mid-2026 open-education cohort as [[sebastian-raschka]] (LLMs-from-scratch / [[ahead-of-ai]]), [[andrej-karpathy]] (nanoGPT / nanochat), [[hamel-husain]] (AI Evals), and [[andrew-ng]] (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera) — practitioners whose code-along + math-along artifacts are the de-facto on-ramp into frontier ML technique for engineers and researchers. In late 2026 he is expanding The Palindrome beyond written newsletters into a broader educational platform: a YouTube channel, books (*Machine Learning From Zero* + a compiled mathematics-and-ML guide), live workshops, contributor collaboration, and open-source publishing tooling.
