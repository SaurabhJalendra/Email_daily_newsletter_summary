## Summary

Project Meridian is a **120-day Pentagon AI-weapons futures study** introduced on Oct 1 2026 by Secretary of Defense **Pete Hegseth**. The study is co-led by **Elon Musk** (CEO of [[xai]] + [[tesla]] + [[spacex]]), **Newt Gingrich** (former Speaker of the US House of Representatives), and **Palmer Luckey** (co-founder of [[anduril]]), and aims to identify which AI technologies the US military should develop. Framed by The Rundown AI as a *"120-day hunt for futuristic weapons"* and by Evolving AI Insights as *"a study that aims to identify which AI technologies the military should develop, with a focus on AI, robotics, and biotechnology."*
