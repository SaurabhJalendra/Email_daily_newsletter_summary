> **Type**: topic

> **Related**: [[tinker-api]], [[thinking-machines]]

## Summary

Fine-tuning remains the main mechanism for adapting pre-trained LLMs to specific domains, tasks, or style/persona. Early October 2025 saw [[thinking-machines]] launch [[tinker-api]] as a dedicated fine-tuning platform — notable because it positioned fine-tuning as infrastructure coming from a high-profile ex-OpenAI lab rather than from a frontier-model vendor's SDK.
