## Summary

**Sebastian Raschka, PhD** is a machine-learning researcher, LLM research engineer, and educator best known for publishing the [[ahead-of-ai]] newsletter and for authoring the pedagogical *"from scratch"* line of LLM books and video courses. His LLMs-from-scratch educational GitHub repository has reportedly passed 100,000 stars, and his 2026 book *Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)* extends the same hands-on, code-first approach from base LLMs to the reasoning-model stack (evaluation, inference-time scaling, reinforcement learning, and distillation). He sits in the same mid-2026 open-education cohort as [[andrej-karpathy]] (nanoGPT / nanochat), [[hamel-husain]] (AI Evals), and [[andrew-ng]] (DeepLearning.AI / Coursera) — practitioners whose code-along artifacts are the de-facto on-ramp into frontier LLM technique for engineers and researchers.

His recent writing covers post-training of open-weight models, efficient reasoning, looped-transformer architectures, agent training, and new open-weight LLM releases. The posture is explicitly reproducibility-first: implement the technique in Python / PyTorch, explain it, and release the code — rather than treating commercial models as black boxes.
