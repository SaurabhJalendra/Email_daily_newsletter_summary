## Summary

The Agentic AI Foundation is a Linux Foundation project announced on 2025-12-11 with [[openai]], [[anthropic]], and Block as founding members. It is positioned as the neutral coordination body for open standards in the agentic AI stack — with [[anthropic]] simultaneously donating [[model-context-protocol]] (MCP) as the foundation's first major project. Coverage on 2025-12-12 expanded the framing to include [[google]] participation. The Foundation marks the first explicit attempt at cross-lab governance of agent interop standards, mirroring the Linux Foundation's role in cloud-native and open-source AI tooling.
