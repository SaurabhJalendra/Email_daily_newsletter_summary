## Summary

Andrew Ng is the Google Brain / Coursera / DeepLearning.AI co-founder positioning **AI Engineering** as the next major professional track for software developers. In August 2026 he published the *AI Engineering Skills Map* based on analysis of over 10,000 job postings + interviews with hiring managers and recruiters, defining four core skill areas: (1) building and deploying AI applications, (2) software engineering fundamentals, (3) mastering coding agents, and (4) shaping the build (product + business judgment). The framework is explicitly broader than the "AI Engineer" job title — Ng frames it as a cross-cutting skill set every modern developer will need before 2027. Coursera separately backed his AI-education startup *LearnVector* with a $100M investment, signaling industry-scale training for AI engineers is now a strategic priority. Ng sits alongside [[hamel-husain]] as one of the mid-2026 canonical educator figures productizing the *evals + engineering + agents* skill stack.
