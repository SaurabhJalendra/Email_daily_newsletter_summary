## Summary

WAICO — the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization — is a China-led intergovernmental AI governance body launched July 16, 2026 in Shanghai at the World AI Conference (WAIC). It was signed by 29 founding member countries including Russia, Belarus, Serbia, Cuba, Brazil, Venezuela, Kazakhstan, Pakistan, Indonesia, and Laos. **Notably absent** from the founding roster are the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and most European Union member states — the split reads as a Global-South-plus-China alternative to any US-led AI-governance body, and lands the same week [[google]] DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis publicly called for a US-initiated FINRA-style Frontier AI Standards Body.

Xi Jinping's WAIC opening keynote framed WAICO as promoting *"a people-centered approach"* and warned against *"overstretching national-security concepts in AI"* — a direct critique of the US export-control regime that pulled [[claude-mythos]] and delayed [[claude-fable-5]]. WAICO's stated goals: promote international cooperation, develop AI regulation across countries, ensure AI is beneficial and safe for humans, and narrow the digital divide via development and capacity-building. WAIC 2026 also featured Huawei unveiling the **Atlas 950 SuperPoD**, claiming 6.7× the compute of NVIDIA's NVL144 — pairing WAICO's governance signal with a competitive hardware-tier signal that reads as *China building both the institutional and the silicon substrate for a non-US-led AI order*.
