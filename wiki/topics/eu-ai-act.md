## Summary

The **EU AI Act** is the European Union's comprehensive AI regulation. Its **transparency obligations went into full enforcement across Europe on 2026-08-02**, requiring AI systems to disclose when users are interacting with AI and to label AI-generated or altered content (including deepfakes). Fines can reach **€15M or 3% of global turnover** for non-compliance, and the European Commission's **AI Office** has already issued formal enforcement penalties reportedly totaling **€47M against three companies** in early August 2026 — the first significant sanctions under the fully implemented Act. Some high-risk-system obligations are deferred to 2027-2028, but the transparency layer is already live and shaping practitioner defaults around dataset curation, logging, deployment, and user-facing disclosure UX for chatbots, generative models, and biometric systems.

The Act sits at the center of a broader "enforcement era" — global AI regulation shifting from drafting to active enforcement — alongside US state laws (California AI Transparency Act SB 942 / AB 853 effective 2026-08-02, plus Texas / Illinois / Utah AI-related laws) and EU-adjacent AI + cybersecurity action plans emphasizing detection, labeling, and oversight of synthetic media.

## Key Facts

- Full enforcement of transparency obligations across Europe: **2026-08-02**
- Enforcement authority: European Commission's **AI Office** + national authorities
- Maximum fines: **€15M or 3% of global turnover** for non-compliance
- First reported enforcement penalties: **€47M against three companies** (early August 2026)
- Required disclosures: users must be told they are interacting with AI; AI-generated or altered content (including deepfakes) must be labeled
- Deferred obligations: some high-risk system requirements deferred to 2027-2028
- Parallel US-state layer: California AI Transparency Act (SB 942 / AB 853) operative 2026-08-02; Texas + Illinois + Utah AI-related laws also active
- Adjacent EU action plans: cybersecurity + AI plans emphasize detection, labeling, oversight of synthetic media + biometric/emotion recognition
