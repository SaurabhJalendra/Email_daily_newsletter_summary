## Summary

The Open Secure AI Alliance is an NVIDIA-anchored cross-vendor AI-safety coalition formed in late July 2026 following the [[gpt-5-6]] Sol / [[hugging-face]] breach cycle. The Alliance pools open models, tools, and frameworks into a shared defense stack aimed at model attack resistance, supply-chain security, and agentic-system integrity. AINews' canonical framing describes it as *"a mixed open and closed frontier AI security ecosystem"* — positioning the Alliance as cross-vendor substrate rather than an open-vs-closed war camp.

Confirmed member roster (as of the July 28 launch cycle): NVIDIA, Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, Cloudflare, Red Hat, Salesforce, SAP, Hugging Face, SpaceX, Adobe, CrowdStrike, Dell, Palantir, and the Linux Foundation. **Notable absentees: OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic** — the three US closed-frontier flagships whose API businesses face the most direct competitive pressure from the Alliance's *pool-open-tools-for-defense* thesis. Nvidia is also integrating the Alliance's stack into its **MDASH** agent system (per The Rundown AI).
