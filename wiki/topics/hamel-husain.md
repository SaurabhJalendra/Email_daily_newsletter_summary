## Summary

**Hamel Husain** is an independent AI-evals practitioner, founder of [[parlance-labs]], and one of the two co-canonical educators for the mid-2026 "evals as core engineering discipline" movement (paired with Shreya Shankar). Prior work at GitHub (Copilot-adjacent ML), Airbnb, and fast.ai (nbdev). He co-teaches the *AI Evals for Engineers & PMs* Maven course that has trained thousands of engineers/PMs including staff at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta, and co-releases the free open-source [[evals-skills]] bundle for coding agents. His public posture reframes LLM evaluation from ad-hoc "vibe checks" into a repeatable trace-review + failure-mode analysis + LLM-as-judge validation loop.
