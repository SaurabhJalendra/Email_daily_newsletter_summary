## Summary

John Jumper is a research scientist who co-led the development of AlphaFold at Google DeepMind, the protein-structure-prediction system that won him a share of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry (alongside Demis Hassabis and David Baker). At Google DeepMind he held a VP-tier position and remained one of the public faces of Google's AI-for-science strategy. In June 2026, multiple newsletters report Jumper has left Google to join [[anthropic]], in the same 48-hour window that *Attention Is All You Need* co-author Noam Shazeer departed Google for [[openai]] — Uncovering AI's framing: "Google loses two Nobel-level minds in 48 hours. This is bigger than a talent story."

The hire connects directly to [[anthropic]]'s 2026-06-21-morning "wet labs and AI-for-science infrastructure" build-out — the company is signaling an explicit move into the AI-for-biology / drug-discovery / structural-science space that AlphaFold has dominated, and Jumper is the strongest possible signal of intent at the personnel layer.
