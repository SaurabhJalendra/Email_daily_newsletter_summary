## Summary

Orbital data centers — putting AI training/inference compute into space — moved from speculative slide deck to capital-formation reality in May 2026. SpaceX's S-1 IPO filing (2026-05-20) discloses plans to deploy data centers in space as early as 2028 and a request for FCC approval to launch up to **one million satellites** for the purpose. Google's [[project-suncatcher]] paired with SpaceX in rocket-launch talks for orbital TPU clusters. New entrant Cowboy Space Corporation (founded by Robinhood co-founder Baiju Bhatt) raised **$275M at $2B valuation** to build rockets carrying AI data centers, with the first launch target before end of 2028 — satellites built directly into the rocket's second stage, each generating 1 megawatt of power for ~800 onboard GPUs. NVIDIA announced AI computing modules designed specifically for space-based data centers and satellite constellations. Anthropic and xAI were reported exploring orbital data centers same window. The thesis: orbital infrastructure could become "the lowest-cost location for AI compute" per SpaceX's framing — exploits constant solar power, sub-zero passive cooling, and zero terrestrial land/water/energy constraints.
