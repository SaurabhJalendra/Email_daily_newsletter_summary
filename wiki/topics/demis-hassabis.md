## Summary

Demis Hassabis cofounded DeepMind in 2010 and led it as CEO through its Google acquisition (2014), the AlphaGo era, and its 2023 merger with Google Brain into **Google DeepMind**. He shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with [[john-jumper]] for the AlphaFold protein-structure work. As DeepMind CEO he sat atop [[google]]'s frontier-model program — Gemini, AlphaFold, AlphaProof, Deep Think, robotics — and functioned as one of the mid-2020s frontier-lab CEO cohort alongside Sam Altman ([[openai]]) and Dario Amodei ([[anthropic]]).

On 2026-08-05 tokens& Weekly reported that Hassabis is **stepping down as DeepMind CEO** — a significant leadership transition for one of the three frontier labs — paired with Jeff Dean's announcement that he is **leaving Google entirely**. Successor and next-step details are not yet public.
