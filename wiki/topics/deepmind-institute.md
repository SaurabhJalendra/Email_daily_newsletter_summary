## Summary

The DeepMind Institute is Google DeepMind's September 2026 in-house think tank for AGI-preparedness research — a formal organizational vehicle for publishing work on how society should adapt to advanced AI systems. It is directed by three named leaders: Demis Hassabis (DeepMind cofounder + Alphabet chief scientist per prior 2026-08-06-morning anchor), Shane Legg (DeepMind cofounder), and James Manyika (former McKinsey Global Institute chairman + Google SVP for research, technology & society). The trio combines cofounder-tier scientific authority with economist/institutional-adaptation research tradition — reads as a *research-institute-with-cofounder-and-economist leadership canonical anchor*.

The Institute's research scope covers three axes: (1) safe AGI development, (2) economic and social effects, and (3) institutional adaptation. It emphasizes an iterative-scientific-methodology posture — *"competing ideas and revising views as new evidence emerges"* — positioning itself as a debate-and-revision forum rather than a dogmatic-position-publishing body.
