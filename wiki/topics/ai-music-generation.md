## Summary

AI music generation entered the main Gemini surface in late February 2026 when [[google]] integrated [[lyria-3]] into Gemini — users generate 30-second tracks from text or images, with 8-language support and SynthID watermarking. The move brings frontier-lab music generation alongside established players like Suno and Udio, and puts provenance (SynthID) front and center given ongoing copyright disputes around generative audio.
