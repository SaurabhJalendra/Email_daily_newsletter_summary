## Summary

AI browsers are a product category where the browser itself acts as an AI runtime — remembering context across sessions, automating routine tasks, and exposing agentic search. In early October 2025 the two reference products were [[opera-neon]] and [[perplexity-comet]], both positioned as direct competitors to Microsoft's Copilot-in-Edge and Google's Chrome AI features.
