## Summary

"World models" refers to AI systems that learn internal, physics-aware representations of environments — they understand 3D space, object permanence, and causal physical dynamics rather than just surface pixels or tokens. Across November 2025 the category crystallized into a distinct frontier-lab battleground alongside LLMs: Yann LeCun announced his departure from [[meta]] to start a world-model startup, Fei-Fei Li's [[world-labs]] launched [[marble]] (the first commercial production-grade world model generating persistent 3D environments from text/images/video), and [[nvidia-cosmos]] continued to anchor the physical-AI-foundation-model end of the stack. Framing throughout the month paired world models with "spatial intelligence" as the follow-on frontier after language.
