## Summary

AEF-1 is a voluntary standard published by the [[ai-evaluator-forum]] (formed Dec 2025) defining minimum operating conditions for independent third-party evaluations of frontier AI systems. It codifies five principles: (1) sufficient access and resources, (2) minimized conflicts of interest, (3) analytic autonomy, (4) transparent methods and results, and (5) protection of sensitive information. Evaluators are expected to publish an AEF-1 compliance checklist alongside results, documenting exceptions where literal compliance isn't possible.

The standard emerged Sep 15, 2026 as [[xai]], [[openai]], and [[anthropic]] all co-signed — the first cross-frontier-lab commitment to a shared evaluator-governance baseline. [[anthropic]] additionally committed unilaterally to giving embedded evaluators employee-like access (desks in offices, access badges, company laptops). The move formalizes evaluation norms that had previously been ad-hoc or bilateral (Apollo, METR, UK AISI).
