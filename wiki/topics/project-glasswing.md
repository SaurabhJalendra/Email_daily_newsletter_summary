## Summary

Project Glasswing is an [[anthropic]]-led cybersecurity coalition announced in April 2026 that uses the unreleased [[claude-mythos]] Preview model to detect and fix vulnerabilities in critical software at scale. Partner list spans most of the US hyperscaler stack — AWS ([[amazon]]), [[apple]], [[google]], [[microsoft]], and [[nvidia]] — positioning Glasswing as an industry-level vulnerability-disclosure initiative rather than a single-vendor tool. The model's capability to surface thousands of previously undetected zero-days is the lever; the coalition structure is framed as the governance mechanism that makes public release of Mythos defensible at all. In early June 2026 the program expanded from a small partner pilot to **150 additional organizations across 15+ countries**, a meaningful step up in [[claude-mythos]] exposure even as the model itself remains unreleased to the general public.

Coverage in the 2026-04-13 cycle ambiguously re-frames "Project Glasswing" as hackers that breached Mythos — this likely reflects newsletter conflation of the codename rather than a change in actor identity, but the contradiction is unresolved at last ingest.
