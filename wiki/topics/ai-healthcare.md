## Summary

By early 2026 healthcare became a concurrent priority at every major AI lab. In a two-week burst OpenAI shipped consumer-facing [[chatgpt-health]] (with medical-records/Apple-Health integration) and enterprise [[openai-for-healthcare]] (HIPAA, GPT-5-class); Anthropic launched Claude for Healthcare as a counterpart; Google updated MedGemma to 1.5; Stanford's SleepFM (predicts 130 diseases from one night of sleep data) became an anchor open-source example; and [[nvidia]] + Eli Lilly announced a $1B AI drug-discovery lab. Regulatory progress matched the product wave — Utah became the first US state to allow AI-autonomous prescription-refill approval.
