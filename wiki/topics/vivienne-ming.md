## Summary

**Vivienne Ming** is a neuroscientist, machine-learning researcher, entrepreneur, and **Chief Scientist at Possibility Sciences**. She is best known in the AI/ML space for advancing the **[[hybrid-intelligence]]** framing: human–AI systems designed to outperform either humans or models alone, with value measured by the outcomes of the human–machine pair rather than by model benchmarks. In 2026 experiments she described a small group of "cyborgs" who actively challenged AI outputs and explored alternative explanations — these teams outperformed the strongest individual humans and the strongest standalone models. The strongest predictors of performance were human traits (curiosity, intellectual humility, fluid intelligence, perspective-taking) rather than access to the largest model.

Her Oct 2026 presence spans an INSEAD AI Forum Americas keynote and the companion [[insead-knowledge]] essay *"What 'Mad Scientist' Vivienne Ming Taught Us About Hybrid Intelligence"* (publishing Oct 26 2026), which argues AI should *challenge people to think better* rather than merely provide answers. Her framing sits alongside [[ethan-mollick]]'s *co-intelligence* framing as a durable practitioner-tier anchor on the human-AI-collaboration arc.
