## Summary

A **Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** is a customer-embedded software/ML engineer who sits inside an enterprise's environment to discover use cases, design data pipelines and integrations, and build production AI systems directly alongside the customer's teams. Unlike traditional solutions engineers who mainly support pre-sales and then hand off to services, FDEs own the full lifecycle from discovery interviews through deployed outcomes, and are measured and compensated on business impact (productivity gains, cost reduction, new revenue) rather than sales pipeline or billable hours. The role originated at Palantir and has spread rapidly across frontier AI labs and hyperscalers as enterprises shift from AI proofs-of-concept to production systems.

By mid-2026 FDE has become one of the highest-paying and most strategically important roles in applied AI. **Palantir**, **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, **Cohere**, and others hire FDEs as a core go-to-market motion. **AWS launched a dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering organization backed by a $1B investment**, embedding "frontier" engineers plus agentic AI tools directly inside customer teams. **Microsoft** announced a new multi-billion-dollar AI business staffed with thousands of forward-deployment engineers. Job postings for FDE-style roles have surged (~800% YoY per one analysis) with US total compensation averaging ~$238K and senior packages at frontier labs commonly exceeding $500K–$600K including equity. Compensation at leading companies ranges from $135K to $1.2M+ total comp.
