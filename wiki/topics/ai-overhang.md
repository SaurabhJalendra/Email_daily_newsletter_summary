## Summary

The "AI Overhang" is [[ethan-mollick]]'s framing (canonicalized in his 18-Sep-2026 One Useful Thing essay "The Overhang") that frontier LLM capabilities — GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Opus 5 — have already produced *transformative-in-hours* work in real domains (a Zork-to-3D-adventure port, a 3D reconstruction of Umberto Eco's library), but the median individual and organization has not caught up on how to use them. The overhang is the gap between what the models can do when properly guided and what most people ever prompt them to do.

Mollick's four-tier human-advantage framework — **deep knowledge, wide knowledge, taste, and agency** — is his prescription for closing the gap without competing with the model:
- **Deep knowledge**: specialized expertise that lets you make accurate decisions quickly and prompt the model precisely.
- **Wide knowledge**: cross-domain literacy that lets you notice relevant patterns and ask the right questions.
- **Taste**: judgment that lets you select among AI-generated outputs based on domain or aesthetic standards.
- **Agency**: willingness to probe the boundaries of what is possible and discover new applications.

The essay is a canonical continuation of Mollick's 07-01-morning *"twilight of the chatbots"* chatbot-to-agent transition thesis, this time reframed around *individual-capability-development* rather than *product-category-transition*.
