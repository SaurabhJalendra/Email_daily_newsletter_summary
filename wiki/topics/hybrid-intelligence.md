## Summary

**Hybrid Intelligence (HI)** describes systems in which humans and AI operate as **interdependent partners** — combining human judgment, creativity, domain expertise, and values with machine speed, scale, and analytical capability — rather than treating AI as a replacement for human work. The headline claim, developed in 2026 experiments by [[vivienne-ming]], is that a small group of "cyborgs" who actively **challenge AI outputs, ask *why***, and **explore alternative explanations** outperform both the strongest standalone humans and the strongest standalone models. The strongest predictors of performance are human traits — **curiosity, intellectual humility, fluid intelligence, perspective-taking** — rather than access to the largest model.

The framing has moved from thesis to practical deployment: a 2026 EU project treats HI as *hybrid collective intelligence* (AI decision support plus multiple human experts); Microsoft has applied the term to Windows architectures that dynamically combine local and cloud AI for secure, efficient agentic computing; and 2026 research proposes evaluation models for human-AI teams and collaboration competencies. Industry applications span healthcare, finance, design, knowledge work, and decision support. HI's practitioner implication is that organizations must **redesign workflows, governance, accountability, and employee skills** so humans and AI complement one another reliably — the competitive advantage is less about model access and more about *organizational capability to combine AI with human curiosity + intellectual humility + perspective-taking*. Sits alongside [[ethan-mollick]]'s *co-intelligence* framing and pairs with [[continual-learning]] in treating AI value as a function of long-running human-AI interaction.

## Key Facts

- **Core claim**: cyborgs (humans who actively challenge AI output and probe for alternatives) outperform both strongest individual humans and strongest standalone models
- **Strongest predictors of team performance**: curiosity, intellectual humility, fluid intelligence, perspective-taking (human traits), not model size
- **Measurement unit**: outcomes of the human–machine pair (not model benchmarks)
- **Associated institutions / vendors**: [[insead-knowledge]] (Oct 2026 essay + AI Forum Americas), Possibility Sciences (Ming's organization), Microsoft (Windows hybrid local/cloud AI architecture), 2026 EU hybrid collective-intelligence project
- **Deployment areas**: healthcare, finance, design, knowledge work, decision support
- **Governance implications**: workflow redesign, human oversight, explainability, resistance to automation bias
