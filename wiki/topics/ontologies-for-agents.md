## Summary

"Ontologies for agents" refers to the mid-2026 revival of Semantic Web ontologies as **deterministic guardrails for probabilistic AI agents**. Ontologies define the types of entities and relationships in a domain of knowledge; when composed with LLM-driven agents, they provide *logical guardrails* that keep probabilistic outputs inside a bounded, type-consistent surface. Frank Coyle and Emil Eifrem canonicalize the framing in AI-engineering discourse; Neo4j uses ontologies inside its agentic products as a *"smarter shared substrate"* to run agents at scale; Kingsley Idehen at OpenLink Software combines ontologies with AI engineering into an *"agent engineering stack"* rooted in Semantic Web technologies.

The framing complements the mid-2026 [[graph-engineering]] discipline (Harnoor Singh 2026-07-24-evening seven-stage roadmap) — where graph-engineering names the *shape* of the agent-harness, ontologies name the *type-system* of the entities and relations the agent operates over.
