## Summary

AI-military coverage tracks how the US Department of War / Department of Defense / intelligence community adopts frontier-lab models — and where labs accept, refuse, or revise those deployments. The February–March 2026 cycle turned this into a multi-lab contest: the Pentagon issued an ultimatum demanding [[anthropic]] remove military safeguards from Claude, [[anthropic]] refused, and the contract pipeline shifted to [[xai]] (Grok) and then to [[openai]]. OpenAI then revised its own Pentagon deal after employee backlash, barring intelligence-agency domestic surveillance without formal contract modifications. The story frames AI safety guardrails as a material procurement constraint, not a brand-positioning exercise.
