## Summary

Simon Willison is a prominent independent developer, writer, and creator of the open-source `llm` command-line tool and Python library — a practical interface for working with multiple large language models across OpenAI + Anthropic + Google + open-weight vendors. His Substack newsletter and companion weblog (simonwillison.net) remain an influential accessible-but-technically-detailed digest of fast-moving AI developments, with recurring coverage of falling model prices, stronger coding agents, open-weight models running locally, prompt-injection and AI-security exploits, and experiments showing language models interpreting + modifying neural-network weights.
