## Summary

Jensen Huang is NVIDIA's co-founder and (until Nov 12 2026) longtime CEO — the person most responsible for building NVIDIA into the dominant supplier of AI accelerators and shepherding it through the generative-AI boom. His reported resignation on November 12 2026 followed reports of two extramarital affairs and an alleged connection between one of the individuals and Chinese intelligence; NVIDIA announced that CFO Colette Kress would serve as interim CEO while the board searches internally for a permanent successor.

Huang has been widely identified by scholar-tier commentators ([[nathan-lambert]] in Interconnects' *"Open-source AI without Jensen"* essay) as the canonical open-source-AI advocate at a chip-vendor tier — through NVIDIA's Nemotron releases, NVIDIA Cosmos, GR00T humanoid-robotics foundation models, the planned acquisition of [[hugging-face]], and his public opposition to restrictive open-weight licensing. The departure has therefore become a *canonical early-Nov-2026 concrete-first-moment-advocate-succession-concern substrate operator* on the open-source AI ecosystem arc as well as a *concrete-business-continuity-risk substrate operator* on NVIDIA's execution during the Vera Rubin ramp, Oracle Cloud expansion, and sovereign AI programs.

By November 16 2026, an independent online campaign called *"Stand with Jensen"* had passed 160,000 signatures — including Elon Musk and other technology executives, employees, and shareholders — urging the board to reinstate Huang while an independent investigation determines the facts. The petition argues that Huang was removed without meaningful due process and calls for greater disclosure. NVIDIA's board had not, as of the cited reports, announced any decision to reverse the resignation, so the campaign represents shareholder-and-industry pressure rather than a confirmed leadership change.
