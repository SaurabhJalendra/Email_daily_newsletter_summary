## Summary

Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) is an open standard announced January 2026, co-developed by Shopify and [[google]], that defines how AI agents discover, negotiate, and transact with merchants. It was announced as [[google]]'s answer to an AI-first commerce layer: Gemini and any UCP-compliant agent can find, pay, and deliver products on behalf of users. It sits alongside the broader agent-protocol wave (MCP, A2UI, Agent HQ) and is positioned to make agent-driven shopping interoperable across platforms.
