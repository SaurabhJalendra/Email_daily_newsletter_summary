## Summary

Paul Christiano is an AI-alignment researcher, president of the Alignment Research Center (ARC), and an OpenAI Foundation board member. He is a central figure in the AI-safety community whose research and public commentary focus on the control of highly capable, potentially self-improving AI systems — including early work on reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback, iterated amplification, and formal evaluation protocols for frontier models.

In mid-November 2026 he became the canonical near-term-takeover-timeline framer in the AI-safety discourse through a Dwarkesh Podcast interview and his 2026 Hinton Lectures: he put the probability at roughly 1-in-3 that superintelligence powerful enough to supersede human decision-making and overpower governments could emerge within a year if the current development trajectory continues without slowing. This is a conditional near-term forecast about trajectory, not a prediction that such a system will definitely be built. Christiano's argument shifts AI-safety concerns from long-term speculation toward near-term governance, evaluation, and control problems, and he has called on labs to pause further algorithmic progress until they can secure model weights and research secrets against theft.
