## Summary

The Fairwind Program is Google's gated-access channel for its cybersecurity-focused Gemini SKUs. Carbon-tier models are released initially to vetted cyber defenders through Fairwind so Google can tune guardrails against observed defender use before broader rollout — the same restricted-access pattern used by [[openai-daybreak]] (OpenAI's cyber-defender gating for [[gpt-5-6-cyber]]) and Anthropic's [[project-glasswing]] (which gates [[claude-mythos-5-1]]).

Fairwind surfaced in the wiki alongside the Oct 2026 public release of [[gemini-4-carbon]], the Gemini-4 family's cybersecurity + complex-software-engineering SKU; Google indicated a broader (non-Fairwind) release would follow once guardrails were tuned based on defender feedback. Fairwind also previously appeared in Argon-coverage as the gating substrate for Gemini-4 cyber-tier access.
