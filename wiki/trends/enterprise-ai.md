## Summary

October 2025 was a sustained enterprise-AI product cycle. [[google]] shipped [[gemini-enterprise]] (no-code workplace AI) and rolled real-time Gemini into Google Meet; [[salesforce]] launched [[agentforce]] 360; ChatGPT added Walmart checkout (consumer-commerce adjacency); Microsoft shipped a new image-gen tool. Late October pushed enterprise AI directly into core productivity apps and developer plumbing: [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] gave ChatGPT grounded answers across Slack/Drive/SharePoint/GitHub/Gmail; [[claude-for-excel]] put a Claude sidebar inside Excel with finance-specific Agent Skills; [[github-agent-hq]] turned GitHub into a multi-agent control plane; Microsoft's "Mico" personality and Copilot App Builder pushed Copilot from chat to platform. [[mistral-studio]] added a European production-AI alternative. The common signal: every major SaaS surface is gaining a native AI agent layer, and frontier labs are now shipping app-shaped products instead of just APIs.
