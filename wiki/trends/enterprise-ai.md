## Summary

October 2025 was a sustained enterprise-AI product cycle. [[google]] shipped [[gemini-enterprise]] (no-code workplace AI) and rolled real-time Gemini into Google Meet; [[salesforce]] launched [[agentforce]] 360; ChatGPT added Walmart checkout (consumer-commerce adjacency); Microsoft shipped a new image-gen tool. Slack expanded AI access and Opal (Google) went global. The common signal: every major SaaS surface is gaining a native AI agent layer.
