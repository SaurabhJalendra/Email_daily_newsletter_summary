## Summary

The **Genesis Mission** is a U.S. federal initiative to apply frontier AI to a set of named scientific-and-national-security verticals — **energy, scientific discovery, national security, fusion, and quantum computing research** — by partnering with commercial AI vendors to channel compute, credits, model access, and training support directly into federal science agencies. The initiative was first referenced on 10-10-morning as the recipient of NVIDIA's $1B five-year commitment and Anthropic's $150M pledge; by 10-10-evening the aggregate industry commitment had risen to approximately **$2.4 billion** across five named vendors: **[[openai]] + [[nvidia]] + [[anthropic]] + [[amazon]] + [[google]]**.

The scheme reads as *the AI-industry-federal-counterpart to [[biohub]]'s $1.8B DOE + NIH biological-dataset initiative*: both are late-2026 billion-dollar-tier commitments that bind frontier AI (compute, models, and credits) into federal R&D infrastructure. Analyst framing per researchFindings notes the commitments *"could materially expand government and laboratory access to frontier models and large-scale compute [and] signal deeper integration between commercial AI providers and national research infrastructure"*, making the Genesis Mission a canonical anchor for the mid-to-late-2026 *AI-vendor-plus-federal-R&D-cohabitation* substrate.
