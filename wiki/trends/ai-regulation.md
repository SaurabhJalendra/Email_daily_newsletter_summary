## Summary

External pressure on AI labs has widened along multiple independent axes. In mid-October 2025 the Japanese government formally asked [[openai]] to stop generating manga/anime lookalikes via [[sora-2]], and the AFL-CIO launched a "Workers First Initiative on AI." By April 2026 a US federal court added a new vector in *United States v. Heppner*: conversations with AI chatbots like Claude are not protected by attorney-client privilege, because the chatbot is not a lawyer and providers' privacy policies allow collection of inputs and outputs. That ruling significantly narrows the legal-privilege expectation around AI chats and has immediate implications for how lawyers, journalists, and regulated professionals use [[anthropic]]'s and similar assistants.
