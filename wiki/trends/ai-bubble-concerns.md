## Summary

In October 2025 multiple institutional voices — the Bank of England, the IMF, and JPMorgan — publicly flagged AI investment levels as comparable to dotcom-era froth. The warnings arrived the same week as [[openai]]'s reported $500B valuation and [[reflection]]'s $2B round, making the tension between institutional risk warnings and private-market enthusiasm explicit.
