# AI Bubble Concerns

> **Type**: trend

> **Last updated**: 2025-10-20
> **Status**: stale
> **Related**: [[ai-funding-rounds]], [[openai]]

## Summary

In October 2025 multiple institutional voices — the Bank of England, the IMF, and JPMorgan — publicly flagged AI investment levels as comparable to dotcom-era froth. The warnings arrived the same week as [[openai]]'s reported $500B valuation and [[reflection]]'s $2B round, making the tension between institutional risk warnings and private-market enthusiasm explicit. The "bubble about to burst?" framing persisted in mid-October newsletters, with renewed commentary through 2025-10-20 ("AI's big problem") alongside narratives about capital rotating into "hard assets" / data infrastructure.
