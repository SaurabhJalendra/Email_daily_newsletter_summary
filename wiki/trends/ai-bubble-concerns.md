## Summary

In October 2025 multiple institutional voices — the Bank of England, the IMF, and JPMorgan — publicly flagged AI investment levels as comparable to dotcom-era froth. The warnings arrived the same week as [[openai]]'s reported $500B valuation and [[reflection]]'s $2B round, making the tension between institutional risk warnings and private-market enthusiasm explicit. The "bubble about to burst?" framing persisted into late October and early November 2025: [[openai]] explored a ~$1T IPO, [[nvidia]] crossed $5T, "Vertical AI Eats SaaS" emerged as the dominant counter-narrative, and "Massive AI Deals Continue To Offset Bubble Concerns" became a recurring Tech Buzz framing through 2025-11-04. Even as voices warned of overheating, the deal flow only accelerated.
