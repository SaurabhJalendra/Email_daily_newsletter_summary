## Summary

"AI slop" describes the late-2025 / early-2026 flood of low-signal, high-volume AI-generated content (videos, images, posts) on mainstream platforms. Coverage in this window centers on two concrete measurements: YouTube's algorithm is serving over 20% of videos classified as "AI slop" to new users (with top AI-slop channels earning millions), and Instagram head Adam Mosseri publicly acknowledges that polished posts are no longer proof of authenticity — the platform "must evolve fast." Together these signals reframe moderation, creator monetization, and discovery algorithms as the new battlefield, even as generative-video/image quality (Sora, Veo, Nano Banana Pro, Luma) keeps improving.
