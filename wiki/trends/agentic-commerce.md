## Summary

Agentic Commerce is the late-2026 paradigm shift in which **AI agents act on behalf of customers to find and purchase products**, potentially bypassing the traditional website-visit funnel. The canonical mid-2026 productization substrate pairs **Shopify Agentic Storefronts** (store-product integration into AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot), the **[[universal-commerce-protocol]]** (Shopify + Google co-developed "shared language that enables any agent to buy from any store"), and **[[claude-commerce-agents]]** (Anthropic's Apache-2.0 shopping-agent + merchant-agent suite) with **Adobe-in-ChatGPT** (Photoshop + Acrobat hands-on controls inside ChatGPT), **DoorDash's text-to-order AI agent** (consumer-tier) + **DoorDash connector for teams** (B2B lunch/snack-run tier), and the broader three-frontier-lab persistent-agent cluster ([[dots]] + [[muse-for-small-business]] + [[grok-team-bots]]).

The canonical late-Sep-to-Oct-2026 adoption-metric anchor is **AI traffic to US retail sites +393% in Q1 2026** (The AI Leverage HIGH 2026-10-01-evening), with the paradigm-shift framing *"having a website will become optional"* as retailers rebuild their presence through agent-readable catalogs and protocol-mediated transactions. This completes the arc that began with Jan-2026 UCP + Walmart-Google Gemini partnership and the Sep-2026 Shopify self-improving-AI-pipeline canonical case-study.
