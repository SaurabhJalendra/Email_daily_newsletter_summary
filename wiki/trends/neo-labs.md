## Summary

"Neo Labs" is AI Supremacy's canonical-naming for the cohort of AI research startups being founded in 2025-26 by elite researchers spinning out of [[anthropic]], [[openai]], Google DeepMind, and Google Brain — each chasing a narrow, ambitious technical thesis rather than a general-purpose frontier-model platform. The cohort sits structurally between two existing wiki categories: the larger headline frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Meta, Mistral) and the earlier 2025 spinout cohort (Mira Murati's [[thinking-machines]], Yann LeCun's [[ami-labs]], Fei-Fei Li's [[world-labs]], Ilya Sutskever's SSI).

Initial Neo Labs roster surfaced in this cycle: [[mirendil-ai]] ("self-accelerating" AI that does the work of an AI researcher), [[engram]] (persistent learned memory layer for AI to reduce cost + improve efficiency), [[general-intuition-ai]] (Large Action Models + world models that perceive/predict/act across virtual and physical spaces), [[project-prometheus]] (Bezos + Vik Bajaj — AI for physical tasks and re-architecting how physical things are made), [[trajectory]] (continual-learning platform letting AI products learn from real-world user interactions). The common pattern is a *single-thesis* focus (self-improvement, memory, action, physical, continual learning) staffed by named senior researchers with frontier-lab pedigrees — and capitalized in the $50M-$1B+ range that signals 2026's AI venture cycle is willing to fund focused research bets, not just product-tier startups.
