## Summary

By August 2026, opposition to new AI data centers has hardened into a **bipartisan US political issue**. Anna's Daybreak News reports that state governors are restricting new data-center projects over concerns about **power draw, water use, and community impact**, with more than **60% of Americans polled opposing nearby data centers**. Forward Future frames it as a campaign issue on both sides, and the **Ohio Senate race** has made data-center siting a centerpiece — while Pennsylvania has separately passed a law making local approval legally binding for AI data centers and banning related NDAs (per researchFindings.missingStories).

The trend runs directly counter to the mid-2026 compute-CapEx surge — [[nvidia]]'s $500B Wall Street financing consortium, the $105B OpenAI Ohio credit facility, and the general shift toward multi-gigawatt AI campuses — and reads as the first sustained cross-partisan political-cost surface on the AI-infrastructure buildout.
