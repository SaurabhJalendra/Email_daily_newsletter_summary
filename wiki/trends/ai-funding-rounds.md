## Summary

Early October 2025 delivered several landmark AI financing events: [[openai]] reached a reported $500B valuation, Reflection raised $2B, and Periodic Labs appeared in funding coverage. Institutional watchdogs (BoE, IMF, JPM) publicly flagged [[ai-bubble-concerns]]. By April 2026 [[anthropic]] was reportedly in investor talks at an $800B-plus valuation, approaching OpenAI levels, and pharma–AI crossover deals started showing up at multi-billion-dollar scale: Eli Lilly agreed to pay up to $2.75B to Hong Kong-based Insilico Medicine for AI drug-discovery work.
