## Summary

The AI Pace Letter is a July 2026 open letter, cosigned by over 1,000 employees from [[openai]], [[anthropic]], Google DeepMind (via [[google]]), [[meta]], and Thinky, that urges the US government to support international efforts developing **"technical and governance tools to deliberately pace"** the development of automated AI research. The letter frames the ask as a cooperation problem — individual labs face intense competitive pressure not to unilaterally slow down, so a coordinated multi-lab (and multi-nation) mechanism is required to address emerging risks, develop security measures, and strengthen oversight.

Structurally, the letter is the first mid-2026 signal in this wiki where a *staff-tier pause request cosigned across multiple frontier labs* is publicly amplified by an *official corporate account* (@OpenAI). It extends the arc that began with [[anthropic]]'s 2026-06-05 Sparks of RSI essay (corporate-authored) + 2026-06-08 Dario Amodei coordinated-pause ask + 2026-07-17 first-experimental-evidence-of-RSI-via-autoresearch-agent milestone into a *cross-lab staff-cohort collective-action* tier. It lands the same cycle as the [[hugging-face]] machine-speed autonomous cyberattack disclosure and the [[claude-mythos]] Preview AES-crack demonstration — pairing *safety-concern amplification* with *capability-demonstration* in the same news cycle.
