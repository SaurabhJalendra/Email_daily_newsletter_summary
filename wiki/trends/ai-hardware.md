## Summary

Early October 2025 showed hardware moving from background to foreground for AI. On silicon: [[meta]] acquired Rivos, [[openai]] announced an [[amd]] partnership, and [[nvidia]] struck a deal with [[xai]] — three signals that frontier labs are racing to diversify and lock in accelerator supply. On form factors: [[meta]] held its AI-glasses lead, [[apple]] pivoted from VR toward AI glasses, and [[figure]] revealed its Figure 03 humanoid. Jony Ive's secretive AI hardware project continued to feed speculation.
