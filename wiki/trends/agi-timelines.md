## Summary

AGI Timelines is the recurring pattern of frontier-lab CEOs publicly canonicalizing AGI-arrival-year predictions as strategic anchors for the industry, regulators, and workforces. As of mid-2026 the three most-cited predictions are Demis Hassabis (DeepMind) at *2-5 years*, Dario Amodei (Anthropic) with earlier 2027-27 framings, and Sam Altman (OpenAI). Hassabis's July 2026 The AI Corner deep-dive is the most-detailed recent framing: AGI arrives in 2-5 years but bio + nuclear risks arrive first, an international body should test frontier systems before release, and text-only models miss the ceiling because true AGI requires understanding the physical world.

Hassabis's 10-point framing productizes several sub-theses: (1) *bench depth* is the key competitive moat and investors misprice it by focusing only on compute + capital; (2) AI is exiting the "slot machine era" toward controllable + iteratively-directed outputs; (3) multimodal + learned-simulator + imagination-driven systems are prerequisites for physical-world understanding; (4) games remain a canonical ladder to scientific progress; (5) [[synthid]]-style mandatory provenance detection becomes a necessary standard as generative-media output volumes climb.
