## Summary

The EU Kids Act is a proposed European Commission law, surfaced 18 Sep 2026, that would ban social-media accounts for children under 13 across the EU and impose strict design rules on platforms. Design mandates include turning off infinite scroll, stopping push notifications at night, blocking unsolicited messages from strangers, reining in recommendation algorithms, and — of direct AI/ML relevance — **turning off AI chatbots by default for minors**. Platforms would also have to estimate the age of existing users from "reasonable proxies" and verify age at signup.

Enforcement: fines up to **6% of a company's global annual revenue** for non-compliance. The proposal sits alongside California SB-243 companion-AI-chatbot regulation and the multi-cycle [[chatgpt-for-teens]] youth-safety substrate as *canonical mid-Sep-2026 concrete youth-AI-safety regulatory-tier substrate cluster*.
