## 📌 If You Only Read One Thing

The single most important story of the week is [[anthropic]]'s **Claude Sonnet 5 launch and simultaneous Fable 5 restoration** — the pair that reveals Anthropic's post-export-control product architecture in its fully articulated form. Sonnet 5 shipped on 2026-07-01 as the *"most agentic Sonnet yet"*, extending the Claude versioned-tier ladder into its fifth Sonnet generation (Haiku 4.5 → Sonnet 4.6 → **Sonnet 5** → Opus 4.7/4.8 → Fable 5 → Mythos). It became the default model on Free, Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise, and API tiers, benchmarked on CursorBench + FrontierCode Extended + Artificial Analysis at *near-Opus performance at 40% of Opus cost* (AlphaSignal's canonical framing) with a 1M-token context window, 128K-token output limit, promotional $2/$10 per million tokens through August 31, and — most tellingly — *"deliberately weak offensive cybersecurity capabilities"*. It was adopted at launch by [[cursor]], [[cognition]], Cline, FactoryAI, [[perplexity]], and VS Code as *"default workhorse model for agentic software stacks"* (AINews framing). Anthropic also introduced a new tokenizer, five distinct reasoning effort settings, real-time cyber safeguards as an inference-time capability, and (per tokens& HIGH) lower cybersecurity benchmarks than *the previous Sonnet version* — a first publicly framed intentional offensive-cyber regression from Sonnet 4.6 to Sonnet 5 that no other frontier lab has publicly announced.

The Sonnet-5 launch pairs cycle-structurally with the **Fable 5 restoration** — 19 days after the 2026-06-15 White House pull, Fable 5 returned globally with a three-safety-changes triad (a new safety classifier, a *temporary* Opus 4.8 fallback for benign-request over-flagging, and a cross-industry *severity* jailbreak-risk framework co-developed with AWS, Microsoft, and Google). Fable 5's Remote Labor Index score of **16.1% on professional remote-work tasks — 2× its nearest rival's capability doubling** — landed as the first concrete percentage anchor on the human-graded freelance-task benchmark, sharpening the 2026-07-02-evening *"highest-score-ever"* framing into a measured 2×-lead over its closest competitor. Together, Sonnet 5 and Fable 5 make explicit the strategic architecture Anthropic is now defending publicly: **Sonnet 5 as the canonical near-frontier / government / regulated-enterprise Claude, deliberately safety-degraded on offensive cyber; Fable 5 and Mythos as the high-capability under-strong-safeguards frontier operating under a US-government-monitored release cadence**. California signed the first US-state government sole-source Claude deal at half price the same week — a structurally novel deployment posture that only makes sense given the safety-tier substitution Anthropic has built. This affects every downstream stakeholder in AI: it defines what regulated-industry-buyable AI looks like in H2 2026, sets the template other frontier labs (OpenAI Sol/Terra/Luna Pro leaked lineup, Google Gemini 3.5 Flash) are likely to imitate, and locks in the tiered-safety-capability-substitution regime as the new post-export-control normal.
