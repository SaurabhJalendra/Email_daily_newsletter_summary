## 📌 If You Only Read One Thing

This was the week that the *agent harness* graduated from developer discipline to first-class competitive substrate — and it happened along three simultaneous axes. On Sunday morning, NVIDIA disclosed that its internal general-purpose coding-agent architecture **AVO (Agentic Variation Operators)**, powered by [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-opus-5]], had achieved a **perfect 100.00 on ARC-AGI-3** across all 183 levels of 25 environments, using ~12% fewer actions than the previous leader. The scaffold-vs-base decomposition is the single most vivid data point yet on this axis: Claude Opus 5 alone scores around 30% on ARC-AGI-3, while AVO+Opus5 reaches 100%. On Saturday evening, [[anthropic]]'s Latent Space essay "The Evolution of the Agent Harness" canonicalized a three-era framework (Bolt-On 1.0 → Co-Training 2.0 → Attention 3.0) — spawning the new [[agent-harness]] topic page and connecting the discipline explicitly to [[recursive-self-improvement]]. And on Friday morning, [[trueforge]] (from TrueFoundry, MIT-licensed) and Cursor's cloud-agent upgrade both shipped, joined the same week by [[chroma-foundation]] (a cross-tool shared-memory substrate that integrates at launch with Codex + [[claude-code]] + [[cursor]] + [[slack]]).

Why it matters: the *harness = everything besides the model weights* framing is now backed by benchmark numbers large enough (30% → 100% on ARC-AGI-3) that vendors, VCs, and regulators cannot ignore the second-order competitive layer any longer. Anthropic named Claude as the leading Harness 2.0 exemplar; AINews declared "agent harnesses are becoming a key competitive layer"; TrueForge, DeepSeek Harness, Xiaomi HarnessX, Vercel Eve, and Flue now form a visible open-source cohort competing head-on with Anthropic's proprietary [[claude-managed-agents]]. Watch for **(a)** every remaining frontier lab to publish its own harness thesis in the next 60 days, **(b)** valuations on pure-harness vendors ([[trueforge]], [[h-company]], DeepSeek Harness) to compress or explode depending on integration with the top three base-model tiers, and **(c)** the mid-2026 [[graph-engineering]] and [[loop-engineering]] discourse to migrate wholesale onto [[agent-harness]] as the umbrella concept.
