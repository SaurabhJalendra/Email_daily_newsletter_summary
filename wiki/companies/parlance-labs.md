## Summary

**Parlance Labs** is a small engineer-led AI consultancy and education firm founded by [[hamel-husain]] (ex-GitHub Copilot-adjacent ML, Airbnb, fast.ai nbdev) that helps teams move LLM applications from prototypes to reliable production systems. Its stated approach is implementation-focused rather than strategy-deck-focused, concentrating on evaluation, observability, debugging, retrieval, agents, and data-driven improvement.

The firm's public-education output anchors it as one of the canonical mid-2026 reference-operators for the *evals-as-core-engineering-discipline* movement: it maintains the free open-source [[evals-skills]] bundle (with Shreya Shankar); teaches the paired Maven course *AI Evals for Engineers & PMs* (5,000+ engineers/PMs taught across cohorts); publishes Hamel's hamel.dev evals-FAQ (five-stage stage-based taxonomy); and ships standalone free sessions like the Oct 6 2026 *Cracking the AI Evals Interview* lesson that address the growing use of evals as a *"weed-out"* filter in AI hiring interviews.
