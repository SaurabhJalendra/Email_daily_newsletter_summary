## Summary

Fauna Robotics is a home-robotics startup acquired by [[amazon]] in Nov 2026 as part of Amazon's renewed effort to build a home robot capable of autonomously navigating dynamic home environments. The deal is reported alongside Amazon's assembly of a new in-house team, positioned as the company's second attempt at a mainstream home robot after Astro — which CNBC frames as having "fans but [looking] destined for the 'trash heap.'"
