## Summary

Stacklok is a startup founded by Kubernetes co-creators Craig McLuckie and Joe Beda, originally focused on software supply-chain security and now pivoting to build cloud-native infrastructure for AI agents. Its two main products are **Mecatl**, a cloud-native agent harness aimed at bringing agent runtimes out of single-user desktops and into centrally operated enterprise infrastructure, and **ToolHive**, an open-source platform for running and governing [[model-context-protocol]] servers. On top of these it offers a commercial **AI Gateway** that layers identity, authorization, policy, and auditing across the open-source components (with plans to open-source the gateway as well). The pitch targets large enterprises that want to lessen their dependency on frontier labs and hyperscalers by centrally managing agents with access control, budgets, provider routing, and reporting. Stacklok raised a $17.5M Series A.
