## Summary

Harmonic is an AI startup co-founded by Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev, focused on AI's mathematical and reasoning capabilities. It surfaced in late November 2025 with a $120M raise valuing the company at $1.45B, and made global news days later when its [[harmonic-aristotle]] system solved a version of Erdős Problem #124 in six hours — a result formally verified in Lean within a minute, framed by the company as a step toward "mathematical superintelligence."
