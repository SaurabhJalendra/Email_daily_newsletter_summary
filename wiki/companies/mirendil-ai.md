## Summary

Mirendil AI is a new frontier-lab spinout founded by former employees of [[anthropic]], [[openai]], [[google]], and [[xai]], announced on 2026-06-25 with a **$200M seed round** — among the largest AI seed rounds on record. The lab's stated focus is **AI that runs its own R&D for self-improving science research** — placing it in the same emerging cohort as [[ineffable-intelligence]] (David Silver's "superlearners" lab, $1.1B seed Apr 2026) and [[recursive-superintelligence]] (Tim Rocktäschel's ex-DeepMind RSI lab, $650M @ $4.65B May 2026). The cross-cohort founding-team composition (four frontier labs simultaneously contributing alumni) is structurally distinct from prior frontier-lab spinouts that typically draw from one or two source labs.

The Rundown AI cycle headline on 2026-06-25-evening frames Mirendil within the same cycle as OpenAI's [[openai-jalapeno]] custom chip unveil, the Anthropic [[claude-tag]] cycle-3 expansion, and the [[waymo]] $16B raise — placing Mirendil as part of the cycle's *new-frontier-lab + self-improving-science* axis alongside the broader [[recursive-self-improvement]] arc.
