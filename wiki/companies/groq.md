## Summary

Groq is the US AI-inference chip startup behind the LPU (Language Processing Unit) — a tensor-streaming architecture that aims at low-latency, high-throughput token generation rather than training. In late December 2025 [[nvidia]] announced its acquisition of Groq for $20B in cash — the largest acquisition in NVIDIA's history — giving NVIDIA explicit control of the inference layer alongside its existing training-GPU dominance and folding Groq's chips into NVIDIA's "AI factory" architecture.
