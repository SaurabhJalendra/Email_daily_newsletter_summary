## Summary

Groq is the US AI-inference chip startup behind the LPU (Language Processing Unit) — a tensor-streaming architecture that aims at low-latency, high-throughput token generation rather than training. Groq's late-December-2025 $20B "acquisition" by [[nvidia]] is now retrospectively framed by [[jonathan-ross]] (The AI Corner, July 14 2026) as a **non-exclusive technology-licensing deal plus a major transfer of Groq talent — including Ross himself — into NVIDIA** ("an acqui-hire in all but name"). NVIDIA is integrating Groq's inference technology into the **Vera Rubin GPU generation via the LPX platform** (shipping summer 2026), with the stated ambition that roughly a quarter of GPU workloads will eventually be paired with Groq-style inference chips. GroqCloud continues to operate independently, but the founding CEO is now inside NVIDIA.
