## Summary

OpenAI is the San Francisco AI lab behind ChatGPT, the GPT model series, and a fast-expanding product surface (Sora for video, [[agentkit]] for agents, [[openai-codex]] now reframed as a ChatGPT+Atlas+Codex superapp). Through October 2025 it became the most valuable private company at a reported $500B valuation, hosted DevDay, and locked in multi-silicon compute via [[amd]] and [[broadcom]]. The late-October to early-November 2025 window saw a step-change: it shipped the [[chatgpt-atlas]] browser, [[chatgpt-company-knowledge]] for workplace RAG, the [[aardvark]] GPT-5 security agent, and Sora 2 cameos / monetization; closed a $22.5B SoftBank tranche, completed its for-profit (PBC) recapitalization with renewed Microsoft terms (~27% stake), and reportedly began exploring a $1T IPO; signed a $38B multi-year compute deal with [[amazon]] AWS; and laid out an explicit AGI roadmap. By April 2026 the Codex surface had absorbed background computer use, parallel agents, an in-app browser, and image generation, and OpenAI shipped a biology-tuned specialty model, GPT-Rosalind, aimed at drug discovery. Its Trusted Access program continued to expand, and the Agents SDK picked up a native harness and sandboxed execution.
