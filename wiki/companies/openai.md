## Summary

OpenAI is the San Francisco AI lab behind ChatGPT, GPT-series models, and a fast-expanding product surface (Sora for video, AgentKit for agents, a rapidly scaling ChatGPT app ecosystem). In early October 2025 it became the most valuable private company in the world at a reported $500B valuation, shipped Sora 2 with major physics improvements, hosted a DevDay focused on agent tooling (AgentKit), and struck a strategic chip partnership with AMD. Its founders alumni continue to shape the field — ex-CTO Mira Murati's [[thinking-machines]] launched its first product (Tinker) during this period.
