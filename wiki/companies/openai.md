## Summary

OpenAI is the San Francisco AI lab behind ChatGPT, the GPT model series, and a fast-expanding product surface (Sora for video, [[agentkit]] for agents, [[openai-codex]] now reframed as a ChatGPT+Atlas+Codex superapp). Through October 2025 it became the most valuable private company at a reported $500B valuation, hosted DevDay, and locked in multi-silicon compute via [[amd]] and [[broadcom]]. By April 2026 the Codex surface had absorbed background computer use, parallel agents, an in-app browser, and image generation, and OpenAI shipped a biology-tuned specialty model, GPT-Rosalind, aimed at drug discovery. Its Trusted Access program continued to expand, and the Agents SDK picked up a native harness and sandboxed execution.
