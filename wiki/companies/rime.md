## Summary

Rime (Rime Labs) is an enterprise voice-AI company whose current flagship is [[rime-prism]], a fine-tunable speech-to-speech model launched Oct 28 2026. Prism combines low-latency audio interaction (~300ms response time) with an inspectable text layer, parallel tool calls, turn detection, and enterprise controls for vocabulary, pronunciation, safety, deployment, and data residency. Rime is explicitly designed to let companies run voice systems within their own infrastructure, positioning it against pure-API voice vendors.

Rime's research program is increasingly focused on **production benchmarks** — interruption handling, pronunciation, customer satisfaction, and real-world call outcomes — rather than speech quality alone. Its models are also available through developer infrastructure such as [[livekit]] Agents and [[together-ai]], indicating broader integration into voice-agent stacks. (Note: "RIME" also refers to unrelated research concepts — Rime Optimization, a physics-inspired metaheuristic for feature selection, and Rule-based Instructions for Music Editing; the Nov 2026 newsletter context points to the voice-AI company.)
