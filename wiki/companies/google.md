## Summary

Google (with Google DeepMind) is OpenAI's strongest large-model competitor, anchored by the Gemini family and a growing agent/developer stack: [[jules]] for coding agents, Opal for app building, and [[gemini-enterprise]] for workplace AI. In early October 2025 it shipped [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]] (UI control), rolled out [[gemini-enterprise]] for no-code workplace AI, opened up [[jules]] with command-line tools and a Jules API, and revamped the Gemini consumer app. Security researchers also disclosed prompt-injection vulnerabilities across three Gemini models.
