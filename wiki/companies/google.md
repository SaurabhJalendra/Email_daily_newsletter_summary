## Summary

Google (with Google DeepMind) is OpenAI's strongest large-model competitor, anchored by the Gemini family and a growing agent/developer stack: [[jules]] for coding agents, Opal for app building, [[gemini-enterprise]] for workplace AI, and [[veo-3]] for video. Through October 2025 it shipped [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]], launched [[gemini-enterprise]] and [[veo-3]] 3.1, rolled real-time Gemini into Google Meet, added Maps data to the Gemini API, and confirmed [[gemini-3]] after a pre-launch leak. April 2026 pushed Gemini onto the desktop: [[gemini-3-1-flash-tts]] for a full audio stack, a native Gemini Mac app with screen-sharing and floating chat, and a Chrome "Skills" feature that lets users save and reuse Gemini prompts across pages.
