## Summary

Google (with Google DeepMind) is OpenAI's strongest large-model competitor, anchored by the Gemini family and a growing agent/developer stack: [[jules]] for coding agents, Opal for app building, and [[gemini-enterprise]] for workplace AI. Through October 2025 it shipped [[gemini-2-5-computer-use]] (UI control), launched [[gemini-enterprise]] and [[veo-3]] 3.1, rolled real-time Gemini capabilities into Google Meet, added Google Maps data to the Gemini API, and confirmed [[gemini-3]] after a pre-launch leak. Security researchers also disclosed prompt-injection vulnerabilities across three Gemini models.
