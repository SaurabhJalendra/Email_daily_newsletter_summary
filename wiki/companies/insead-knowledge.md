## Summary

**INSEAD Knowledge** is INSEAD's research + thought-leadership publication covering AI/ML, strategy, leadership, and business. In Sep–Oct 2026 INSEAD announced commercially available **Immersive AI Cases** and more than **40 AI-powered learning experiences**, framing the next competitive advantage as *organizational ability to combine AI with human curiosity, intellectual humility, perspective-taking, and sound decision-making* rather than access to any particular model.

The Oct 26 2026 flagship essay **"What 'Mad Scientist' Vivienne Ming Taught Us About Hybrid Intelligence"** canonicalizes the [[hybrid-intelligence]] framing on INSEAD's institutional surface and is paired with INSEAD AI Forum Americas coverage of [[vivienne-ming]]'s claim that *AI should challenge people to think better rather than merely provide answers*. Related essays include *"Why We Underestimate Boring Conversations"* (Nadav Klein) and *"A Manager's Guide To Thinking Philosophically"* (José Luis Alvarez).
