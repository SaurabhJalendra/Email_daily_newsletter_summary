## Summary

Speechmatics is a UK-based speech-technology vendor that ships enterprise-grade speech-to-text (STT) infrastructure. In September 2026 it launched [[linden-stt]] ("Linden"), a new STT model purpose-built for voice agents — not general-purpose transcription. Linden targets high accuracy in noisy environments, tolerates strong accents, supports 1,000+ custom words per session (useful for industry jargon), and runs across 55+ languages at 350ms latency. Priced at $0.30/hour ($0.15/hour after discounts), it positions Speechmatics as a canonical voice-agent-tier STT substrate competing with AssemblyAI, ElevenLabs, and OpenAI's Realtime speech APIs.
