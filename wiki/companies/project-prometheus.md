## Summary

Project Prometheus is Jeff Bezos's frontier-AI venture, framed across newsletters as an **"artificial general engineer" (AGE)** that automates **CAD + physics-based simulation for physical-object design** — positioning Bezos as a peer-funder to the major-lab autoresearch agenda but with an explicit physical-engineering vertical rather than the digital-cognitive vertical the major frontier labs target. First surfaced in this wiki on the [[openai]] timeline at 2026-05-26 (AI Breakfast cycle that named Project Prometheus alongside OpenAI's Codex-traffic and Gartner Magic Quadrant signals); resurfaced 2026-06-12-evening with a concrete **$41B framing** across two independent newsletters (The Rundown AI cycle-headline + TLDR cycle-headline), establishing it as the most heavily-capitalized AI-engineering venture publicly named outside the existing major-lab cohort.

The "artificial general engineer" framing is a deliberate parallel to "artificial general intelligence" (AGI) — Bezos is positioning Prometheus as the *physical-engineering* analogue to the AGI framing pursued by [[openai]] / [[anthropic]] / [[google]], with the explicit thesis that the next general-purpose AI capability after digital-cognitive reasoning is automating the design of physical objects (CAD models that can be simulated for physical correctness and manufactured). This puts Prometheus adjacent to but distinct from [[humanoid-robotics]] (which automates physical *agents*, not physical *object design*) and from [[ineffable-intelligence]] (which automates *learning* via "superlearners", not engineering).
