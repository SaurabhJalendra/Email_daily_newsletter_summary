## Summary

**Biohub** (Chan Zuckerberg Biohub Network) is a nonprofit biomedical research network co-founded by Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan, with hubs in San Francisco, Chicago, and New York. In Oct 2026 Biohub announced a $1.8B co-investment with the US Department of Energy (DOE) and the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to build large-scale biological datasets for training AI models — explicitly aimed at AI-driven discovery of new ways to prevent and treat diseases.

The announcement positions Biohub as the *dataset-provider side* of the emerging AI-for-biology stack, complementing the *compute/model-provider side* ([[anthropic]] Claude Science + wet lab + [[john-jumper]] Life Sciences org, [[google-deepmind]] AlphaFold lineage) and the *application side* (Endura Therapeutics using Claude for CRISPR-based medicine, Novo Nordisk–Anthropic drug-discovery partnership). It extends the [[ai-biosecurity]] canonical arc with a federal-funded biological-dataset-provider substrate alongside [[ai-healthcare]].
