## Summary

Inherent is an AI research-automation startup founded by [[google-deepmind]] alumni, first surfaced in the Aug 25 2026 morning cycle via TechCrunch reporting on its debut model **Faraday** (see [[faraday-27b]]). Faraday is a **27-billion-parameter** model positioned as an *"AI teammate"* for scientific research, and Inherent claims it **outperformed larger frontier models from [[anthropic]] and [[openai]] at replicating scientific research papers** — a benchmark that measures end-to-end reproduction of experimental setups, methods, and results from published papers.
