## Summary

Fireworks is an NVIDIA-backed AI inference-and-fine-tuning startup that reached a **$17.5B valuation** in mid-July 2026, driven by enterprise demand for cheaper AI-model access as companies increasingly deploy open-source frontier models ([[kimi-k3]], [[inkling]], [[glm-5-2]], [[longcat-2]]) rather than relying exclusively on OpenAI + Anthropic + Google closed-model APIs. Fireworks sits in the *NVIDIA-adjacent inference-hosting cohort* alongside [[baseten]] ($13B, June 2026), [[together-ai]] ($8.3B, July 2026), [[friendliai]], and [[sambanova]] ($11B, July 2026) — of which Fireworks currently tops the valuation range at $17.5B.

The company's positioning aligns with the broader [[open-source-models]] thesis of *"frontier parity at a fraction of the cost"* — as open-weight frontier models (Kimi K3 at $3/M input + $15/M output pricing, Inkling under Apache 2.0) close the capability gap with closed models, inference-hosting providers like Fireworks capture the demand for *cheap-third-party-hosting of open-weight frontier models* on NVIDIA infrastructure.
