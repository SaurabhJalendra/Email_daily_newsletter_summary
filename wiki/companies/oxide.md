## Summary

Oxide Computer Company builds the **Oxide Cloud Computer**, a rack-scale system that pairs purpose-built hardware with open-source software for compute, storage, networking, and cloud management — delivered inside a customer's own data center or colocation facility. The product targets organizations that need cloud-style APIs, self-service provisioning, and integrated infrastructure but want control over data residency, latency, security, or deployment location. Oxide positions itself for agentic AI, high-performance computing, data engineering, classical ML, retrieval, and selected CPU-based inference workloads rather than as a direct competitor to specialized GPU-cloud providers — the thesis is that running AI *agents* (plus the CPU-intensive work of executing tasks after model generation) will drive demand for enterprise-owned, full-stack cloud infrastructure.

Oxide announced a **$445M Series D on October 9, 2026**, led by Eclipse, following a $200M Series C in February 2026. The company reports reaching profitability earlier in 2026 and expanding manufacturing capacity ~20× year-over-year, with demand continuing to exceed supply. A September 2026 partnership with **Carahsoft** targets the public sector, including government and classified-data workloads.
