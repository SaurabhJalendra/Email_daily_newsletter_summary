## Summary

Neuralink is Elon Musk's brain-computer-interface (BCI) company, developing implantable neural devices that decode brain activity into actions on computers and prosthetics. The company enters this wiki (Oct 2026) via a disclosed pretraining dataset of 50,000+ hours of unlabeled, freeform neural data accumulated since the start of its clinical trials — used to pretrain decoders aimed at being "more accurate and long-lived." The move extends the LLM-era pretraining paradigm into neural-signal substrate, and reads structurally alongside foundation-model generalizations into [[world-models]] and [[tabular-foundation-models]] as another modality-tier where large unlabeled corpora + pretraining is becoming the dominant method.
