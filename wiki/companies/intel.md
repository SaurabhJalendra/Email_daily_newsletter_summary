## Summary

Intel is the US semiconductor incumbent, now explicitly positioning its roadmap against the **demand shock from AI compute** (growing ~3× per year for the last decade). Intel's technology stack targets higher transistor density and performance via **gate-all-around transistors**, **backside power**, **CFET (complementary field-effect transistor)**, new interconnect materials such as **ruthenium** (potentially replacing copper), and **hybrid bonding** for chip-to-chip connections. CTO Pushkar Ranade's canonical framing (Dwarkesh Patel interview, Oct 2026) is that **the global stock of chipmaking tools limits how much the industry can double leading-edge wafer production** — a tool-availability constraint that colors the entire AI-era semiconductor roadmap.

Named partnerships include Elon Musk's **[[terafab]]** (joint fab-tech acceleration — part of the broader [[tesla]] + [[spacex]] five-tier vertical AI-stack framing). Intel's 14A process node and ASML's EUV production (<100 EUV machines/year industry-wide) sit at the center of the "how do we scale leading-edge wafer production" question.
