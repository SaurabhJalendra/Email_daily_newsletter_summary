## Summary

TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company) is the world's leading contract chipmaker and a strategic chokepoint in the AI hardware supply chain. It fabricates the leading-edge logic for [[nvidia]] GPUs, [[amd]] accelerators, [[broadcom]] custom ASICs, Apple silicon, and the custom chips of frontier AI labs. As AI-driven demand has surged — TSMC reported a 59.7% year-over-year revenue rise in October 2026 — the company has become the structural constraint on frontier-AI-capacity growth: advanced packaging (CoWoS), HBM memory integration, and leading-edge node throughput now gate frontier training and inference deployment more than model design.

On November 10 2026, TSMC's board approved approximately **$40.93 billion in capital appropriations** to expand advanced-node wafer production, advanced packaging, fab construction, facility systems, and 2027 R&D. The board also approved ceilings of up to $500 million each for strategic investments in [[anthropic]] and [[openai]], plus up to $500 million for a separate semiconductor-ecosystem investment vehicle — a meaningful shift from pure manufacturing-partner posture toward direct financial exposure to frontier labs.
