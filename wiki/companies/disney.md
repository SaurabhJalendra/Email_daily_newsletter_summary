## Summary

Disney enters newsletter coverage in mid-December 2025 as the year's most consequential rights-holder–AI deal. On 2025-12-13 Disney signed a multi-year, ~$1B partnership with [[openai]] granting Sora and ChatGPT Image access to 200+ characters from the Disney/Pixar/Marvel/Star Wars catalog — and on the same day filed suit against [[google]] over AI usage of its IP. The combination — license OpenAI, sue Google — became the period's clearest signal that major rights-holders are willing to pick AI-platform winners rather than blanket-ban generation. Follow-up coverage (Dec 16) clarifies Disney holds 1-year exclusivity for Sora characters.
