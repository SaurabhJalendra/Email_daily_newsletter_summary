## Summary

Amazon (AWS) is one of the three hyperscalers most central to the AI compute build-out. In late October 2025 it suffered a global AWS outage, drew critical coverage of "AI lag and internal bureaucracy," and announced 30,000 corporate-workforce layoffs. The story flipped on 2025-11-04: AWS signed a $38B, multi-year deal with [[openai]] to provide hundreds of thousands of NVIDIA GPUs across AWS data centers — OpenAI's largest single diversification away from Microsoft Azure and Amazon's most consequential AI-cloud customer win to date. Amazon Q3 earnings landed strong (AWS revenue +20.2% to $33B), helping reframe the layoff narrative.
