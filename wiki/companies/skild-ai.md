## Summary

**Skild AI** is a robotics foundation-model startup building general-purpose models for embodied AI. Its flagship model **[[skild-s1]]** demonstrates **in-context learning for robotics** — a single 10-minute video of a task is enough for S1 to reproduce the behavior on real hardware without fine-tuning, positioned as *"a significant step beyond typical robot foundation models"* (per TLDR Hardware). Coverage frames Skild as competing structurally with the world-action-model cohort ([[world-action-models]], [[nvidia-cosmos]], [[isaac-groot]]) but with a *single-shot video-demonstration* substrate rather than an *ingest-massive-training-corpora* substrate.
