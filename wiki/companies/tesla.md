## Summary

Tesla is Elon Musk's electric-vehicle company, now positioned as a **vertically-integrated AI hardware + application company** spanning chip-design + fab-tier ownership (via the [[terafab]] JV with [[spacex]]) + AI training compute (Dojo) + humanoid robotics ([[humanoid-robotics]] Optimus program) + autonomous-vehicle stack (Cybercab program). Tesla's AI-substrate strategy has increasingly diverged from consumer-vehicle margins into direct AI-infrastructure ownership — the mid-2026 Terafab JV productizes a *five-tier vertical AI stack* (silicon-fab → compute-infrastructure → chip-design → robotics/AV application → consumer product), one of the deepest vertical-integration postures in the AI-substrate landscape.

Tesla's AI-substrate footprint intersects with [[spacex]] in multiple ways: (a) shared AI-chip supply via [[terafab]] JV supplying both Optimus/Cybercab and SpaceX orbital DCs; (b) common Musk-controlled capital-allocation across cross-Musk-company JVs; (c) shared talent/leadership pipeline across Tesla + SpaceX + xAI. Tesla's [[tesla-megapod]] modular AI DC hardware also enters the AI-infrastructure-vendor market against [[nvidia]] DGX, extending Tesla's silicon-tier position downstream into rack-scale AI systems.
