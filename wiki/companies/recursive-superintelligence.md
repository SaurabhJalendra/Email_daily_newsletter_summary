## Summary

Recursive Superintelligence is a San Francisco AI startup founded by ex-DeepMind researcher Tim Rocktäschel that emerged in May 2026 with $650M raised at a ~$4.65B valuation in a single day. The company's founding thesis is the "open-endedness" approach to recursive self-improvement — AI systems that continuously evolve through autonomous ideation, implementation, and validation cycles, with little or no help from human developers. Rocktäschel cites Stanisław Lem's concept of the "information barrier" as the founding framing. Notable team additions include former Google research chief Peter Norvig. Lands alongside [[ineffable-intelligence]] (David Silver's RL "superlearners" lab) and the broader 2026 wave of frontier-lab spinouts pursuing post-LLM paradigms — including Yann LeCun's [[ami-labs]] world-models bet and the [[andrej-karpathy]] → [[anthropic]] move targeting Claude-on-Claude pretraining acceleration.
