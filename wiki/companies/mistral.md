## Summary

Mistral AI is the Paris-based frontier lab known for its open-weight model line (Mistral 7B, Mixtral, Codestral) and a more recent commercial / enterprise push. In late October 2025 it launched [[mistral-studio]], a platform for moving AI prototypes into production with built-in performance tracking, testing, and security tooling — its clearest enterprise-facing product to date and an answer to OpenAI's AgentKit and Anthropic's Claude for Excel by way of "production AI infrastructure" rather than first-party agents.
