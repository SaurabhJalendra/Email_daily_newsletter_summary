## Summary

Trajectory is one of the AI Supremacy [[neo-labs]] cohort — a research startup focused on **building a platform for continual learning, enabling AI products to learn continuously from real-world user interactions**. The thesis sits structurally between two existing wiki surfaces: the fine-tuning / RLHF tier ([[fine-tuning]], Tinker API) and the persistent-memory tier ([[ai-memory]], [[agentmemory]]). The "continual learning from real-world user interactions" framing reads as a *productized RLHF-as-a-service-on-live-traffic* bet — a substrate other product teams can plug into rather than building their own RL/fine-tuning loops.
