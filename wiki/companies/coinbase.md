## Summary

Coinbase is the US-listed crypto exchange (founded 2012) that in mid-2026 emerged as one of the largest publicly-disclosed production AI-agent operators. CEO Brian Armstrong revealed the company runs **1,200 full-time AI agents** with a recursive-self-improvement loop (agents collect customer feedback → prioritize → draft code → generate pull requests → humans approve + feed corrections back into the underlying context) that has driven **2× year-over-year code-per-developer output**. Armstrong's canonical operating principle: treat AI agents as collaborators — humans review output and update the *underlying context that generated it*, not the output itself.

The company simultaneously productized regulated AI-driven finance via **Coinbase Advisor**, an SEC-registered investment-advice product that removed the traditional *"this is not investment advice"* disclaimer and executes trades + offers tax-loss harvesting + financial-literacy education. Armstrong's forward thesis frames a *"bank for the agentic economy"* — one AI agent orchestrates hundreds of thousands of others to buy goods and services, with financial infrastructure built for *non-human principals*. Positioning frame: [[openai]] and [[anthropic]] "will not build every company" — regulated-finance is a defensible vertical where Coinbase's 13-year trust and regulatory-infrastructure head start wins.
