## Summary

AMD is a chipmaker positioning itself as an alternative to [[nvidia]] in AI accelerators. In October 2025 it announced a strategic partnership with [[openai]] (2025-10-07) and then with [[oracle]] (2025-10-15), planting AMD in two of the biggest AI compute buyers within a two-week window. In July 2026 AMD stock surged 6.6% on the Turing self-driving 10%-AI-training-workload-on-AMD-GPUs signal — first publicly framed *"AI training work shifting away from Nvidia's near-monopoly"* concrete-share datapoint, activating the AMD-as-Nvidia-alternative thesis at the sub-frontier-lab-customer tier.
