## Summary

TypeSafe AI is the vendor behind [[jev]], the September 2026 "System One Model" that returns typed decisions with calibrated confidence scores instead of free-form text. The company was founded by Diogo Almeida — an ex-[[openai]] employee credited as a co-creator/co-inventor of [[chatgpt]] — and formalized publicly on 2026-09-17-morning after Jev's launch on 2026-09-15/16.

Jev is TypeSafe AI's first product and defines the company's positioning: a *"non-generative decision-model substrate"* that lives inside software and makes judgment calls (routing, classification, ranking, gating, policy decisions) rather than producing prose. Positioned at ~200× faster + ~400× cheaper than [[chatgpt]] and [[claude-opus-5]] for equivalent decision workloads, with a training-time architecture that outputs a probability distribution over a *predefined answer space* — architecturally guaranteeing that the model *cannot hallucinate free-text*. Training uses a novel Almeida-authored algorithm called **RLCD** and 100%-synthetic training data.
