## Summary

METR (**M**odel **E**valuation & **T**hreat **R**esearch) is an independent AI-safety and evaluation nonprofit that studies frontier-model capabilities, autonomous AI systems, AI R&D acceleration, and risks from loss of control. Its work includes long-horizon capability evaluations, predeployment assessments conducted with AI developers, monitoring-system stress tests, and investigations of AI-agent incidents. Recent work has expanded its Time Horizon evaluation suite and examined whether AI systems are materially accelerating AI R&D.

In Nov 2026, METR published a **high-access assessment of Anthropic** conducted from Aug 12 to Oct 29 2026, measuring how much AI was accelerating AI R&D. The accompanying external review of Anthropic's automated-R&D risk analysis concluded that **internally deployed models as of July 15 posed low risk of directly automating R&D in key domains or creating acute catastrophic acceleration risks**. METR reports roughly **$71 million in new commitments over the prior six months**, supporting work on autonomous capabilities, recursive self-improvement, monitoring, risk assessment, and incident investigation. The assessment sits structurally alongside [[anthropic]]'s own near-RSI S-1 disclosure and governance commitment as a concrete independent-eval-org counterweight on the [[recursive-self-improvement]] arc.
