## Summary

Meituan is a Chinese super-app and local-services giant that, since Nov 2026, has become a frontier-tier open-weight AI vendor via its LongCat model family. The company's AI team — the LongCat team — has shipped [[longcat-2-5]], a native multimodal mixture-of-experts system with ~1.6 trillion total parameters, ~48 billion active parameters, and an approximately one-million-token context window, under the MIT license. The release positions Meituan alongside [[deepseek]], [[alibaba]] (Qwen), [[moonshot-ai]] (Kimi), [[zhipu-ai]] (GLM), and [[baidu]] (ERNIE) in the canonical mid-2026 Chinese open-weight frontier-cohort — and distinctively productizes Meituan as the *canonical first-moment-trillion-parameter-open-weight-vendor-publicly-attributed-fully-Chinese-hardware-trained-plus-Huawei-Ascend-deployed substrate operator*, per the Nov 16 2026 report that LongCat-2.5 was trained on Chinese hardware with a sample deployment on Huawei Ascend chips. Reports place LongCat-2.5 near the top of Terminal Bench results.
