## Summary

Baseten is an AI inference + post-trained-open-models infrastructure company productizing the "owned intelligence" thesis — companies running, fine-tuning, and retaining control over their AI models and data rather than relying solely on external APIs from frontier labs. The June 23, 2026 Series F raise of $1.5B at a **$13B valuation** lands as one of the cycle's headline enterprise-AI infrastructure funding events; revenue is publicly framed as growing roughly **20× in a year**, positioning Baseten in the AI-inference-infrastructure category alongside Coreweave + Crusoe + Lambda Labs + Nebius. The strategic positioning is structurally distinct from pure compute-rental Neoclouds (SpaceX, Coreweave): Baseten is explicitly framed around *post-trained open models + inference as critical component of enterprise control planes*, productizing the open-weights inference + customer-controlled-fine-tune surface (relevant to [[glm-5-2]] / [[ollama]] / open-frontier-model adoption inside enterprises).

The cycle pairs Baseten's funding with the broader SpaceX Neocloud framing (AINews HIGH "SpaceX is already a $28B/yr Neocloud") + Reflection $6.3B open-frontier-models deal + Sakana Fugu multi-agent orchestration cycle — the broader cycle reads as a *deepening of the open-source-and-enterprise-controlled AI infrastructure* axis. AINews canonicalizes the "owned intelligence" thesis: companies want to *run, fine-tune, and retain control* over their AI models — a strategic shift from API-consumer to AI-platform-operator at the enterprise tier. Baseten is positioned as the *commercial pure-play* on this thesis.
