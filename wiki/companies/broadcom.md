## Summary

Broadcom is the US semiconductor company positioned as a custom AI-silicon partner for frontier labs. The October 2025 [[openai]] partnership extended the industry pattern of labs diversifying away from sole [[nvidia]] dependence. In April 2026 Broadcom's role expanded: it became the co-partner (with [[google]]) in Anthropic's gigawatt-scale TPU expansion powering the Claude family.
