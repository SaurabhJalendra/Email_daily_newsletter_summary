## Summary

Canva is the Sydney-headquartered design platform that, in late October 2025, moved decisively into the "AI-native design" frame: it shipped [[canva-design-model]] (its own foundation model trained specifically on design principles) plus a "Creative Operating System" framing and Video 2.0 with AI editing. Coverage placed Canva alongside [[adobe]] (Firefly + Express AI) and Anthropic's [[claude-design]] in the design-tooling battleground that defined the late-2025 / early-2026 window.
