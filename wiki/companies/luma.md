## Summary

Luma AI is a video-generation lab best known for its Dream Machine / Ray model line. In late December 2025 it ships Luma Ray3 Modify — a model that lets users reshape existing footage while preserving the original performance (motion, expression, identity), moving the category from pure text-to-video generation into AI-assisted video editing.
