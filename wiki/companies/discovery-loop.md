## Summary

Discovery Loop is a **public benefit corporation** founded on August 5, 2026 by **Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le** — four of [[google]]'s longest-tenured and most senior AI/systems researchers — with a mission to **automate machine learning, science, and engineering**. Announced via a Jeff Dean X-post the same day [[demis-hassabis]] stepped down as [[google]] DeepMind CEO, the departure lands as the most consequential concurrent AI-leadership exodus from Google since the DeepMind–Brain merger.

Google is a named investor and has agreed to **provide computing power for at least the next year**, framing Discovery Loop less as a rival lab and more as a spin-out with continuing infrastructure ties. Positioning is "autoresearch" — using AI to accelerate breakthroughs in ML, science, and engineering — echoing the DeepMind AlphaFold + AlphaEvolve + AlphaProof lineage but as an independent PBC. The launch adds Discovery Loop to the mid-2026 wave of frontier-adjacent independent labs alongside [[safe-superintelligence]] (Sutskever) and [[thinking-machines]] (Murati).
