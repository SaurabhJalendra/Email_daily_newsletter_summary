## Summary

Stripe is a US payments infrastructure company. It entered this wiki's AI-relevant coverage in July 2026 with the launch of **[[stripe-kai]]** — an internal enterprise **Knowledge AI Platform** for data-warehouse queries + account research + incident triage + revenue modeling + compliance-review prep. On 2026-08-17 Stripe took a decisive external-facing step by **agreeing to acquire [[openrouter]] for over $7B** — the model-routing platform providing unified API access to 400+ AI models — giving Stripe a foothold in AI infrastructure and canonicalizing model routing as a core enterprise AI infrastructure primitive worthy of multi-billion-dollar M&A.

Together the two motions position Stripe as an *AI-infrastructure builder* alongside its payments-API franchise, rather than only a payments company with an internal AI tool.
