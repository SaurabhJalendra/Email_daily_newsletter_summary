## Summary

Nebius is an AI cloud company expanding from raw compute into inference and agentic platforms, aiming to become a full-stack AI-workload platform. Its **Token Factory** managed inference platform is the core offering — designed as a scalable, efficient runtime for AI models in production. Nebius made three acquisitions in six weeks — **Eigen AI** (model + runtime optimization), **Clarifai** (system-layer), and **Tavily** (agentic search) — to deepen its expertise across the full inference-to-agentic-orchestration stack. CTO **Danila Shtan** emphasizes *execution speed as moat* and positions Nebius as go-to infrastructure for independent builders. Nebius sits in the same neo-inference-cloud cohort as [[baseten]] / [[fireworks]] / [[together-ai]] / [[friendliai]], all riding the mid-2026 *"center of gravity in AI is shifting from training to inference"* thesis with Chinese-open-weight and mid-tier-model runtime demand.
