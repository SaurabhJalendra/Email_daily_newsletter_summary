## Summary

Oracle is the enterprise cloud / database vendor whose role in the AI build-out is increasingly as a GPU-heavy cloud supplier. In October 2025 it announced a partnership with [[amd]] to pull non-NVIDIA AI silicon into its big-cloud fleet. On 2026-04-03 Oracle began executing a mass layoff — up to 30,000 employees — explicitly to free $8-10B of annual expense to redirect into its AI data-center buildout, making it one of the largest "AI pivot" workforce restructurings of the cycle alongside [[amazon]]'s and [[block]]'s and slotting Oracle into the same AI-efficiency-replaces-headcount narrative Jack Dorsey articulated the same day.
