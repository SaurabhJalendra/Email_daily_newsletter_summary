## Summary

Oracle is the enterprise cloud/database vendor increasingly positioned as a **GPU-and-accelerator-heavy cloud supplier**. In October 2025 it partnered with [[amd]] to pull non-NVIDIA AI silicon into its fleet. On 2026-04-03 Oracle began a mass layoff — up to 30,000 employees — explicitly to redirect $8-10B/yr into AI data-center buildout. On October 27 2026 Oracle and [[google]] announced that **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) will deploy Google's 8th-generation TPUs** ([[tpu]] 8t for training + TPU 8i for inference) starting in 2027, with capacity ramping over several years toward reported gigawatt scale — the first hyperscale cloud outside Google to offer TPUs. Oracle supplies the data-center power, cooling, and networking (OCI liquid-cooled DCs + Acceleron SmartNIC fabric). The agreement positions Oracle as a *silicon-neutral hyperscaler* alongside [[nvidia]] GPUs + [[amd]] AI silicon + Google TPUs — a materially broader AI-compute portfolio than either Google Cloud or AWS can offer in-house.
