## Summary

Xiaomi is a Chinese electronics + AI conglomerate that has expanded from consumer-devices distribution into first-party AI research and open-source model releases. The company's AI surface in this wiki now has four named layers: (1) **on-device + robotics distribution** — [[openclaw]] deployed across Xiaomi smartphones and smart-home devices for natural-language control (2026-03-29); (2) **coding-specialized model release** — [[mimo-code]] coding model (June 2026 three-cycle convergence); (3) **agent framework research** — Xiaomi's **Darwin Agent Team** ships the [[harnessx]] *"agent foundry"* framework (July 2026); (4) **trillion-parameter frontier-tier open-weights model release** — the **MiMo team** ships [[mimo-v2-6]] (Sep 2026), a 1.02T-parameter MoE that debuts as the top open-weights model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (46) on the Pareto frontier at $0.13/task, trained for **$3M** under MIT license with **>7,000 training environments + technical report + RL code** all published. Fuli Luo, a former [[deepseek]] star engineer, is publicly attributed for the MiMo team's frontier work. AINews's *"crowning a new Chinese frontier lab"* cycle-headline canonicalizes Xiaomi as *seventh named Chinese-frontier-open-lab* alongside the six named Chinese AI Tigers.
