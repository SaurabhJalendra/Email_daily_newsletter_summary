## Summary

Apple's AI story in October 2025 is catch-up: an internal-only Siri chatbot revamp, a pivot toward AI glasses to close the gap with [[meta]], a scaled-back VR focus, and public speculation about a Tim Cook successor. Mid-month it shipped the [[apple-m5]] chip — its strongest on-device-AI hardware push yet — alongside an (unnamed) AI startup acquisition and further AI-team reshuffles. The story shifted on 2025-11-03: Apple effectively abandoned its solo-AI strategy by licensing a private [[google]] [[gemini]] model to power the next Siri — a structural reversal that re-frames the rest of the year's catch-up moves. Apple Q3 2025 revenue landed strong at $102.5B.
