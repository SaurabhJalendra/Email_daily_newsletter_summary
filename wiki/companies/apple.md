The story shifted on 2025-11-03: Apple effectively abandoned its solo-AI strategy by licensing a private [[google]] [[gemini]] model to power the next Siri — a structural reversal that re-frames the rest of the year's catch-up moves. Apple Q3 2025 revenue landed strong at $102.5B.
