## Summary

Unitree is a Chinese robotics manufacturer that has emerged as one of the canonical mid-2026 humanoid + quadruped-robot vendors. It is credited as the **first humanoid robot maker listed on China's stock market** (Shanghai IPO priced Aug 2026 at 150.8 yuan/share, ~$9B valuation on $238M 2025 revenue and >4× YoY growth). AgiBot briefly overtook Unitree in mid-August 2026 as the world's largest humanoid vendor, illustrating dense and rapidly-rotating competitive tier among Chinese humanoid manufacturers.

Unitree's canonical product-tier motion in mid-August 2026 is the **"Superman"** humanoid — a robot that can *run faster than Usain Bolt* but *has no working hands*. Cross-newsletter coverage framed this as the canonical demo-capability-vs-real-world-utility gap narrative: impressive speed on-camera, missing basic manipulation capabilities off-camera. The robot participated in the Beijing World Humanoid Robot Games (2,056 robots, 16 countries, remote controls banned — autonomy required).
