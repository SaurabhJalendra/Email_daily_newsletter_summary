## Summary

Tenex is an applied-AI company (CEO Alex Lieberman, CJ Hess) that helps Fortune 2000 enterprises transition from traditional software development to an AI-native software development lifecycle ([[ai-native-sdlc]]). Rather than framing AI agents as coding copilots, Tenex treats them as participants across the full engineering process: requirements and access, planning, design review, build, demo checkpoints, testing and QA, shipping, and hypercare/handoff. The company's internal "MetaHarness" tool integrates reusable agent skills, project documents, a CLI, and integrations with GitHub, Linear, and other systems to support shared context between humans and agents.

As of October 2026 the company employs ~75 people (~90% engineers or AI strategists), pays its engineers on an output basis via a story-point system that reportedly produced a 2× output increase over the past year, and is bearish on vertical-specific AI solutions, citing the difficulty of delivering customized solutions at software margins against the risk that frontier models absorb those tasks. Tenex is working to deploy agents into production for non-engineering work with permissioning and access control as a focus.
