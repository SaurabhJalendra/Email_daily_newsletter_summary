## Summary

Axiom Math is an AI-for-math research organization that uses AI-assisted swarms of agents to translate large, decades-long mathematical proofs into machine-checkable form. Its first canonical productization is a **Lean formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups (CFSG)** — a roughly 15,000-page proof informally called the "Enormous Theorem" — delivered as approximately 43.1 million lines of Lean code during a 24-day effort. Human mathematicians directed swarms of AI agents through Axiom's **AxiomProver** platform; the released repository is described as *"sorry-free"* and is checked by Lean's kernel using standard axioms.

Structurally, Axiom sits alongside Mistral's [[leanstral]] + Shieldstral theorem-proving line and Anthropic's Oct 2026 formal-verification research publication as a *concrete late-2026 AI-for-formal-mathematics substrate operator* — distinct in that it targets formalization of *existing* mathematical proofs at million-line scale rather than new program verification, and that its workflow is explicitly a *human-directed agent-swarm orchestration* rather than a single model.
