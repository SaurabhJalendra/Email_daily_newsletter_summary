## Summary

Perplexity is an AI-first search company. In early October 2025 it appeared in newsletters in connection with [[perplexity-comet]] — its AI browser that pushes the category beyond chat-over-search into full browser-level agents. By mid-October it was being compared head-to-head against OpenAI's GPTs via its "Spaces" feature, framing Perplexity as a consumer-workspace competitor. Late October to early November 2025 added two distinct stories: a Getty Images licensing/sourcing deal and a publicly framed defense-in-depth security posture for [[perplexity-comet]] in response to the [[chatgpt-atlas]] hidden-text browser-hijack class of attacks (see [[prompt-injection]]). On 2025-11-03 Mindstream framed a court ruling as Perplexity gaining "legal permission" — a positive content-licensing/data-rights signal.
