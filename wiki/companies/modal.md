## Summary

Modal is a US AI-infrastructure platform offering **serverless AI compute** — developers run code without provisioning clusters, predicting load, or paying for idle resources. Founded by CEO Erik Bernhardsson (ex-Spotify, ex-Better.com, author of Annoy approximate-nearest-neighbor library), Modal has grown from a 2022 seed into a $16B-valuation infrastructure company. Modal's product surface spans functions, sandboxes, training, and batch processing, aiming at a multi-product platform for AI applications. Sandboxes alone account for roughly a third of revenue after nearly 3 billion sandbox spin-ups.

Modal is representative of the "serverless-GPU + agent-cloud" infrastructure thesis — elastic GPU access with per-second billing, enabling AI products that would otherwise require always-on cluster provisioning. The company competes-and-coexists with [[baseten]], [[fireworks]], [[together-ai]], [[nebius]] and [[sf-compute]] in the AI-inference / AI-compute middleware tier.
