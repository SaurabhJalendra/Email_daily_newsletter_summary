## Summary

Qualcomm — long the dominant supplier of mobile-phone silicon — has expanded across two strategic axes during 2025–2026: first a push into data-center AI inference (AI200 / AI250 accelerators, late 2025), and then, in June 2026, a CEO-level declaration that *the app era is over* — agents are replacing apps across 40+ new device designs spanning smart glasses, wearable pins, and earbuds. The June 2026 declaration is structurally significant: Qualcomm sells silicon into virtually every consumer device class outside Apple, so a Qualcomm-CEO-tier framing that the *app-tier* of device interaction is being displaced by *agent-tier* interaction is also a statement of where Qualcomm intends to invest its on-device-AI silicon roadmap.
