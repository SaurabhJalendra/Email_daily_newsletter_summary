## Summary

Qualcomm — long the dominant supplier of mobile-phone silicon — is making a 2025 push into AI inference for data centers. Its AI200 (shipping 2026) and AI250 (2027) inference accelerators were unveiled in late October 2025 alongside news that Qualcomm had also acquired Arduino. The strategic story: diversification from mobile into edge / data-center AI compute, against a market dominated by [[nvidia]] and increasingly contested by [[amd]] and [[broadcom]]. Shares jumped roughly 20% on the data-center announcement.

- Acquisitions: Arduino (Oct 2025)
