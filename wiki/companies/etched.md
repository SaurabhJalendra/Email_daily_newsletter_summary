## Summary

Etched is an AI inference chip startup that exited stealth mode on July 1 2026 after raising $800M, securing over $1B in contracts, and preparing to ship its first inference racks in summer 2026. The company reached a $5B valuation at the stealth-exit event. Etched is framed by newsletter coverage as an *Nvidia challenger* whose specialized inference silicon competes on cost/perf against GPU-based inference stacks — joining an increasingly crowded inference-chip competitive surface that includes [[cerebras]] (wafer-scale, powering OpenAI Codex), [[groq]] (LPU / pivot to inference cloud), and OpenAI's own [[openai-jalapeno]] custom ASIC.

The stealth-exit event pairs newsletter framings around a *working inference chip* (Forward Future) with concrete commercial-traction metrics ($1B+ in contracts, first racks shipping this summer) — significantly de-risked posture vs the typical pre-revenue AI-chip startup stealth-exit. Positioned as one of the highest-profile inference-specific silicon plays outside the OpenAI ecosystem heading into H2 2026.
