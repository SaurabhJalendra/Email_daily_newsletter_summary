## Summary

Skool is an online community and course-hosting platform that has become a default surface for AI-education communities aimed at operators, freelancers, and small businesses. It combines discussion boards, membership management, and structured learning modules in one product, letting AI-focused creators run cohorts, share implementation resources, and gate paid tiers with weekly live calls. In the AI/ML space, Skool hosts communities like [[ai-automation-society]] — 305K+ members focused on no-code AI automation with [[n8n]] and [[claude-code]] — positioning Skool as part of the *AI-education distribution infrastructure* alongside YouTube, Substack, and Discord.

Skool matters less as a technical AI product and more as a *venue signal*: the platform's rapid adoption by AI-automation creators suggests that AI-adjacent education is consolidating away from generic social platforms into purpose-built community/course environments. That in turn shapes how non-technical users encounter and learn to deploy AI tools.
