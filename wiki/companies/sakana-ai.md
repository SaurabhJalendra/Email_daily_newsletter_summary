## Summary

Sakana AI is a Tokyo-based AI research lab whose wiki appearance is anchored on its 2026-06-06 launch of a dedicated **RSI Lab** ([[recursive-self-improvement]]) in Tokyo. The lab is the first publicly framed *named* organizational unit explicitly dedicated to RSI at a frontier-adjacent lab, landing the day after [[anthropic]]'s "Sparks of RSI" essay and concretizing the framing into a structural commitment outside the US frontier labs. Sakana's broader research history centers on AI scientist / autonomous research and evolutionary / nature-inspired model architectures, which positions the RSI Lab as a natural continuation of its autonomous-research agenda rather than a pivot.
