## Summary

Fractile is a specialized-inference-processor startup building silicon around an **in-memory-computing architecture** — a design that co-locates compute with memory to sidestep the memory-bandwidth bottleneck that dominates LLM inference cost + latency on conventional GPUs. The company claims its chips can run large-language-model inference **up to 25× faster** than conventional silicon at *a fraction of the operating cost*. Reports position Fractile at a **~$6.5B potential valuation**, placing it in the mid-2026 non-NVIDIA-inference-silicon capital-tier cohort alongside [[etched]], [[sambanova]], and [[groq]].

Fractile canonicalizes **in-memory-computing** as a fifth substantially-differentiated inference-silicon substrate axis in the mid-2026 landscape (alongside wafer-scale [[cerebras]] / ASIC [[etched]]/[[taalas]] / LPU [[groq]] / thermodynamic [[extropic]]).
