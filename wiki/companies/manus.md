## Summary

Manus is a Singapore-headquartered AI agent startup — with roots in Chinese AI research — whose flagship product is a general-purpose agent that performs open-ended deep-research and task-execution workflows from a single user prompt. In late December 2025 [[meta]] acquires Manus for $2B+ to integrate its agent capabilities into Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. The deal is notable as one of the first instances of a major US tech company buying a startup with Chinese roots in the geopolitically tense AI environment, and it cements Meta's bet on "personal superintelligence" / general-purpose agents as a social-platform feature.
