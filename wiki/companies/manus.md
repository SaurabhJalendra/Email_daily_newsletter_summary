## Summary

Manus is a Singapore-headquartered AI agent startup — with roots in Chinese AI research — whose flagship product was originally a general-purpose agent that performs open-ended deep-research and task-execution workflows from a single user prompt. In late December 2025 [[meta]] agreed to acquire Manus for $2B+ to integrate its agent capabilities into Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, but China formally blocked the acquisition in July 2026 amid growing geopolitical tension over AI ownership, data, and compute infrastructure. In September 2026 Manus recovered onto a standalone path with **Manus 2.0** — a from-scratch platform rebuild introducing a new architecture and product line — and shipped its first named consumer product, **[[cue]]**, a personal-AI-agent app in which each agent gets its own email address, phone number, digital wallet, and computer with user-defined-budget payment authority. Vaibhav Sisinty's newsletter framed Cue as *"the cutest one since Grok Bot"*, positioning Manus in the late-Sep-2026 concrete-personal-AI-agent-substrate cohort alongside [[grok-bot]] + [[instinct]] + [[meta-muse-agent]].
