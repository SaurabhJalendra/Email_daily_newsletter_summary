## Summary

AI Automation Society is a large [[skool]]-hosted community founded by Nate Herk, positioned as one of the biggest global hubs for building and monetizing no-code AI automations. Its membership footprint is reported at ~305,600 to ~420,000 depending on source and window, spanning small businesses, freelancers, and agency operators who use tools like [[n8n]], [[claude-code]], and Hermes to automate business workflows. The community pairs a free public tier with a paid "Plus" tier that unlocks weekly Q&A calls with Nate Herk and structured job-oriented resources.

Programming skews toward practical implementation: YouTube-style member videos (e.g., Nate Herk on Claude Code + Clay for lead generation), community project showcases (voice dictation systems, luxury-real-estate automations, scraper tools), and cohort-style live sessions. The Society matters as a distribution + education layer for the [[no-code-ai-automation]] trend — training non-technical operators to build and sell AI-powered services.
