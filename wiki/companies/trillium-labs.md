## Summary

Trillium Labs is a US non-profit introduced October 2 2026 by Interconnects author Nathan Lambert and co-founder Tom Zick, aimed at *fostering the open science of frontier AI*. The organization plans fully open post-training recipes — data, code, evaluations, and intermediate checkpoints — and intends to expand into open infrastructure for studying recursive self-improvement, reward-hacking, multi-agent systems, and other emerging frontier-lab topics. Launch backing comes from Halcyon Futures and Schmidt Sciences, with plans to fundraise from a broader coalition.

Trillium's framing is a direct response to the increasing opacity of frontier labs: as commercial AI research locks away training recipes, Trillium positions itself as the independent-science counterweight needed to reproduce, audit, and extend frontier-lab claims — pairing structurally with [[ai2]]'s open-model lineage and the broader [[open-source-models]] trend.
