## Summary

Conviction is an AI-focused venture capital firm founded by Sarah Guo (previously Greylock GP), with roughly a billion dollars across three funds. Its thesis is a **bet against the frontier-labs-build-everything hypothesis**: rather than backing foundation-model-training companies, Conviction invests in companies that **apply AI models** — the application, inference, and workflow layers built on top of the labs. Guo's argument: labs will become metered-input infrastructure providers, while the biggest opportunities live *above* the foundation models (Harvey for law, Baseten for owned-inference, etc.). She also argues that the market for AI is *labor*, not software budgets, and that even large companies have only one A-team — most of their product surface is not staffed by it, so they can't out-execute focused startups outside their core.

Six of Conviction's portfolio companies have crossed **$10B in valuation**, including [[baseten]] (run-your-own-models infrastructure) and Harvey (legal-AI tools).
