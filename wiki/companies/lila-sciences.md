## Summary

Lila Sciences is an AI-for-science startup building a *"lab as data center"* — an automated wet-lab facility that runs AI-guided robotics + lab equipment to produce new experiments 24/7, generating a massive library of scientific reasoning tokens as substrate for a general reasoner capable of tackling any scientific problem. As of mid-July 2026, Lila reports a library of over **10 trillion experimentally validated scientific tokens** built via a data-generation pipeline where *nature acts as the verifier* — reinforcement learning optimizing for fast round-over-round iteration rather than big noisy multiplexed screens.

Publicly framed by CTO **Andy Beam** and CSO **Rafa Gómez-Bombarelli** on Latent.Space, Lila's thesis is that *"the scientific method is the last untapped internet-scale dataset"* — a structural extension of the Sutton *Bitter Lesson* framing into a wet-lab-as-training-data-factory architecture. Lila's approach is structurally distinct from AI-for-science efforts that accelerate *existing* wet-lab workflows: it architects the wet lab itself as an RL training-data pipeline.
