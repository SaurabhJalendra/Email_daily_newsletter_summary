## Summary

Microsoft anchors enterprise AI distribution through the Copilot surface (Windows, Office, GitHub, Edge) and its deep [[openai]] partnership. The late-October 2025 cycle pushed Copilot from chat assistant to platform: "Mico" gave Copilot a friendly personality, Copilot App Builder turned Copilot into a no-code application surface, Vision/Voice features expanded multimodal use, and an AI-browser layer surfaced. On the partnership side, Microsoft renewed its [[openai]] terms in late October 2025 — keeping a reported ~27% economic stake through the for-profit recapitalization. On the infrastructure side, Microsoft signed a $9.7B compute deal with IREN. Microsoft also remains a named hub in [[github-agent-hq]]'s multi-agent control plane. Build 2026 (June 2026) marked the company's clearest decoupling from OpenAI to date: it shipped seven in-house MAI models (with [[mai-thinking-1]] as a Claude-class reasoning model), introduced its first always-on agent [[microsoft-scout]], previewed an agent-first device platform (Project Solara) and the Majorana 2 quantum chip, and announced a Mayo Clinic healthcare AI partnership.
