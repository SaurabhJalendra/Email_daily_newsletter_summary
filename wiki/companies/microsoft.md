## Summary

Microsoft anchors enterprise AI distribution through the Copilot surface (Windows, Office, GitHub, Edge) and its deep [[openai]] partnership. The late-October 2025 cycle pushed Copilot from chat assistant to platform: "Mico" gave Copilot a friendly personality, Copilot App Builder turned Copilot into a no-code application surface, Vision/Voice features expanded multimodal use, and an AI-browser layer surfaced. On the partnership side, Microsoft renewed its [[openai]] terms in late October 2025 — keeping a reported ~27% economic stake through the for-profit recapitalization. On the infrastructure side, Microsoft signed a $9.7B compute deal with IREN. Microsoft also remains a named hub in [[github-agent-hq]]'s multi-agent control plane.
