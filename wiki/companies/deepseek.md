## Summary

DeepSeek is the Chinese frontier AI lab known for releasing competitive open-source models, including DeepSeek-V3 and the R1 reasoning family. In late October 2025 it shipped [[deepseek-ocr]], an open-source OCR model whose architectural twist — converting text into visual tokens for ~10× compression — drew sustained coverage from AI-engineering newsletters. The release reinforced DeepSeek's pattern of practical, infrastructure-relevant open-source drops aimed at long-context efficiency rather than headline benchmarks.
