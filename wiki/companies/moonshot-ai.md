## Summary

Moonshot AI (the company behind the "Kimi" assistant) is a Chinese frontier-lab player that broke into global newsletter coverage in early November 2025 with [[kimi-k2-thinking]]. The model, released open-source, reportedly tops GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 on several benchmarks — making Moonshot, alongside [[alibaba]] (Qwen), [[deepseek]], and [[baidu]] (ERNIE), part of the four-lab cluster now framed as the Chinese open-source answer to Western closed-source frontier labs.
