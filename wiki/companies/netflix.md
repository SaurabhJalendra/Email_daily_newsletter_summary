## Summary

Netflix's principal 2026 AI/ML signal is the **$587 million acquisition of InterPositive**, Ben Affleck's 16-person filmmaking-technology startup that builds **project-specific AI post-production tools** trained on each production's own dailies. InterPositive's system handles reframing, relighting, color mixing, rotoscoping, wire removal, background enhancement, and continuity fixes — explicitly *non-generative*, workflow-focused AI positioned as a force-multiplier for human crews rather than a replacement.

Netflix has framed the deal as an investment in *"creator-led innovation that keeps filmmakers at the center of the process"* and states it will use InterPositive's tools internally for Netflix productions rather than sell them commercially. Reported deal size is **$587M–$600M** in cash plus performance-based earn-outs — one of Netflix's largest acquisitions, second only to its ~$700M Roald Dahl Story Company purchase. The entire 16-person InterPositive team joins Netflix, with Affleck becoming a **senior adviser** on AI-driven film technology. Industry-wide, the acquisition signals that major streamers view proprietary production datasets (dailies) as strategic assets worth premium-priced vertically-integrated AI, potentially cutting below-the-line costs by 10–20% and reshaping post-production economics.
