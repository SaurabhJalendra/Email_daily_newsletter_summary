## Summary

Adobe is the creative-software incumbent (Photoshop, Premiere, Illustrator, Lightroom, Express, Firefly) that has spent 2025–2026 rolling AI into every surface. In April 2026 it shipped Firefly AI Assistant, a conversational agent that spans six of its apps and executes multi-step creative workflows from a single natural-language prompt. Coverage paired the launch with [[anthropic]]'s [[claude-design]] release the same week, framing them together as direct pressure on Figma and Adobe's own traditional UI-driven design model.
