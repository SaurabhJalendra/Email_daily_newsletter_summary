## Summary

Nscale is an AI infrastructure and cloud-compute provider that ships large-scale GPU + specialized-accelerator capacity for frontier-lab customers. Its canonical wiki-tier signal is the **$45 billion, ~6-year compute-rental agreement with [[anthropic]]** covering approximately **460 megawatts** of compute capacity at an Nscale data-center development in **West Virginia** — one of the largest single-customer compute-substrate commitments in the mid-2026 AI-hardware-substrate cycle, and Anthropic's first WV-tier geographic anchor.

Structurally Nscale sits alongside [[terawulf]] ($19B Anthropic lease), [[volta]] ($10B / 133MW Vera Rubin), and [[spacex]] (Colossus 1 lease) as one of the four multi-substrate compute-diversification providers Anthropic is using to secure long-term training + inference capacity for its next generation of frontier models and agents.
