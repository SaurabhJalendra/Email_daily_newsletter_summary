## Summary

World Labs is Dr. Fei-Fei Li's AI startup, organized around the thesis that "spatial intelligence" — systems that understand the physics and geometry of the real world — is the next frontier after language. In mid-November 2025 it launched [[marble]], the first production-grade commercial world model, generating persistent 3D environments from text, images, video, or 3D layouts. The launch coincided with Yann LeCun's departure from [[meta]] to start his own world-model lab, marking the moment when "world models" became a formally contested category alongside LLMs.
