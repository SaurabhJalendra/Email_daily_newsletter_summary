## Summary

**Ahead of AI** is [[sebastian-raschka]]'s independent technical publication on Substack, launched in October 2022. It provides detailed explainers, research-paper analyses, educational material, and practical discussions of LLM architectures, attention mechanisms, fine-tuning, reasoning, evaluation, and local deployment. By October 2026 its research-context footprint cites >223,000 subscribers, making it one of the prominent independent sources of technical AI/ML analysis sitting alongside Nathan Lambert's Interconnects, Swyx's Latent.Space, and the Dwarkesh essay / podcast cohort.

Its significance is informational rather than commercial: Ahead of AI helps engineers, researchers, and technically sophisticated decision-makers interpret new model releases and research trends in a field where primary papers and product announcements often outpace documentation. The publication is directly affiliated with Sebastian Raschka's broader educational output (his *from-scratch* books and video courses) — subscribers get both the newsletter and a steady feed of code-along artifacts.
