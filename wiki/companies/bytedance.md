## Summary

ByteDance is the Chinese tech giant best known for TikTok / Douyin and now operates a fast-growing media-AI surface. Its [[seedance-2]] model anchors the company's video-generation push (multimodal text/image/audio/video inputs, native audio output, 2K resolution, 15-second clips), shipping through ByteDance's Jimeng AI video platform and creator tools. Alongside Seedance, ByteDance has surfaced an in-preview Seedream 5.0 image model and a Seed 2.0 family of base models reportedly competitive with [[gpt-5-2]] and [[gemini-3]] Pro at much lower cost.
