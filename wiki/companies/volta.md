## Summary

Volta is an AI-infrastructure startup that emerged from stealth on August 5, 2026 with a reported six-year, $10 billion compute agreement with [[anthropic]] and a funding round at a $2.4 billion valuation. Backed by [[nvidia]], Volta will deliver **133 MW** of capacity to Anthropic using **NVIDIA Vera Rubin** chips, alongside co-provider Bitdeer Technologies (a Nasdaq-listed compute-infrastructure operator pivoting from Bitcoin mining into AI-DC hosting). Volta joins [[terawulf]] as a mid-2026 emergent-AI-compute-supplier tier serving frontier labs — [[spacex]] Colossus 1 + Amazon Trainium + Google TPU are Anthropic's other primary substrates. The company's stealth emergence pairs Anthropic's diversification play with NVIDIA's growing motion as anchor-financier of frontier-AI compute infrastructure (SSI $5B / OpenAI $250B Ohio backstop / Reflection backing).
