## Summary

Ineffable Intelligence is the new AI company founded by **David Silver**, the AlphaGo / AlphaZero / MuZero creator and DeepMind reinforcement-learning veteran. Announced 2026-04-29 with a $1.1B seed round, it explicitly targets building "superlearners" — agents that can learn independently and scale indefinitely via reinforcement learning rather than relying on next-token-prediction language-model scaling. The launch lands in the same week as DeepSeek V4's open-source release and Kimi K2.6's trillion-parameter drop, but on a fundamentally different research thesis: superlearner agents that improve through environment interaction rather than human-curated text. The $1.1B seed is one of the largest ever recorded for a pre-product AI company and joins [[thinking-machines]] (Mira Murati), [[ami-labs]] (Yann LeCun), and [[merge-labs]] (Sam Altman) in the cohort of frontier-lab-spinout mega-seeds.
