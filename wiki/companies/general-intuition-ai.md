## Summary

General Intuition AI is one of the AI Supremacy [[neo-labs]] cohort — a research startup focused on **Large Action Models (LAMs)** and **world models** that perceive, predict, and act in real time across virtual and physical spaces. The thesis fuses two strands already tracked in this wiki: the world-models line ([[world-labs]] Marble, [[ami-labs]] LeCun world models, [[project-genie]]) and the action-tier agent line ([[humanoid-robotics]], NVIDIA Isaac, OpenAI Robotics restart).
