## Summary

Substack is a subscription publishing platform that hosts independent newsletters and functions as a central distribution venue for AI/ML commentary — including Nathan Lambert's **Interconnects** (which drives much of the open-model + post-training + AI-policy discourse in this wiki) alongside Latent.Space, Ahead of AI (Sebastian Raschka), Simon Willison, Deep (Learning) Focus (Cameron Wolfe), and many of the project's subscribed cohort newsletters. In Oct 2026 Substack rolled out an **AI-detection feature powered by Pangram** — readers can scan posts, Notes, replies, and comments for estimated AI-generated or AI-assisted content, while writers can disclose their production process and scan eligible drafts.

The AI-detection feature positions Substack at the center of the emerging debate over authorship, trust, and platform governance in an AI-saturated publishing environment, though detection tools remain imperfect and the platform has faced criticism over labeling, consent, opt-outs, and risk of false positives. Separately, Substack has been reported to be exploring **MCP integrations** with AI assistants — potentially allowing [[chatgpt]] or [[claude]] to read, write, and perform creator workflows on the platform via [[model-context-protocol]] — an indication that its role may extend from *AI-commentary-hosting venue* to *AI-assisted-publishing infrastructure*.
