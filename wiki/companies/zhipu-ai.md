## Summary

Zhipu AI is one of the principal Chinese frontier labs, developer of the open-weight GLM model family. In late December 2025 its [[glm-4-7]] model is reported as the #1 open model on WebDev and #6 overall — surpassing multiple GPT-5 variants and other closed models — pushing Zhipu into the same tier of newsletter attention as [[deepseek]], [[alibaba]]/Qwen, and [[moonshot-ai]].
