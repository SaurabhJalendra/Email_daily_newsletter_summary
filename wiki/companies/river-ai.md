## Summary

River AI is a new AI startup founded by [[xai]] co-founder Igor Babuschkin, publicly launched on 2026-08-12 with $1.1B in initial funding. The company aims to build a *"new type of home computer that allows people to own and use AI without oversight from big corporations"*, with a stated plan to share its AI technologies as open-source software. It sits at the intersection of the 2026-H2 *user-owned AI-hardware* and *anti-hyperscaler open-source* movements.
