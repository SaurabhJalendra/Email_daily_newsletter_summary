## Summary

Character.AI is the companion-chatbot platform that lets users converse with persona-style AI characters. In late October 2025 it announced that — effective 2025-11-25 — users under 18 will no longer be able to have open-ended conversations with characters; they'll be limited to generating content (videos and similar). The change followed a wave of lawsuits from parents (including one tied to a teen's death) and growing public concern about emotional dependence on AI companions. Character.AI's policy shift becomes a reference point for [[ai-mental-health-safety]] and [[ai-regulation]] discussions.
