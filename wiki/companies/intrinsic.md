## Summary

Intrinsic is Alphabet's industrial-robotics software subsidiary. In September 2026, Intrinsic open-sourced its core robotics stack (**Intrinsic Core**) at ROSCon 2026, positioning it as a lower-barrier development substrate for industrial robotics applications. The stack includes a hardware-agnostic real-time control framework, 6-DoF pose estimation, automatic motion and grasp planning, and pre-tuned ROS drivers. Intrinsic's open-sourcing move canonicalizes Alphabet's industrial-robotics posture as *concrete open-source substrate operator* alongside NVIDIA's Isaac ROS 5.0 canonical robotics-AI-agent productization at the same ROSCon 2026.
