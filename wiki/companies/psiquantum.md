## Summary

PsiQuantum is a US photonic quantum-computing company developing utility-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computers using silicon photonics. Its late-October 2026 relevance to AI stems from an Optical Circuit Switching (OCS) initiative: the company is adapting the same low-loss photonics platform built for quantum computing to help AI data centers route optical GPU interconnects between racks without repeated optical-to-electrical conversion. This positions PsiQuantum as a *dual-substrate operator* — long-term fault-tolerant quantum computer vendor + near-term AI-data-center-networking supplier — with a potential business path to monetize its silicon-photonics manufacturing stack well before its quantum program reaches scale.

PsiQuantum's quantum program is backed by major US government support, including a ~$100M CHIPS-related award, a $125M DARPA agreement, and a conditional loan commitment of up to $150M. The same manufacturing + optical primitives support both the quantum computer and the OCS products.
