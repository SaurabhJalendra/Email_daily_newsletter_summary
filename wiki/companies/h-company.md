## Summary

H Company is an AI startup shipping **computer-use agents** that run on both desktop and browser and plug into third-party coding-agent and agent-runtime surfaces including [[claude-code]], [[cursor]], and [[nous-research]]'s Hermes. Surfaced in AlphaSignal's HIGH August 2026 issue as a cycle-headline entrant, H Company positions its agents as a *harness-agnostic computer-use substrate* — the agent binds to another vendor's coding-agent or agent-framework host and executes GUI-driven actions on the user's machine, extending those hosts' capabilities into the desktop-automation tier.
