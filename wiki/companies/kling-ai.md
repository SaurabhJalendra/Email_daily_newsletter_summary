## Summary

Kling AI is a Chinese AI video-generation company spun off from short-video platform Kuaishou Technology. In July 2026 the company announced a major funding round with investors including Alibaba and Tencent injecting over 19 billion yuan (~$2.8B), valuing the AI video arm at roughly $15B pre-money. Kling AI focuses on short-form AI video generation, positioning it as a major player in the generative media ecosystem alongside [[runway]], [[luma]], and [[bytedance]] Seedance. The Alibaba + Tencent co-investment is structurally significant as it aligns two of China's largest tech companies behind a single AI-video-generation platform.

The $15B valuation places Kling AI at valuation-parity with frontier-model startups, signaling that generative media has become a core industry vertical in the AI investment landscape and that Chinese generative-video capacity is being aggressively capitalized inside China rather than acquired abroad (contrasting with the same-cycle China-block of Meta's $2B [[manus]] acquisition — see [[ai-regulation]]).
