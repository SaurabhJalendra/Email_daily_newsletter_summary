## Summary

Databricks is a data-lakehouse and AI platform vendor that has become one of the central infrastructure providers for enterprise AI workloads — combining a distributed compute engine (built on Spark) with model training, evaluation, and serving surfaces. In mid-July 2026 the company announced a **$188 billion Series M** funding round, positioning it among the most highly valued private AI-infrastructure companies globally and reflecting the surge of enterprise investment in AI training and analytics stacks. Databricks has also been active as a **third-party benchmark authority** for coding agents — its comparative benchmarking of [[gpt-5-6]] + [[grok-4-5]] was cited in [[gpt-5-6]]'s cycle-16 as one of the three third-party benchmark cohorts alongside Artificial Analysis and METR.
