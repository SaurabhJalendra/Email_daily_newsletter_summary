## Summary

Radical Numerics is a mid-2026 bio-security AI company using biological chain-of-thought and multimodal perception to build and scale **Genomic Language Models (GLMs)** — models that tackle a wide range of biological problems, extending well beyond generating DNA. CEO **Eric Nguyen** frames bio-defense as an AI arms race, arguing that "the same models that increase biological capability can also keep defense from falling behind" and that defense is *currently losing this battle*. Radical Numerics' GLMs can already handle RNA and protein via clear DNA-sequence markers, generalize to 3D protein structure + epigenetics + natural language, and produce entire bacteriophage genomes that have been synthesized into functional viruses. Early DNA-language models like Evo and Evo-2 (Arc Institute, 2025-2026) built the substrate that made this possible via long-context modeling innovations from ~3 years ago.
