## Summary

**Inception Labs** is the AI research + product company behind the **Mercury family** of diffusion-based large language models. Its flagship product [[mercury-3]] launched October 19 2026 as one of the first production-tier **diffusion-based LLMs** at frontier-adjacent capability — a design that iteratively refines multiple tokens rather than generating one token at a time, aimed at improving throughput for real-time generation, coding assistants, and agent workloads.

Inception's canonical wiki-tier signal is the Mercury 3 release: reportedly generating **>1,000 tokens/second on NVIDIA GPUs** (vendor-claimed ~4-8× faster than autoregressive comparators), supporting a **1-million-token context window**, and distributed through Inception's own API plus [[baseten]] + [[openrouter]] + [[vercel]]. Mercury 3's vendor-reported benchmarks place it ahead of [[gpt-6-luna]] on **SWE-Bench Atlas (51.1 vs 33.3)** but behind [[claude-haiku-5-5]] on **DeepSWE** and one long-context evaluation — the industry impact depends on independent replication and real-world reliability.

Structurally, Inception sits on the frontier-model-architecture-alternatives substrate as the *concrete-production-tier-diffusion-LLM-vendor-tier* operator, distinct from the autoregressive-attention substrate that dominates frontier labs (OpenAI + Anthropic + Google + Meta + DeepSeek).
