## Summary

Block is the fintech parent of Square and Cash App (Jack Dorsey). In early March 2026 it cut roughly 40% of its employees (>4,000 people) despite posting its best quarter in company history — attributing the workforce reduction to an internal AI agent called "Goose." On 2026-04-03 Dorsey co-authored a widely circulated post explicitly framing the cuts as "a bet on AI, not a response to weakness" and arguing that AI can replace middle management: managers exist to route information, and a live "world model" of the business can now do that. Dorsey maps every remaining Block role onto three categories — builders, problem-owners, and player-coaches — setting a template other AI-forward employers quickly cite.
