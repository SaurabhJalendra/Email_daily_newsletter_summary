## Summary

Salesforce is the large enterprise SaaS vendor moving aggressively into AI agents for workflow automation. In October 2025 it launched [[agentforce]] 360 — an expanded generation of its agent platform targeting customer service, sales, and back-office workflows. On 2026-04-03, Salesforce simultaneously (a) took the wraps off a 30-feature AI update to [[slack]] (which it owns) centered on an AI-native Slackbot plus Agentforce integration, and (b) was reported to have invested in [[anthropic]]'s recent funding round rather than [[openai]] — read as a strategic vendor signal pointing away from the ChatGPT/OpenAI stack and toward Claude for Salesforce's own AI roadmap.
