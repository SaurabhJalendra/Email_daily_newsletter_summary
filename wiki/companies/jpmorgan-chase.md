## Summary

JPMorgan Chase is the world's largest investment bank and one of the most aggressively AI-adopting financial institutions. Its internal and customer-facing generative AI stack is centered on its proprietary **LLM Suite** platform — a secure, model-agnostic interface that aggregates leading large language models (including [[openai]]'s GPT family and [[anthropic]]'s Claude) behind a single employee-facing surface. Chase committed publicly in June 2026 to becoming a **"fully AI-powered megabank"** where every employee has an AI assistant, most internal processes are driven by AI agents, and client interactions increasingly involve AI concierges. The bank reports **400–450+ active gen-AI use cases** spanning research analysis, code generation, document summarization, fraud detection, marketing, compliance, and wealth planning, and frames AI as the central pillar of its ~**$18 billion-per-year technology budget**. Chase is also part of the [[claude-mythos]] cybersecurity-defender partner cohort first disclosed on 2026-05-01.
