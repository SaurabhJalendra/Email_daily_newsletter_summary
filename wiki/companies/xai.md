## Summary

xAI is Elon Musk's AI lab, maker of the Grok model family. In early October 2025 it surfaced in newsletter coverage via a compute deal with [[nvidia]]. By April 2026 xAI's profile had shifted toward infrastructure: Musk's "Terafab" team began sourcing chipmaking equipment from suppliers despite semiconductor-industry skepticism, and xAI started repurposing its massive compute stockpile for external customers — effectively entering the compute-rental business.
