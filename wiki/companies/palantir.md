## Summary

Palantir is a data-analytics and AI-defense platform led by CEO Alex Karp. In July 2026 Karp publicly attacked the dominant token-pricing model used by frontier AI labs — framing "tokenmaxxing" as a national-security issue and promoting Palantir's own model-agnostic *own-your-weights* product line in partnership with [[nvidia]]. The framing positions Palantir as an alternative to token-metered API access from labs like [[openai]] / [[anthropic]] / [[google]] for defense and enterprise customers who want deterministic cost profiles and weight ownership rather than per-usage billing.
