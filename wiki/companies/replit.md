## Summary

Replit is a browser-based development platform. In early October 2025 newsletters highlighted its strategic pivot from professional developers toward users who can't code — credited with growing ARR from $2.8M to $150M, illustrating a wider market shift in developer tools toward natural-language / "vibe coding" audiences. In June 2026 Replit was integrated directly into Anthropic's Claude product (announced as "Replit is now available in Claude"), enabling seamless transitions from design to development inside Claude's surface.
