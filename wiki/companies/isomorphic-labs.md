## Summary

**Isomorphic Labs** is an Alphabet-spun AI-for-drug-discovery company founded by Demis Hassabis that applies the [[google]] DeepMind AlphaFold research lineage and related foundation-model-plus-simulation methods to pharmaceutical R&D. The company positions itself as an AI-native drug-discovery vendor and sits inside the broader cohort of foundation-model-driven biology startups (alongside [[lila-sciences]], [[periodic-labs]], [[radical-numerics]], [[inherent]], and others) that scale experimental and computational workflows for molecular design, protein-target identification, and clinical translation.

In November 2026, Isomorphic reportedly secured a **$5 billion Series C** financing round led by **Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital**, with participation from **Andreessen Horowitz** and existing backers including **GV and Thrive Capital**. If confirmed, the round would rank among the largest single investments into an AI-native drug-discovery company and signals strong investor confidence in foundation-model-driven biology. The financing could accelerate model development, pharmaceutical partnerships, and clinical translation — a canonical mid-Nov-2026 concrete-first-moment mega-round at AI-native drug-discovery substrate.
