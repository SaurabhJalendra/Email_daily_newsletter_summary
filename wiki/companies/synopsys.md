## Summary

Synopsys is a long-established Electronic Design Automation (EDA) incumbent whose software is used across the semiconductor industry to design, verify, and tape out chips. The company surfaces in this wiki (Oct 2026) through a reported multiyear strategic partnership with [[openai]] to develop [[gpt-synopsys]], a specialized model intended to operate semiconductor-design tools and automate increasingly complex chip-engineering workflows — effectively positioning Synopsys as the *EDA-vendor side* of OpenAI's broader chip-design-stack posture, which already includes the in-house [[openai-jalapeno]] custom-silicon arc (co-designed with [[broadcom]]) and the XLS/DSLX open-source toolchain disclosed earlier in 2026.
