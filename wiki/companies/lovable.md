## Summary

Lovable is a European "vibe-coding" startup whose product turns natural-language prompts into shippable web/mobile app scaffolding. Newsletter coverage picks it up in late December 2025 when the company raises $330M at a $6.6B valuation — one of the largest vibe-coding rounds to date — placing it alongside [[claude-code]], [[cursor]], and Andrej Karpathy's framing of a broader shift in how software gets built (see [[vibe-coding]]).
