## Summary

SoftBank is the Japanese investment and telecom group that has become OpenAI's largest single capital backer through a stack of Vision Fund investments, the Stargate compute JV with Oracle, and a series of tranches that took its OpenAI stake past 11% by early 2026. The group has been a recurring funder of frontier-AI rounds (including $3B into [[xai]] via the [[humain]] vehicle and a planned $100B "Roze AI" robotics spin-off) and, in mid-2026, doubled down with a new strategic partnership with [[openai]] specifically aimed at financing and scaling AI infrastructure — data centers, connectivity, and enterprise/telecom deployment of frontier models. The 2026-06-28 framing positions SoftBank not just as an investor but as an infrastructure-and-distribution channel tying OpenAI's stack into its global portfolio.
