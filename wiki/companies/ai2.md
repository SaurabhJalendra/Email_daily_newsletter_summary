## Summary

Ai2 (the Allen Institute for AI) is a Seattle-based nonprofit AI research lab founded by the late Paul Allen. In late February 2026 it released PreScience — an open benchmark that tests an AI's ability to forecast how scientific research will unfold — introducing a new metric called LACERScore for scoring abstract alignment. The release slots into Ai2's pattern of open, research-integrity-focused contributions.
