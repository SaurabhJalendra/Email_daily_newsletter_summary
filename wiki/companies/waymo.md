## Summary

Waymo is Alphabet's autonomous-driving subsidiary and the current commercial leader of the US robotaxi market. The AI Supremacy "How Google's Waymo is Scaling Robotaxis in 2026" HIGH-priority deep-dive on 2026-06-25-evening lands Waymo's headline financing event and the cleanest single-source operational snapshot in this wiki: **$16B raise at a $126B valuation**, **500,000+ paid rides per week across 11 US cities**, target of **1M rides/week by end of 2026**, and active deployments expanding into **London and Tokyo**. The sixth-generation **Waymo Driver** stack powers the fleet, and Waymo has introduced **Waymo Premier**, its first subscription tier. Vehicle supply is the binding constraint; partnerships with **Hyundai** and **Zeekr** are framed as the planned path to fleet growth.

Strategically, Waymo's stack is moving toward a foundation-model-for-autonomy posture: the **EMMA** end-to-end multimodal driving model is built on [[gemini]] and processes raw sensor data into trajectories + detections + road-graph understanding, and the **Waymo World Model** uses DeepMind's Genie 3 to generate synthetic driving environments from text prompts for rare-edge-case training. Waymo's safety record (lower serious-injury-or-worse crash rate than human drivers) anchors its regulatory and competitive position. The Uber relationship is reframed in the cycle as *increasingly transactional* — Uber is now investing in other AV companies while Waymo expands into new cities independent of Uber. Main competitors are Chinese players: **Pony.ai**, **WeRide**, and Baidu's **Apollo Go**.
