## Summary

InterPositive is a 16-person filmmaking-technology startup co-founded by **Ben Affleck** that builds **AI-powered post-production tools designed "by and for filmmakers."** Rather than generating content from text prompts, InterPositive trains **project-specific models** on a production's own dailies to understand that project's visual logic, editorial consistency, and cinematic rules — then applies them to reframing shots, relighting scenes, color mixing, rotoscoping, wire removal, background fixes, and continuity error correction. The explicit design tenet is *non-generative*: tools work only on footage from the specific production, positioned as force-multipliers for human crews rather than replacements.

In July 2026 **Netflix acquired InterPositive for a reported $587–600 million** in cash plus performance-based earn-outs — ranking among Netflix's largest-ever acquisitions and validating the *vertically-integrated, production-focused AI* thesis in Hollywood. The entire 16-person team joins Netflix; Affleck becomes a **senior adviser** on AI-driven film technology. Netflix will use the tools internally on Netflix productions rather than sell them commercially. Industry framing positions InterPositive as an intentional counterpoint to generative video AI's Hollywood labor tensions — targeting high-cost, tedious post-production tasks (VFX, color, wire removal) instead of scriptwriting or acting.
