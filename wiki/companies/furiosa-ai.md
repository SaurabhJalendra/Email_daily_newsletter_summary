## Summary

FuriosaAI is a Korean AI inference-chip startup challenging NVIDIA at the datacenter inference tier. Its flagship RNGD (pronounced "renegade") accelerator is a 180W inference chip that the company claims matches GPU throughput at half the power or less. On October 30, 2026, FuriosaAI raised $620M at a $2.9B valuation to scale RNGD production — positioning the company alongside [[tenstorrent]] (US independent AI silicon at $6.2B valuation per Oct 2026) and the broader inference-specialist cohort of [[groq]] + [[cerebras]] + [[sambanova]] as *canonical late-2026 non-NVIDIA inference-silicon operators*.
