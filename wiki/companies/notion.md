## Summary

Notion is the collaborative workspace app (docs, databases, wikis) that in late February 2026 launched Custom Agents — autonomous AI teammates that can automate tasks and answer questions without being prompted per turn. By late June 2026 Notion announces a native [[anthropic]] Claude integration that allows users to build and run Claude-powered AI agents directly inside their Notion workspace *without needing a separate Anthropic account* — productizing Notion as a Claude-distribution surface aimed at the workspace-native enterprise market.
