## Summary

Cohere is a Toronto-based enterprise LLM vendor with its own Command model family and the North AI enterprise platform. On 2026-03-28 it released Transcribe — open-source ASR topping HuggingFace's leaderboard across 14 languages. On 2026-05-21 it shipped **[[cohere-command-a-plus]]** — a 218B-parameter dense frontier-tier model under **Apache 2.0**, becoming one of the first major Western labs to release weights this size with fully permissive commercial licensing. The release lands amid the broader 2026 open-weights surge ([[deepseek-v4]], [[gemma-4]], [[kimi-k2-6]], MiMo 2.5, GLM-5.1, [[mistral-3]]) and positions Cohere in the open-frontier-tier competitive lane.
