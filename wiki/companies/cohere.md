## Summary

Cohere is a Toronto-based enterprise LLM vendor with its own Command model family and the North AI enterprise platform. It surfaced on 2026-03-28 with Transcribe, its first speech-to-text model — released open-source and reported to top HuggingFace's accuracy leaderboard across 14 languages, positioned for low-word-error-rate production use. Transcribe integrates with North AI and is framed alongside Mistral's Voxtral TTS and ElevenLabs as part of a wave of open-weight audio models pressuring proprietary incumbents.
