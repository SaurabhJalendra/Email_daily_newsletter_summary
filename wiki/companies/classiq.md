## Summary

Classiq is an Israeli quantum-computing software company whose hardware-agnostic platform automatically synthesizes quantum algorithms into executable circuits across backends. On October 26 2026 [[intel]] announced its intent to acquire Classiq, combining its software stack with Intel's silicon spin-qubit technology, quantum software, and semiconductor-manufacturing capabilities to create a full-stack quantum-computing business. The transaction is subject to regulatory approval and expected to close in the first half of 2027; the companies will remain independent until closing. Multiple reports put the price at ~$2.3B in cash and stock, though Intel has not officially disclosed the figure.
