## Summary

DoorDash is a US local-commerce platform best known for restaurant and grocery delivery, now positioning itself as an AI-enabled commerce-and-logistics operator. In Q3 2026 the company launched three named AI agents — **Ask DoorDash** (discovers similar restaurants and builds grocery baskets), **Text DoorDash** (ordering by SMS), and a **Model Context Protocol interface** that lets external AI agents place corporate orders. In October 2026 DoorDash also shipped a **connector for teams** that enables companies' own AI agents to handle lunch and office-snack runs, canonicalizing the vendor on the [[agentic-commerce]] arc alongside Shopify Agentic Storefronts + Adobe-in-ChatGPT.

On Oct 29 2026 DoorDash announced a **definitive agreement to acquire [[instacart]] for approximately $17 billion** ($65/share, ≥75% cash + up-to-25% DoorDash stock), expected to close in late 2027 or H1 2028 subject to shareholder and regulatory approval. The combined entity would pair DoorDash's restaurant-delivery network with Instacart's grocery, fulfillment, advertising, and AI technologies (including Instacart's **Clementine** AI shopping assistant, Caper smart carts, and shelf-scanning computer vision) while retaining the Instacart brand and app — producing one of the largest grocery-plus-restaurant AI commerce stacks and attracting likely antitrust scrutiny.
