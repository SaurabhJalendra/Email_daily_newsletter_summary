## Summary

Periodic Labs is an AI-for-science startup pursuing *synthesis superintelligence* — a loop of simulations + high-throughput automated experiments + machine learning aimed at discovering new materials and material properties. Co-led by Liam Fedus and Ekin Dogus Cubuk (both ex-Google-Brain/DeepMind researchers), the company builds AI scientists end-to-end: ML models, a "matter compiler" for simulation and property prediction, custom robotic-arm and lab-hardware stacks, and automated experimentation infrastructure.

Target applications include **room-temperature superconductors, new magnets, and more efficient computing materials**. The company has received significant funding and is expanding operations + hiring. It sits in the *AI-for-science foundation-lab cohort* alongside [[lila-sciences]], [[radical-numerics]], [[inherent]], and [[faraday-27b]].
