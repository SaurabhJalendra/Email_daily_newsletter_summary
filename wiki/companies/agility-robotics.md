## Summary

Agility Robotics is a leading humanoid-robotics vendor focused on deploying its general-purpose humanoid **Digit** into real industrial environments — manufacturing, warehousing, logistics. The company was previously canonicalized in this wiki for its **60K-sq-ft training facility near Tesla Fremont** (2026-07-26) and **Digit v5 unfenced-human-robot-collaboration + cycloidal-actuators + December-2026 early-customer-deployment** announcement (2026-08-13).

Digit has logged **>65,000 hours of real production experience** across multiple customer facilities. Digit 5 (see [[digit-5]]) pushes toward mass deployment: **50-lb payload** (+40% over Digit 4), swappable grippers, **~9-minute rapid recharge** enabling ~20 working hours/day, and cooperatively safe operation alongside humans without traditional safety cages. Multi-year orders exceed **$300M** with commitments from Toyota and others; **EU/UK rollout** planned via CE marking; early access H1 2027 → broader availability by end of 2027. An anticipated public listing via SPAC deal is in play.
