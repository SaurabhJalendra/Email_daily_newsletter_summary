## Summary

HANDO Labs is building a **crowdsourced data network for robotics training**, where individual users record short clips of themselves performing physical tasks — *"fastening buckles or sorting objects"* — and robotics teams license the resulting dataset. In return, contributors receive **tokenized exposure to public stocks like Nvidia and Apple**, aligning contributors' upside with the broader AI/robotics equity trade rather than paying flat cash.

The model is structurally novel two ways: it (a) inverts the closed-lab-generates-its-own-training-data thesis by treating humanoid training data as a *marketplace commodity*; and (b) offers a **crypto-adjacent tokenized-stock-exposure compensation primitive** — an alternative to cash payment for micro-task workers, tying contributor earnings to the broader AI-equity boom. It joins [[mecka-ai]] (Sequoia-backed human-motion-data vendor at $500M valuation) as a canonical humanoid-training-data-vendor operator on the mid-2026 physical-AI training-data substrate.
