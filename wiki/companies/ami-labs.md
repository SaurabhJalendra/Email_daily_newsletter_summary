## Summary

AMI Labs is Yann LeCun's post-Meta AI startup, confirmed in late December 2025 following his earlier-announced departure from [[meta]]'s Chief AI Scientist role. AMI Labs is pitched around "world-model" AI that moves beyond language prediction to simulate environments — the same research thesis LeCun has publicly championed for years — and is positioned as a European frontier-lab anchor. Initial newsletter coverage describes AMI Labs as targeting €3B in funding to drive European AI innovation, placing it alongside [[world-labs]] (Fei-Fei Li) as the two most prominent world-models-as-company bets of the 2025–2026 cycle.
