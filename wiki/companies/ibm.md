## Summary

IBM is the century-old enterprise-software + hybrid-cloud incumbent whose AI product surface centers on watsonx (its AI development + deployment platform) and the Granite open-weight model family. In the mid-2026 frontier-lab cycle it re-enters the *agentic coding platform* competitive surface with [[ibm-bob]] — an AI coding partner for enterprise dev teams that coordinates planning, coding, testing, and validation across the SDLC — positioning IBM against [[claude-code]] (Anthropic), [[cursor]], [[openai-codex]] + ChatGPT Work, and [[github-agent-hq]] (Microsoft) on enterprise-lifecycle-coordination rather than pure code-generation.

IBM's Watson Health $4B AI-healthcare bet (2015–2022) is the canonical enterprise-tier cautionary tale of AI-hype-outpacing-validation, cited widely in 2026 discourse: the project trained models on *"imaginary patients"*, failed to integrate real clinical data at scale, and had partner pilots (MD Anderson Oncology Expert Advisor) end after tens of millions spent with *zero patients treated*, before being sold to private equity in 2022. IBM has since pivoted to a more grounded watsonx + hybrid-cloud + AI-consulting posture.
