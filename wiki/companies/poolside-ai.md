## Summary

Poolside AI is an American-open-weights neolab building foundation models via a **"Model Factory"** approach — a small team of top researchers with tooling that enables rapid iteration and improvement of models. Co-CEO Eiso Kant emphasizes **open research and collaboration** as the foundation of the company's methodology, with **persistence and verification** as core model-development behaviors. The lab ships the **[[laguna-s]]** family, most recently Laguna S 2.1 — a 118B MoE with 8B active parameters per token and up to 1M-token context — positioned as *"cheaper than Deepseek v4 Flash, better than Deepseek v4 Pro"* and community-tier candidate for *"strongest American open-weight model"*. Poolside's thesis includes balanced trade-offs between model size and compute efficiency, low-precision training, and use of specialized hardware (GPUs and TPUs). The company is hiring for applied research and engineering roles.
