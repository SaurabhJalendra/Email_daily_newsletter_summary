## Summary

Tavus is the vendor of [[tavus-griffin]], a real-time video+voice "Human Interaction Model" launched October 2, 2026. The company's flagship capability combines computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning into a conversational avatar that can look, listen, and talk back live — citing a test figure that ~48% of live participants in video calls mistook the model's output for a real human. Tavus is currently testing a Griffin-Lite variant with trusted users to refine safety and disclosure features before a public rollout.

Structurally, Tavus occupies the real-time video-avatar substrate alongside Synthesia (which same-cycle launched Sessions, a conversational-avatar platform for roleplay and survey interviews) and ElevenLabs' voice-agent cohort — productizing the Turing-test-adjacent live-video-interaction tier that AlphaSignal flagged as a defining substrate of mid-2026 video-first agentic UX.
