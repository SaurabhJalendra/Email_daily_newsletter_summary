## Summary

MiniMax is one of the Chinese AI labs active in the December 2025 open-weights push. Late-December coverage centers on MiniMax M2.1 — a coding, agent, multi-step reasoning, and tool-use model — followed by an enhanced version with expanded multi-language support (Rust, Java, Golang, C++) plus native Android and iOS development capabilities.
