## Summary

Midjourney is a bootstrapped (no outside funding) generative AI lab originally known for its diffusion-based text-to-image models and a very large creator community. It produces the namesake Midjourney text-to-image product and [[niji-7]] (anime-style image model, Jan 2026), and shipped its first video model, **V1**, in 2026. AINews labels it "the only bootstrapped frontier lab" and frames the June 2026 launch of the **Midjourney Medical Scanner** as its **second product** and second category — a sharp pivot from creative-software into AI-driven medical imaging hardware led by CEO David Holz.

## Timeline

- **2026-06-18-evening**: **Midjourney unveils its first hardware product — the Midjourney Medical Scanner**, a full-body ultrasonic CT system positioned as **"the first new whole-body medical imaging modality in 50 years"** (David Holz framing). The system uses ultrasound through a water-immersion tank instead of MRI or CT radiation, claims to be **~10× cheaper and ~60× faster than an MRI**, and is scheduled to debut inside **Midjourney Spa**, a planned spa-like facility in San Francisco that doubles as testing ground and amenity (hot tubs, saunas, gym). Long-term plan: scale to **50,000 scanners** and **1 billion scans/month**, enabling high-frequency sub-millimeter differential tracking for preventive medicine. Initial FDA regulatory path is body composition (lowest-risk classification); diagnostic and therapeutic uses to follow. **Evolving AI Insights frames the device as potentially preventing close to 30% of all deaths** through early detection. AINews subject ("scan your organs like you step on a scale") sharpens the consumer-frequency framing. This is Midjourney's **second product** and **second category** per AINews, and the first publicly framed AI-lab-built whole-body medical imaging hardware in this wiki.
