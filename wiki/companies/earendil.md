## Summary

Earendil is the vendor behind **Pi**, an open-source, MIT-licensed minimal AI coding-agent harness. The company reached its first stable release ([[pi-1-0]]) on October 1, 2026, and simultaneously announced [[pi-durable]], a TypeScript port targeting production-grade reliability (crash survival, portability, concurrency, multiplayer/state sync).

Earendil's design philosophy is deliberately minimal and user-customizable rather than a prescriptive platform — a contrast to vendor-locked harnesses like [[claude-code]] and Codex CLI, and sitting in the broader open coding-agent-harness cohort. Pi's AINews cycle-headline coverage positions Earendil as a credible open-source alternative in the mid-2026 harness market.
