## Summary

CoreWeave is a US-listed pure-play GPU-cloud AI-infrastructure operator that rents NVIDIA GPU capacity to frontier labs + hyperscaler customers. On November 10 2026 the company reported third-quarter revenue of **$3.66 billion**, roughly **2.67× YoY growth** from $1.37 billion in Q3 2025 and above analyst expectations, while telling investors it is still targeting profitability in **2028**. The quarterly print lands as canonical early-Nov-2026 reference anchor for AI-infrastructure demand durability — concrete evidence that GPU-cloud demand continues to compound even as macro + bubble concerns build ([[ai-bubble-concerns]]).

The two sides of the Q3 narrative are: (a) revenue growth is extraordinary for a pure-play GPU-cloud at this scale, and the Meta $21B deal + Anthropic / Microsoft customer relationships back it with multi-year committed capacity; and (b) investors remain concerned about CoreWeave's debt load financing the GPU build-out and the durability of AI-infrastructure demand if frontier-lab economics weaken. Pairs cycle-structurally with the [[anthropic]] IPO prep at $1.4T + [[openai]] $11B at $1.25T + [[groq]] Series B $8B canonical early-Nov-2026 AI-infrastructure financing cluster.
