## Summary

TeraWulf is a US-listed compute-infrastructure operator that has pivoted from Bitcoin mining to AI datacenter hosting. In July 2026 it landed a **$19B multi-year AI-infrastructure lease deal with [[anthropic]]** — first publicly framed *pure-play compute-infrastructure operator* alongside hyperscalers in Anthropic's compute-supply diversification arc (AWS + Google TPU + Azure Foundry + Samsung-custom-chip + now TeraWulf-lease). Initial capacity comes online in the second half of 2027, structurally aligning with the same-week Sarah Friar OpenAI *"late-2027 compute crunch"* framing — Anthropic pre-books capacity to exit the projected industry-wide compute-crunch window with dedicated leased infrastructure.

TeraWulf's positioning validates the *former-crypto-miner-to-AI-DC-operator* subsector as a first-tier Anthropic compute channel and productizes the broader industry pattern where prior-cycle Bitcoin-mining operators (Core Scientific, Riot Platforms, Marathon Digital, TeraWulf) pivot facilities to AI-workload hosting once crypto-mining unit economics deteriorate. The $19B lease structure lets TeraWulf carry the capex-burden while Anthropic pays operating-expense-tier lease payments — structurally distinct from OpenAI's $19B Stargate JV or the reported ~$300B Nvidia commitment.
