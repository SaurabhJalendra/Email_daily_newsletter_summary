## Summary

Ramp is a US corporate-finance platform (spend management, corporate cards, expense automation) that in August 2026 entered the AI-inference layer by launching **Router.com** — a single-endpoint LLM aggregator that switches among frontier models from [[openai]], [[anthropic]], [[deepseek]], [[xai]], and others via one API. Ramp's pitch centers on cost: it claims routing based on benchmarks, cost, or difficulty cuts inference spend by roughly 40% versus single-vendor use. The service is free through the end of 2026 and ships with a $26 launch credit — a fintech-flavored on-ramp for developers that positions Ramp against [[openrouter]] and other model-router incumbents on the *cost-lever + zero-friction-signup* axis.
