## Summary

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI safety lab behind the Claude model family. Through October 2025 it shipped a dense sequence of product updates: [[claude-skills]] (reusable capabilities), plugins for [[claude-code]] (CLI coding agent), Agent Skills for modular task automation, and [[claude-haiku-4-5]] (a fast, low-cost coding model). It also named a new CTO and continued aggressive hiring.
