## Summary

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI safety lab behind the Claude model family. Through October 2025 it shipped a dense sequence of product updates: [[claude-skills]] (reusable capabilities), plugins for [[claude-code]], Agent Skills for modular task automation, and [[claude-haiku-4-5]]. The late-October to early-November 2025 window pushed Claude into both browsers and the workplace: [[claude-code-web]] brought Claude Code into the cloud/browser, [[claude-for-excel]] put a sidebar Claude inside Excel with finance-focused Agent Skills, and a $50B+ multi-year [[google]] Cloud TPU deal (reportedly up to 1M TPUs) gave Anthropic a hardware moat to match its hyperscaler rivals. By early November Dario Amodei was articulating an explicit AGI-by-2030 timeline and Anthropic was reported in talks at a ~$300B valuation. Activity accelerated again into April 2026: Anthropic released [[claude-opus-4-7]] (stronger coding, 3.75 MP vision, xhigh effort, file-based memory), launched [[claude-design]] as a direct Figma/[[adobe]] challenger, added always-on Routines to [[claude-code]] for workflow automation, and opened/expanded a London presence framed as a direct push against [[openai]] in Europe. Reports also place Anthropic in talks for an $800B-plus valuation, nearly matching OpenAI.
