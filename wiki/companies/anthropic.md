## Summary

Anthropic is the San Francisco AI safety lab behind the Claude model family. Through October 2025 it shipped a dense sequence of product updates: [[claude-skills]] (reusable capabilities), plugins for [[claude-code]], Agent Skills for modular task automation, and [[claude-haiku-4-5]]. Activity accelerated into April 2026: Anthropic released [[claude-opus-4-7]] (stronger coding, 3.75 MP vision, xhigh effort, file-based memory), launched [[claude-design]] as a direct Figma/[[adobe]] challenger, added always-on Routines to [[claude-code]] for workflow automation, and opened/expanded a London presence framed as a direct push against [[openai]] in Europe. Reports also place Anthropic in talks for an $800B-plus valuation, nearly matching OpenAI.
