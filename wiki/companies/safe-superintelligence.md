## Summary

Safe Superintelligence (SSI) is Ilya Sutskever's frontier-lab startup, founded after his 2024 departure from [[openai]] where he was Chief Scientist. SSI's stated mission is to build superintelligence safely, with a single-product focus and no near-term commercial product diversion. On 2026-07-28 SSI announced a **Nvidia strategic partnership** including a reported **$5 billion investment**, access to Nvidia's next-generation **[[vera-rubin]]** systems, and **co-development of future AI hardware platforms**. Forward Future frames the compute-side upside as *"expanding its compute capacity by an order of magnitude"* — a 10× step from SSI's prior compute footprint.

The Nvidia partnership graduates SSI from the [[neo-labs]] cohort into a *frontier-lab-tier peer* of Anthropic/OpenAI/xAI, with Nvidia becoming SSI's canonical hardware anchor. Nvidia now has three named frontier-lab hardware co-development partners (SSI + [[anthropic]] + [[openai]]), canonicalizing a *Nvidia-backed frontier-lab hardware cohort* pattern.
