## Summary

Reflection is an NVIDIA-backed US AI lab positioned as an American counter to Chinese open-source frontier models, founded by ex-Google DeepMind researchers. After a $2B round in October 2025 and a March 2026 $2.5B-sought $25B-valuation round explicitly framed as countering Chinese AI's open-source lead, Reflection re-enters wiki coverage in June 2026 via the **$6.3 billion SpaceX compute deal at Colossus 2** — first publicly framed flagship operational scale-up commitment in this wiki, securing access to **Nvidia GB300 chips** at SpaceX's new Memphis Colossus 2 data center. The deal positions Reflection as the second publicly disclosed external SpaceX compute customer at multi-billion-dollar contract scale (after [[anthropic]] at Colossus 1) and sharpens its US-open-source frontier-alternative positioning into a *funded, multi-year operational deployment* posture.
