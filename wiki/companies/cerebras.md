## Summary

Cerebras Systems is the wafer-scale AI inference-silicon vendor whose chips power [[openai]]'s [[gpt-5-3-codex-spark]] (1000+ tokens/sec). It signed a $10B / 750MW deal with OpenAI in January 2026 and filed for an IPO under Nasdaq ticker **CBRS** in April 2026. The IPO completed 2026-05-14: priced at $185/share, sold 30M shares to raise **$5.55B** at IPO, then surged **+68% on debut** to close at $311.07, reaching a market cap near $66-95B — the largest US tech IPO since Uber's 2019 debut. Anchored by the $10B / 750MW OpenAI commitment, Cerebras's wafer-scale architecture (900K active cores, 44GB on-chip SRAM, 21 PB/s aggregate memory bandwidth on WSE-3) positions it as the inference-throughput specialist alongside Groq, with NVIDIA's Vera Rubin as the training-side anchor.
