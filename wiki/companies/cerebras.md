## Summary

Cerebras Systems is the wafer-scale AI inference-silicon vendor whose chips power [[openai]]'s [[gpt-5-3-codex-spark]] (1000+ tokens/sec). It signed a $10B / 750MW deal with OpenAI in January 2026 and filed for an IPO under Nasdaq ticker **CBRS** in April 2026 at a reported $22-25B valuation — making it one of the first AI-inference-silicon vendors to reach the public market. Cerebras's wafer-scale architecture is positioned as an inference-throughput specialist alongside Groq, with NVIDIA's Vera Rubin as the training-side anchor.
