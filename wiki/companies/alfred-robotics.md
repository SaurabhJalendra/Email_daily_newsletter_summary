## Summary

Alfred Robotics (referred to as **Alfred** in coverage) is a stealth robotics-software startup that surfaced in the 2026-06-07 morning cycle when **Sam Altman quietly invested** in it through his investment vehicle Hydrazine Capital, alongside Khosla Ventures and SV Angel. The company builds **software that helps engineers design robots** — i.e., a developer-tools layer for physical-AI hardware rather than a robot product itself. Reported valuation at the time of the news cycle is **$40M**.

The Altman bet is framed by Superhuman coverage as part of the OpenAI CEO's broader expansion into physical AI and robotics — explicitly positioned alongside OpenAI's same-cycle restart of its robotics program. Altman has separately argued that society is unprepared for an imminent "humanoid robot moment," and the Alfred bet operationalizes that view at the developer-tooling layer.
