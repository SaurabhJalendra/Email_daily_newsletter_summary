## Summary

Meta continues to push AI into consumer hardware (AI glasses) and foundational infrastructure. In early October 2025 it acquired chip startup Rivos, held its AI-glasses lead, drew criticism for defaulting users into AI-training data collection, and poached a [[thinking-machines]] co-founder. By April 2026 Meta had made two major moves: a strategic pivot away from its open-weights release strategy, introduced alongside the new [[muse-spark]] multimodal reasoning model, and a $21B deal with CoreWeave for AI cloud infrastructure that includes some of the earliest deployments of NVIDIA's Vera Rubin GPUs.
