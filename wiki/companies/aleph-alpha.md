## Summary

Aleph Alpha is a Heidelberg-based European sovereign-AI LLM vendor that made its name as one of Europe's flagship independent AI labs, positioning itself around European-sovereign-AI, GDPR-compliant enterprise LLM deployment, and explainable AI research. Its Luminous LLM family and PhariaAI platform served regulated German-and-European enterprise, financial, and government customers as an alternative to US frontier labs.

On 2026-09-19, Aleph Alpha and [[cohere]] signed a definitive merger, forming a 1,000-plus-person combined-entity headquartered in Toronto and Berlin. The merger canonicalizes mid-Sep-2026 as a cross-Atlantic Western-open-weights vendor-consolidation inflection window — Aleph Alpha's European-sovereign-AI tier substrate combines with Cohere's Canadian-enterprise-tier Command-family model substrate under one entity, retaining both HQs.
