## Summary

Suno is a Cambridge, MA AI music-generation platform that turns a single text prompt into a complete song — lyrics, vocals, and instrumentation — and is often described as "ChatGPT for music." It positions itself as a new kind of music company built around a foundational "music model," with free + paid subscription tiers and usage-based limits on downloads of AI-generated tracks. The product has reached tens of millions of users and ranks among the most downloaded music apps in Apple's App Store.

Suno sits at the center of the **AI-vs-human-musicians** debate. It has faced copyright lawsuits from the major labels (Universal, Sony, Warner) for training on copyrighted recordings — and in 2026 **settled with Warner Music Group** in a landmark licensing deal that lets opted-in artists' voices, names, and likenesses appear in AI-generated songs in exchange for new compensation mechanisms. Universal and Sony lawsuits remain ongoing. In parallel, Suno has become a venture-capital outlier: a late-2025 $250M Series C at $2.45B was followed in June 2026 by a **$400M Series D at $5.4B** led by **Bond Capital**, explicitly earmarked for new tools and a *first music model built in collaboration with the music industry*.
