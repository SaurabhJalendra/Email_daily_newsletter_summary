## Summary

Samsung is the Korean electronics conglomerate whose Foundry division operates as [[tsmc]]'s primary competitor at leading process nodes. The arc most relevant to this wiki is Samsung Foundry's late-2026 Gate-All-Around (GAA) 2nm ramp: in Q3 2026, Samsung publicly confirmed it had won additional 2nm contracts from major cloud hyperscalers and AI/HPC customers (including hyperscale data centers), and reported that 2nm project wins are expected to more than double year over year. Mass production of the 2nm GAA process began during Q3 2026. Customer names, order volumes, and contract values were not disclosed by Samsung directly, though adjacent disclosures from Faraday Technology identified a US cloud-AI chip project on Samsung's 2nm GAA process with tens of millions of dollars in design and development work and first revenue expected in H1 2027.

The development matters because it gives Samsung stronger validation for its 2nm GAA process in the rapidly expanding market for custom cloud-AI accelerators, where foundry capacity, power efficiency, and performance are strategic constraints. These wins could improve Samsung's advanced-node utilization and strengthen its position against TSMC — although independent reporting has also noted that some large 2nm negotiations remain slower than expected and that production scale and yields are not yet fully established.
