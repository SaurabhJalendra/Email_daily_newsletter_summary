## Summary

Cloudflare is a global content delivery network and edge-platform vendor whose AI-era story first surfaces in May 2026 around a wave of AI-first restructuring. On 2026-05-10 the company laid off ~1,100 employees (roughly 20% of its workforce) citing a shift to an **"agentic AI-first operating model"** — the first publicly disclosed >1K-employee tech layoff with an explicit AI-first framing rationale (extends the Apr-19 [[snap]] 1,000-layoff precedent into the >20%-of-workforce tier and is the largest AI-attribution layoff to date in [[ai-jobs-displacement]]). The 2026-05-12 cycle paired the restructuring with stronger-than-expected quarterly revenue and profit growth — sharpening the "AI-as-margin-expansion" framing that the wider sector now uses to defend headcount cuts during business-strength quarters.
