## Summary

Thomson Reuters is the legal + professional-services publisher that in August 2026 launched its own proprietary legal AI model, trained on decades of the company's proprietary legal/journalism content, and reported benchmark leads over Claude Opus 4.8 + Gemini 3.1 Pro + GPT-5.5 in certain legal-domain areas. The model was built by *retooling Alibaba's open-source Qwen* — canonical mid-2026 example of an enterprise vertical-domain incumbent choosing an **open-weights foundation** as its substrate rather than paying a frontier-lab API tax. Total cost: $40M in staff + compute; the most recent training run cost $450K. Sits alongside [[harvey]] (frontier-lab-integrator strategy) as one of the two dominant legal-AI incumbent postures; Thomson Reuters uniquely proves the *vertical-domain outperforms-frontier via open-weights + proprietary data* thesis.
