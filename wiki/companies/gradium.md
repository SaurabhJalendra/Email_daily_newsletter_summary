## Summary

**Gradium** is the AI research company behind the **[[phonon]]** family of small, state-of-the-art text-to-speech (TTS) models. Phonon models stream audio as it is generated and reportedly exceed real-time playback by more than 100× on Apple Silicon, with support for five languages and dozens of voices. Gradium entered the wiki via its Oct 2026 integration with **[[ollama]]**, which made Phonon the first speech model available through Ollama's new `/v1/audio/speech` OpenAI-compatible endpoint — making Gradium Ollama's launch partner for the speech-model category and placing it in direct competitive proximity to [[elevenlabs]], [[fish-audio]], and [[mistral]]'s [[voxtral-3]] on the open/local voice-AI tier.
