## Summary

Epoch AI is a research organization that quantifies the trajectory of frontier AI — compute, cost, capabilities, and economic impact. It publishes the Epoch Capabilities Index, the Epoch Brief newsletter, and interactive data explorers for ChatGPT usage, benchmark reviews, cyber vulnerabilities, and FrontierMath. Its October 2026 analyses canonicalize two load-bearing late-2026 claims: (1) the cost of achieving a fixed level of AI performance has fallen roughly **47% per quarter (~13×/year)** over the last three years — a decline described as faster than any transformative technology in history, and (2) **nearly 2 billion AI agents could soon exist** if they run on cheap models, based on 2027 AI-chip shipments.

Epoch's October 2026 Brief also canonicalizes Chinese-vs-US AI revenue (China's six leading AI companies generate ~1/10 the AI-related revenue of OpenAI + Anthropic combined), semiconductor-smuggling evidence (>$3B of chips to China via Malaysia), AI's growing role in scientific research (~6% of math preprints credit AI at coauthor level), and OpenAI's internal compute consumption (median researcher daily coding-agent usage grew from <$1 in Jan 2026 to ~$600 by mid-Aug — ~600× in 8 months).
