## Summary

Ghost is a San Francisco AI startup founded by Zain Javaid that emerged from stealth with ~$11M in seed funding led by Andreessen Horowitz. Its debut product, [[core]], is a $3,499 screenless personal AI computer designed to run autonomous agents continuously on local hardware — an NVIDIA RTX Pro 4000 SFF Blackwell GPU (24GB GDDR7), AMD Ryzen 5 7600, 64GB RAM, 1TB SSD. Users interact with it through a phone app while models, personal memory, and agent activity remain encrypted and on-device. Ghost positions Core as a private "brain in a box" and part of the shift from cloud-chatbot assistants toward persistent personal agents running on dedicated local infrastructure.
