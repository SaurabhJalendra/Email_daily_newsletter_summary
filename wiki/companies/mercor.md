## Summary

Mercor is the human-resources / contractor-marketplace startup that supplies the human labor behind AI training — running ~30,000 contractors who help large language models learn how to think and speak. In late October 2025 it raised a $350M round at a $10B valuation, vaulting it into "AI labor infrastructure" prominence. Mercor is also being sued by Scale AI for alleged trade-secret theft, situating it inside the broader fight over the human-data layer. In Sep 2026 Mercor extended into frontier-agent evaluation with [[apex-agents]] — a leaderboard ranking models by productivity on long-horizon multistep economically-valuable work.
