## Summary

ElevenLabs is the voice-AI platform widely used for TTS, voice cloning, and voice agents. Across November 2025 it pushed along three axes: (1) hosting LLMs inside its Agents Platform to cut latency and reasoning cost for voice agents, (2) launching an Iconic Voice Marketplace that lets brands license AI-replicated celebrity voices (28 options at launch) with permissioning and compensation plumbing, and (3) releasing ElevenLabs Music — AI-powered custom music tracks and sound effects — extending from speech into full creative audio.
