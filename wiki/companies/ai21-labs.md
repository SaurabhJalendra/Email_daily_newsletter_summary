## Summary

AI21 Labs is the Israeli AI lab founded by Yoav Shoham and team, best known for its Jurassic language model family and the Jamba hybrid state-space / Transformer architecture. In early January 2026 newsletter coverage reports [[nvidia]] in advanced talks to acquire AI21 Labs for up to $3B — a move that, alongside NVIDIA's $20B [[groq]] acquisition the week prior, would extend NVIDIA's direct footprint from silicon into model-layer assets.
