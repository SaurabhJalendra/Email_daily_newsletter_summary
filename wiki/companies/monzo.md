## Summary

Monzo is a UK digital bank best known in the AI wiki for **Agent Chip**, its in-house coding-agent ecosystem that spans Slack, Linear, GitHub, local developer environments, internal documentation, repositories, and observability. Agent Chip authors roughly **10% of merged pull requests** and runs more than **1,800 tasks per day**, with nearly all engineers using at least one coding agent. It started as a lightweight Slack emoji that turned messages into prompts and evolved into an **MCP-gateway-controlled** workflow — the gateway providing permissioned access to internal tools and knowledge, with isolated sandboxes for remote agents, orchestration, and human review.

Monzo is a canonical **regulated-company agentic-SDLC** case study: the critical innovation is not a proprietary model but the surrounding infrastructure (MCP gateway, sandboxes, orchestration, review), pointing toward agentic software development as a platform capability rather than a standalone coding assistant.
