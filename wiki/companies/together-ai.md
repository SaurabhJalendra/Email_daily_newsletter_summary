## Summary

Together AI is an open-source AI infrastructure company positioning itself as the "cheap open-source plumbing" that other models rely on. Its $800M raise at an $8.3B valuation in July 2026 marks a major funding milestone that anchors the "make frontier AI accessible to all" thesis — providing hosted inference, training, and serving infrastructure for open-source models as an alternative to token-metered API access from proprietary labs.
