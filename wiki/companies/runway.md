## Summary

Runway is the AI video-generation company behind the Gen-N video model series. In early December 2025 it released [[runway-gen-4-5]], which surpassed Google's Veo 3 and OpenAI's Sora on the Artificial Analysis text-to-video benchmark — a direct shot at the two largest frontier-lab video products. The model emphasized physical accuracy: realistic momentum, fluid motion, and material coherence.
